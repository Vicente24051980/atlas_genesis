# SIZE-BLIND POINT ZERO Ω — 80-TICKER AUDIT

**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Status:** RESEARCH SNAPSHOT / DISCOVERY QUEUE  
**Universe:** 40 Nasdaq-100 + 40 S&P 500, zero duplicate tickers  
**Engine:** Size-Blind Point Zero Ω v1.0 + Expectations Debt Ω + Breadth Rotation Ω  
**Authority:** discovery and research-priority only. This report does not create BUY/SELL orders, does not bypass Hard Gates or Replacement Firewall, and does not convert external model/newsletter claims into canonical facts.

## Mission

Run the newly merged Size-Blind Point Zero Ω engine across a deliberately mixed universe of 80 large, mega, mid and smaller companies without awarding or subtracting points for market capitalization itself.

The question is not whether small caps or megacaps are better. The question is:

> **Where are per-share economics entering or sustaining a new acceleration phase before expectations have fully caught up?**

This is a Stage-1 discovery screen. A final canonical P0 score requires a complete, same-definition historical data pack for FCF/share, EPS/share, valuation multiples, revisions, dilution and risk. Where those series were not fully loaded, this report classifies research priority rather than inventing false precision.

## Constitutional rules applied

- Market-cap score contribution = **0**.
- Raw price appreciation is not penalized when FCF/share or EPS/share has caught up or outrun price.
- Low P/E is not automatically value; value-trap risk remains a hard control.
- High growth is not automatically P0 when Expectations Debt is already extreme.
- Aggregate revenue cannot substitute for per-share economics where dilution matters.
- Cyclical trough/base effects do not qualify as structural acceleration by themselves.
- Breadth Rotation Ω can improve discovery priority but cannot rescue weak company economics.
- KKR and APO require the specialist alternative-asset-manager audit based on FRE/ANI/SRE and credit/insurance Red Team; ordinary GAAP P/E screens are not comparable.

## Breadth regime — working read

Current public market evidence supports **BROADENING / positive breadth**, not an unconditional `BROADENING_CONFIRMED` declaration. Smaller-company indices have shown stronger 2026 relative performance and several external forecasts point to faster small-cap earnings growth, but forecast sets conflict materially. Therefore the breadth signal is used as a minor routing input only, consistent with the 5% constitutional cap in Size-Blind P0 Ω.

# PRIMARY-EVIDENCE CHECKS FOR PROMOTED NAMES

## INTU — reset with fundamental support

FY26 company-reported facts:
- revenue +14% to $21.4B;
- Global Business Solutions +16%; Online Ecosystem +19%;
- GAAP operating income +20% to $5.9B;
- GAAP EPS +20% to $16.46;
- FY27 revenue guide +9% to +10% and GAAP EPS +22% to +24%.

Interpretation: the equity reset is not accompanied by business contraction. The central falsifier is whether the market is correctly pricing a structural deterioration in moat/AI positioning rather than temporary expectations compression.

## EME — price run-up does not automatically equal Expectations Debt

Q2 company-reported facts:
- revenue $5.15B, +19.8%;
- operating income $547.3M, margin 10.6%;
- diluted EPS $9.06, +34.8%;
- RPO $17.14B, +43.9%;
- FY revenue and EPS guidance raised.

Interpretation: EME is a canonical example of why Size-Blind P0 does not punish raw price performance in isolation. The required next step is FCF/share and backlog-quality decomposition.

## ICE — quiet per-share compounding

Q2 company-reported facts:
- net revenues $2.7B, +5%;
- GAAP EPS $1.69, +14%;
- GAAP operating income +7%;
- adjusted operating margin 61%;
- record open interest across the exchange complex;
- $1.8B returned to shareholders through June 30, including $1.2B buybacks.

Interpretation: not explosive top-line growth, but a strong candidate for earnings/share compounding at a non-extreme expectations level.

## ADSK — operating leverage plus reset

Q2 FY27 company-reported facts:
- revenue $2.046B, +16%;
- GAAP operating margin 29%, +4ppt;
- FCF $561M, +24%;
- current RPO +12%;
- FY27 revenue/billings expectations increased, including MaintainX contribution.

Interpretation: the clean thesis is not “AI software”; it is a possible economic reset through operating leverage and workflow/platform durability. MaintainX financing/integration is the main new complication.

## ISRG — de-rating without operating deterioration

Q2 company-reported facts:
- combined da Vinci/Ion procedures +16%;
- da Vinci installed base +12%, Ion installed base +21%;
- revenue $2.89B, +19%;
- GAAP EPS $2.29 vs $1.81;
- 0.9M shares repurchased for $0.38B.

Interpretation: high-quality reset candidate, but not “cheap”; valuation remains a decisive gate.

## JCI — backlog/order inflection

Q3 company-reported facts:
- sales +9%, organic +10%;
- organic orders +27%;
- backlog $21.0B, +32% organically;
- North American order/backlog growth was supported by sustained data-center and mission-critical demand.

Interpretation: current acceleration is visible in orders and backlog before complete revenue/FCF conversion. This makes backlog quality and cash conversion mandatory falsifiers.

## ADI — cyclical reset must prove durability

Q3 company-reported facts:
- revenue $4.02B, led by Data Center and Industrial;
- GAAP gross margin 67.3%;
- GAAP operating income +97%;
- GAAP EPS $2.74, +163%;
- TTM operating cash flow $5.5B and FCF $4.9B.

Interpretation: one of the cleanest cycle-reset signals, but the engine must not confuse trough recovery with a new multi-year structural growth rate.

## SYK — operational recovery

Q2 company-reported facts:
- reported sales +9.4%, organic +9.0%;
- reported operating margin 25.2%; adjusted margin +170bp to 27.4%;
- reported EPS +44.1%, adjusted EPS +17.9%;
- FY26 organic sales-growth guide narrowed to 8.3%–9.3%.

Interpretation: advance because earnings/margins are recovering while price has not experienced the same rerating as many market leaders. Cyber recovery/base effects remain an explicit falsifier.

# FALSIFIERS COMMON TO ALL ADVANCED CANDIDATES

A candidate must be downgraded when any of the following becomes true:

- revenue/order acceleration fails to translate into FCF/share or EPS/share;
- price appreciation again outruns per-share economics and comparable multiples expand materially;
- dilution absorbs the operating growth;
- balance-sheet/refinancing risk rises enough to consume expected return;
- the apparent inflection is only a cyclical base effect or one-off accounting item;
- guidance/revisions deteriorate for two successive evidence points without a credible temporary explanation;
- backlog/RPO/orders fail to convert on schedule or gross margins deteriorate materially;
- catalyst proves temporary, replicable or competitively bypassed.
