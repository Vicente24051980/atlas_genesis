## Deep-audit evidence: physical bottleneck leaders

### GEV
FACT: Q2 orders $24.2B, +88% organically; backlog $176B; gas equipment backlog plus slot reservations 116GW; data-center orders exceeded $5B YTD, more than double all of 2025; Q2 FCF $5.1B. Economic Proof and backlog-to-cash conversion are both strong.
FALSIFIERS: turbine capacity ramp slips, project cancellations, margin dilution, AI/payback-driven power-project deferrals.
STATE: strongest integrated generation + grid bottleneck evidence in the physical cohort.

### ETN
FACT: Q2 sales +21%, organic +14%; Electrical Americas rolling orders +41%; Electrical Global +33%; electrical backlog +43% YoY; FCF $874M. Data centers are a key driver but demand is broad, improving resilience.
FALSIFIERS: backlog normalization, acquisition integration, price/cost reversal, electrical capacity overshoot.
STATE: core physical bottleneck compounder.

### PWR
FACT: Q2 revenue $9.56B vs $6.77B; FCF about $0.89B; total backlog $53.4B and Electric backlog $43.8B; full-year expectations raised. Skilled workforce and self-perform capability are scarce execution assets.
FALSIFIERS: project execution, labor inflation, acquisition leverage/integration, utility/customer timing.
STATE: one of the cleanest ways to monetize grid/transmission construction scarcity.

### VRT
FACT: Q2 sales $3.274B +24%; adjusted operating profit +51%; adjusted margin 22.6%; adjusted FCF $925M; full-year organic growth guide 30%-32%. This is no longer narrative-only cooling exposure: revenue, margins and cash conversion are visible.
FALSIFIERS: hyperscaler concentration, accelerated capacity expansion causing oversupply, thermal architecture shifts, valuation compression.
STATE: core cooling/power bottleneck.

### FIX
FACT: Q2 revenue $3.27B vs $2.17B; EPS +92%; quarterly operating cash flow $1.14B; backlog $14.06B vs $8.12B one year earlier. Skilled mechanical/electrical execution is a real constraint.
FALSIFIERS: backlog cancellation, labor scarcity turning from moat into margin pressure, customer/project concentration, valuation.
STATE: exceptionally strong Economic Proof.

### SU.PA
FACT: Schneider raised 2026 revenue growth guidance to 10%-13% and adjusted EBITA growth to 14%-19%; H1 adjusted EBITA €4.09B beat company-provided consensus. It supplies cooling, racks and critical power distribution and has broader end-market diversification.
FALSIFIERS: FX, Middle East/supply-chain disruption, data-center growth normalization, integration/capital allocation.
STATE: institutional-quality, diversified physical bottleneck leader.

### EME
FACT: Q2 revenue $5.15B +19.8%; operating margin 10.6%; EPS +34.8%; RPO $17.14B +43.9%; revenue and EPS guidance raised. Network/communications led by data centers remains a major demand contributor.
FALSIFIERS: labor/project execution, booking normalization, margin reversion, customer/geographic concentration.
STATE: high-quality skilled-trades capture.

### GNRC
FACT: Q2 C&I sales +29%; FCF $63M vs $14M; data-center backlog about $1.6B, including nearly $700M committed with one hyperscaler for 2027; second hyperscale agreement not yet included. Reuters reports $250M of planned factory investment through 2027.
FALSIFIERS: capacity-ramp execution, hyperscaler concentration, lower-margin C&I mix, weak residential business, cancellation/renegotiation risk.
STATE: highest inflection/asymmetry in the deep cohort, but materially more fragile than ETN/PWR/GEV/VRT.

## Industry-level evidence
- Reuters, 2026-08-19: data-center demand is propagating through generators, cooling, transformers, machinery, cabling and prefabricated structures; Wood Mackenzie estimates the U.S. data-center electrical-equipment market could rise from about $33B in 2025 to $66B in 2030.
- Reuters, 2026-07/08: transformer and high-voltage equipment lead times remain measured in years; some GSU transformer lead times exceeded 160 weeks.
- Reuters Breakingviews, 2026-03-26: Big Tech AI CAPEX can be constrained by grid connections, skilled labor, turbines, transformers and cooling; physical delay/overinvestment is also the principal systemic falsifier.
