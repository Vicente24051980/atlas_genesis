## 7. Earnings timing architecture — new RFCs

A major development in the thread was the creation of a pre/post earnings learning system designed to “get ahead” without turning ATLAS into a prediction engine.

### RFC-PEV-001 — Post-Earnings Validation Ω

Purpose: measure whether the market validates and retains a fundamental improvement after results.

**Flow:** Results → Guidance → Reaction → Persistence.

#### PEV Ω components

- EPS Surprise Ω — 15%
- Revenue Surprise Ω — 15%
- Guidance Revision Ω — 25%
- Earnings Reaction Ω — 15%
- Gap Retention Ω — 20%
- Estimate Revisions Ω — 10%

#### Gap Retention Ω

Measures how much of the post-earnings repricing remains after roughly 5–10 sessions.

Example discussed: Coca-Cola.

- Pre-earnings close: $84.07
- Post-earnings close: $88.27
- Subsequent high: $89.08
- Later close: $87.03

Initial jump ≈ +5.0%.  
Retained gain ≈ +3.52%.  
Retention ≈ 70%.

Interpretation: the market did not merely react for a few hours; a significant portion of the repricing persisted.

#### Fundamental Confirmation Gate Ω

PEV is considered confirmed only when at least 2 of 4 are true:

1. EPS > consensus.
2. Revenue > consensus.
3. Guidance maintained/raised.
4. Post-report estimates do not deteriorate.

If not, PEV is price movement, not evidence.

#### Earnings Quality Signal Ω

- A+ — Structural Beat
- A — Confirmed Beat
- B — Beat not confirmed
- C — Neutral
- D — Warning
- F — Structural deterioration

Coca-Cola was used as an “A — Confirmed Beat” example.

### RFC-PEA-001 — Pre-Earnings Anticipation Ω

Purpose: estimate the probability of positive/negative surprise before earnings by using signals that exist before the report.

PEA Ω is not designed to guess the result. It measures evidence drift.

#### PEA Ω components

- Estimate Revisions Ω — 20%
- Guidance Drift Ω — 15%
- Peer Read-Through Ω — 15%
- Demand / Backlog Ω — 15%
- Margin Setup Ω — 10%
- Relative Strength Ω — 10%
- Options / Expectations Ω — 5%
- Management Credibility Ω — 10%

#### Highest-value signals identified

1. Estimate revisions.
2. Peer read-through.
3. Guidance consistency.

#### Evidence Gate Ω

PEA cannot exceed 85 unless at least three independent signals are aligned.

A rising stock price alone is not sufficient evidence.

### Expectations Gap Ω

Critical distinction:

> Strong business ≠ good earnings trade.

The model compares fundamental improvement with expectations already embedded in the price.

A company can deliver excellent results and still fall if expectations were even higher.

AMD was discussed as the archetype: strong growth, but an expectations gap that could still lead to a negative price reaction.

### Earnings Opportunity Ω

Before earnings:

PEA Ω + Expectations Gap Ω + Valuation Ω → Earnings Opportunity Ω.

Possible outputs:

- Anticipatory buy.
- Wait for results.
- Buy post-beat.
- Avoid.
- Reduce risk.

### Learning Loop Ω

Every quarter store:

PEA before → actual result → PEV after → model error.

Over time, ATLAS should learn:

- which companies guide conservatively;
- which consistently beat;
- which sell the rumor;
- which preserve earnings gaps;
- which reverse after the first reaction.

This was identified as a potentially durable analytical edge because the system learns company-specific behavior rather than treating all earnings identically.

## 21. Atlas Conspiraciones — epistemic protocol

A strict separation was repeatedly required:

### Facts
Observable/verifiable events.
