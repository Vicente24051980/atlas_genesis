## Most important discoveries outside the current 49

### Thomson Reuters (TRI)
A high-quality, lower-capex receiver candidate. Q2 2026 recurring revenue growth in Legal and Corporates was explicitly driven by Westlaw and CoCounsel, and CoCounsel has reached one million professionals. This is stronger economic evidence than generic 'AI-enabled' marketing because the company links named AI products to recurring revenue growth.

Primary evidence:
- https://ir.thomsonreuters.com/news-releases/news-release-details/thomson-reuters-reports-second-quarter-2026-results
- https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en/press-releases/2026/february/one-million-professionals-turn-to-cocounsel-as-thomson-reuters-scales-ai-for-regulated-industries

### Palantir (PLTR)
One of the clearest high-growth examples of enterprise/government AI scaling beyond pilots. Q2 revenue rose 93% and management raised annual guidance again. Operational proof is strong; portfolio suitability is a separate issue because valuation and volatility can invalidate an otherwise excellent business candidate.

Corroboration:
- https://www.reuters.com/technology/palantir-raises-annual-revenue-forecast-strong-demand-us-government-commercial-2026-08-03/

### Snowflake (SNOW)
A data-layer receiver with increasing AI usage. Management raised product-revenue guidance citing meaningful uplift from AI capabilities and deeper AWS integration around agentic AI. It remains T1/T2 candidate rather than strict T2 because AI revenue is not separately recognized.

Corroboration:
- https://www.reuters.com/business/snowflake-raises-annual-product-revenue-forecast-enterprises-ramp-up-ai-2026-05-27/

### SAP (SAP)
Potential European system-of-record receiver. Cloud revenue rose 24% at constant currency and Cloud ERP Suite 27%; the economic advantage is embedded enterprise data/workflow. Current proof is still adoption/platform-level because AI-specific revenue is not separately disclosed and AI/data acquisitions are dilutive to near-term profit.

Primary evidence:
- https://news.sap.com/2026/07/sap-announces-q2-and-half-year-2026-results/

### Oracle (ORCL)
A real AI/cloud revenue receiver but currently a weaker portfolio fit under low-volatility objectives because the infrastructure build is extremely capital intensive. FY26 cloud infrastructure grew 77%, but capex reached roughly 174% of operating cash flow and the company plans substantial financing. Must pass CFQ + Financed Demand + Cash Reality before any operational promotion.

Primary/corroborating evidence:
- https://investor.oracle.com/investor-news/news-details/2026/Oracle-Announces-Record-Q4-and-FY-2026-Results-Driven-by-Cloud-Infrastructure--Cloud-Applications/
- https://www.reuters.com/business/ai-investment-boom-puts-big-techs-free-cash-flow-under-pressure-2026-07-22/
