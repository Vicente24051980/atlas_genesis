# DUAL PERSISTENCE LAW Ω — GitHub + Notion

**Status:** CANONICAL · INVIOLABLE  
**Effective date:** 2026-08-12  
**Scope:** ALL ATLAS branches, not only Financial.

## Supreme rule

Every material ATLAS input, information item, research finding, evidence record, decision, rule, engine, algorithm, universe change, portfolio/watchlist change, thesis update, health update, architecture change, workflow change, implementation, audit, correction or canonical amendment must be persisted in **both GitHub and Notion**.

**GitHub = technical/versioned source of truth.**  
**Notion = operational/documentary mirror.**

A chat-only update is incomplete and must not be treated as fully implemented.

## Mandatory execution rule

For every material ATLAS change or information intake:

1. Validate/classify the information under Evidence Integrity Ω.
2. Persist the technical/versioned record in GitHub.
3. Mirror the operational/documentary record in Notion.
4. Keep both records semantically aligned.
5. If either persistence step fails, mark the operation **PERSISTENCE_INCOMPLETE** and do not claim full implementation.

## Coverage

This law applies to, at minimum:

- ATLAS Financial / investments
- Portfolio and Watchlist
- Quality, Growth, Valuation, Risk, GREEN CONTINUITY, Bottom Detection, Recovery, Rotation, CAPEX and specialized engines
- Market/news/research information
- Health and personal tracking stored within ATLAS
- Gemelo Digital / Biblioteca Atlas
- Conspiraciones Atlas and research hypotheses
- App, API, mobile and architecture changes
- Governance, canon, rules and decision logs

## Exceptions

Only an explicit later instruction from Vicente may suspend or amend this law. Silence, convenience, missing time, chat context, or a secondary tool limitation do not constitute an exception.

## Integrity statement

**INFORMATION OR CHANGE NOT PRESENT IN BOTH GITHUB AND NOTION = NOT FULLY PERSISTED.**
