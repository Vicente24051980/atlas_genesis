# Life ROI Ω / Personal Capital Allocation Ω v1

Status: CANONICAL MODULE
Date: 2026-08-12
Scope: Personal finance, health/time allocation, opportunity design, risk protection
Source context: User-provided summary of Richard Gracia video "INVIERTE en estas 10 cosas en cuanto tengas DINERO". This module is separate from ATLAS Ω stock-selection engines.

## 1. Mission

Life ROI Ω governs non-market capital allocation: spending and investing money to improve health, time, clarity, relationships, capability, opportunity and protection against ruin.

Canonical sentence:

> Money is not only invested in financial assets; it should also buy health, time, mental clarity and protection against irreversible errors.

## 2. Separation rule

Life ROI Ω is not a stock-picking engine and must not overwrite Principal Ω, Green Continuity Ω, Money Rotation Ω, Good Companies Cheap Ω, Historical Dislocation Ω or any market engine.

It answers a different question:

- Market engines: where should capital compound financially?
- Life ROI Ω: where should spending increase human capacity, reduce fragility or protect the life that owns the portfolio?

## 3. Five-question gate before spending

A discretionary spend is favored only if one or more answers are clearly yes:

1. Does this improve health?
2. Does this return time?
3. Does this improve relationships or meaningful experiences?
4. Can this create income, skill, opportunity or professional leverage?
5. Will this actually be used often enough to justify the cost?

If all five answers are no, default classification is CONSUMPTION / AVOID.

## 4. Priority stack

### Tier 1 — Health and recovery

Highest priority when relevant.

Includes:

- Sleep quality: mattress, pillow, temperature, light, noise control.
- Physical recovery: physiotherapy, rehabilitation, prevention.
- Real food and nutrient quality.
- Dental, medical and preventive reviews.
- Exercise infrastructure when physically cleared.

Rationale: bad health taxes every financial and personal decision.

### Tier 2 — Time buyback

Favored when a payment removes recurring low-value friction.

Includes:

- Delegating repetitive domestic tasks.
- Reducing commute and logistics drag.
- Tools that remove daily workflow friction.
- Services that prevent energy leakage.

Rule: buy time when the cost is materially below the value of the recovered time or when the task meaningfully drains health/attention.

### Tier 3 — Work tools / cost-per-use

Buy quality when daily use is high and durability/productivity justify cost.

Examples:

- Ergonomic chair.
- Better monitor.
- Reliable mobile / laptop setup.
- Input devices that reduce pain or friction.
- Durable buy-it-for-life items.

Decision metric: cost per use + productivity gain + reduced friction.

### Tier 4 — Capability and opportunity

Invest in learning only when linked to usable leverage.

Favored areas:

- Sales.
- Negotiation.
- Marketing.
- Programming / automation.
- Finance / tax literacy.
- High-quality mentorship with evidence of fit.

Avoid: course accumulation, hype credentials and learning that does not change behavior or income capacity.

### Tier 5 — Relationships, experiences and hobbies

Approved when they create lasting memory, bonding, discipline or restoration.

Rules:

- Experiences with family or close relationships can have high Life ROI even when financial ROI is zero.
- Hobbies do not need to be monetized.
- Avoid consumption that only creates short-lived stimulation or status signaling.

### Tier 6 — Tax and legal optimization

High priority once income, business activity or portfolio complexity justifies professional advice.

Includes:

- Tax planning.
- Legal structure.
- Documentation hygiene.
- Avoiding fines and preventable mistakes.

### Tier 7 — Catastrophic risk protection

Insurance is favored when it transfers risks that could destroy years of progress.

Examples:

- Home / fire.
- Disability / incapacity.
- Civil liability.
- Life insurance when dependents exist.
- Health cover where relevant.

Rule: insure ruin risks; self-insure small inconveniences.

## 5. Classification system

Each spend can be tagged:

- GREEN — High Life ROI: health/time/protection/capability impact is clear.
- YELLOW — Conditional: useful but depends on price, frequency of use or timing.
- RED — Avoid: status, impulse, low use, no strategic value.

Optional score 0-100:

- Health impact: 25
- Time returned: 20
- Capability / income leverage: 20
- Relationship / experience value: 15
- Protection against downside: 15
- Actual usage probability: 5

## 7. Interaction with ATLAS portfolio

Life ROI Ω can justify holding back some spending/investment capacity for health, time or protection, but it does not change stock BUY/SELL signals by itself.

Portfolio construction remains governed by market engines. Life ROI Ω governs personal capital allocation outside the securities selection layer.

## 8. Decision safety

- Do not call every purchase an investment.
- Do not let austerity damage health, time or relationships.
- Do not let comfort spending disguise itself as ROI without evidence of use.
- Spending that prevents a severe downside can have higher ROI than chasing incremental market return.
