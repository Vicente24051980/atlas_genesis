## Change request

Promote a unified macro-regime layer that forces ATLAS Ω to evaluate global duration, Japanese repatriation economics, Treasury debt-management/liquidity plumbing, energy-to-inflation transmission, gold/real-asset signals and equity rotation under strict evidence discipline.

## New active gates

1. `JAPAN CAPITAL REPATRIATION GATE Ω`
2. `TREASURY LIQUIDITY / MANAGEMENT GATE Ω`
3. `MACRO DISCOUNT-RATE GATE Ω`
4. `ENERGY-INFLATION PASS-THROUGH Ω`
5. `GOLD / REAL-ASSET SIGNAL Ω`
6. `NOTIFICATION GATE Ω` for macro regime changes

## Mandatory monitored set

UST 10Y/30Y; JGB 10Y/30Y; USDJPY; DXY; Brent/WTI; diesel/distillates and crack spreads; Nasdaq-100/S&P 500 futures; PCE/CPI and relevant inflation measures; TGA; Treasury buybacks; issuance composition; Fed balance sheet/reserves; Japan Treasury holdings/flows when available; gold; geopolitics; breadth/sector rotation.

## New canonical laws

- `CORRELATION != CAUSATION`.
- `INTRADAY MOVE != REGIME CHANGE`.
- `JGB YIELD UP != JAPANESE UST SELLING`.
- `TREASURY BUYBACK != QE`.
- `TGA CHANGE != MONETARY POLICY BY ITSELF`.
- `CRUDE DOWN != REFINED-PRODUCT INFLATION CLEARED`.
- `GOLD UP != BUY GOLD`.
- Macro deterioration changes discount-rate/risk posture before it changes company fundamental state.

## Japan gate requirement

Repatriation may be considered economically plausible from JGB/UST/FX hedge economics, but **confirmed repatriation pressure requires direct holdings/flow evidence plus consistent market transmission**. TIC or equivalent official data is preferred. ATLAS must never fabricate selling from USDJPY or JGB moves.

## Treasury gate requirement

Treasury buybacks are classified as debt/liquidity management. Their effects on off-the-run liquidity, duration supply, TGA and reserve plumbing must be separated from Federal Reserve balance-sheet policy. QE requires central-bank asset/reserve expansion, not Treasury liability management alone.

## Energy gate requirement

Crude and refined products must be analyzed separately. Diesel/distillates and crack spreads receive explicit priority as potential cost pass-through channels into freight, logistics, agriculture, industry and ultimately CPI/PCE/margins.

## Discount-rate requirement

Translate rates/inflation/FX/energy into WACC and multiple pressure for long-duration assets. AI Economic Proof may remain positive while Macro Discount Rate deteriorates; no automatic fundamental falsification is allowed.

## Notification policy

User-facing notification only when evidence supports:

- `MATERIAL_REGIME_CHANGE`,
- `MATERIAL_DETERIORATION`, or
- `CLEAR_RELIEF`.

Otherwise state is internally `NO_MATERIAL_CHANGE / NO_NOTIFICATION`.

Every notification must state changed variables, causal chain, alternative explanations, evidence grade, gate transitions and portfolio effect/action.

## Portfolio authority

This MCR does not create BUY/SELL authority. Portfolio changes remain subordinate to Evidence Integrity, Falsifier Veto, Valuation, Competition for Capital and Portfolio Construction Ω.

## Integration

`Sovereign Liquidity Plumbing / Global Liquidity Transmission -> GLOBAL MACRO REGIME GATES Ω -> Cross-Engine Synthesis -> Portfolio Construction -> Decision Safety Gate`.
