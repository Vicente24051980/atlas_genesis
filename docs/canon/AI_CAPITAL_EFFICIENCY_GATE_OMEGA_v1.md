# AI CAPITAL EFFICIENCY GATE Ω v1.0

Status: canonical transversal falsification gate
Date: 2026-08-10

## Mission
Determine whether AI-related incremental investment creates economic value rather than merely increasing revenue, capacity or narrative exposure.

## Constitutional rule
AI growth is not evidence of value creation. Evidence must show that incremental capital earns an adequate return after CAPEX, financing, dilution and competitive pass-through.

## Mandatory sequence
1. CAPEX growth.
2. Incremental revenue attributable to the investment.
3. Incremental NOPAT.
4. Marginal ROIC = incremental NOPAT / incremental invested capital.
5. Compare Marginal ROIC with WACC.
6. Verify FCF/share after CAPEX.
7. Verify debt, interest burden and dilution used to finance growth.
8. Test competitive pass-through: cost savings available to all competitors may accrue to customers rather than shareholders.
9. Classify VALUE_CREATION / NEUTRAL / VALUE_DESTRUCTION / INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.

## Decision safety
- VALUE_CREATION: PASS only when Marginal ROIC exceeds WACC by an adequate margin and financing/FCF falsifiers are absent.
- NEUTRAL: REVIEW. Growth may be real without sufficient economic value creation.
- VALUE_DESTRUCTION: BLOCK any BUY whose thesis materially depends on the AI investment until new evidence removes the falsifier.
- INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE: never infer a PASS.

This gate does not create BUY signals and does not average scores across engines. It is a transversal falsifier used by Principal Ω and any specialized engine whose thesis materially depends on AI CAPEX.

## Evidence integrity
Social media, YouTube and analyst commentary are ClaimRecords/discovery inputs only. Financial values used by the gate require traceable evidence and temporal normalization. MARKET_CAP_CHANGE, PRICE_RETURN and narrative enthusiasm are not capital-return evidence.
