## 1. Trading 212 24/5 operating rule

ATLAS Ω adopts the following broker rule for Vicente's Trading 212 workflow:

**Trading 212 24/5 prices should remain OFF by default for portfolio construction.**

Rationale:

- 24/5 prices can create false urgency through pre-market, after-hours and overnight moves.
- Extended-hours liquidity can be thin, spreads can widen and first reactions can reverse once regular-session volume enters.
- ATLAS Ω portfolio construction is a 3-6 year evidence workflow, not an extended-hours impulse workflow.
- Disabling 24/5 display reduces notification noise, psychological pressure and bad execution risk.

Canonical sentence:

> 24/5 is a radar, not a decision engine. If visible, it informs; it does not decide. Regular-session evidence, volume and limit-price discipline govern execution.

## 2. Broker Ω execution guardrail

Default execution stance:

- Regular market hours are the preferred window for portfolio construction.
- Extended-hours movement is not sufficient evidence for BUY or SELL.
- No market orders should be placed during extended-hours sessions.
- Any exceptional extended-hours action requires a limit order, a known catalyst, acceptable spread and explicit thesis/falsifier check.
- A missed upside move is an opportunity cost; a bad extended-hours fill is real portfolio damage.

Operational default for Trading 212 mobile:

- `Precios 24/5`: OFF.
- Volatility alerts: allowed only at high thresholds to avoid noise.
- Price alerts: allowed when attached to planned levels, not to chase momentum.
