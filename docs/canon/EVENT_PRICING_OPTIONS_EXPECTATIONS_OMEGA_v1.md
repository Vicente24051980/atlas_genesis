# EVENT PRICING / OPTIONS EXPECTATIONS Ω v1.0

Date: 2026-08-10
Status: CANONICAL INDEPENDENT TACTICAL ENGINE
Scope: ATLAS Ω ENTERPRISE — event expectations, options pricing and post-event attribution

## Mission

Measure what the market is pricing before a known event and compare it with what actually occurs, without confusing options positioning, volatility or price reaction with business quality or structural thesis evidence.

## Independence

EVENT PRICING / OPTIONS EXPECTATIONS Ω is independent from GREEN CONTINUITY Ω, Business Quality Ω, Good Companies Cheap Ω, Historical Dislocation / Burry Ω, Money Rotation Ω and Energy Rotation Ω.

It must never overwrite another engine's BUY/HOLD/WATCH/SELL state. Its outputs are tactical context and event evidence only.

## Pre-event record

When reliable data exist, capture:

- ticker and canonical identifier;
- event type and timestamp;
- spot price and synchronized market cut;
- ATM-straddle implied move;
- implied price range;
- put wall;
- hedge/gamma pivot when available;
- call wall;
- historical average absolute post-event move;
- historical maximum post-event move;
- implied-versus-history ratio;
- expiry used;
- source, capturedAt and asOf.

Put/call/hedge walls are positioning-derived levels, not guaranteed support or resistance.

## Post-event record

Capture:

- actual one-day and event-window price move;
- implied range breach/pass;
- revenue/EPS/guidance surprise when applicable;
- operating KPI surprise;
- macro surprise when applicable;
- yields/USD/index reaction when material;
- volatility repricing;
- attribution: FUNDAMENTAL, MACRO_RATES, POSITIONING_VOLATILITY, MIXED or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.

## Evidence rule

Options-derived data from aggregators or tactical platforms are secondary evidence unless reconciled with sufficiently reliable market data. They cannot alone change Business Quality, structural conviction or create a structural BUY/SELL.

A market rally caused primarily by falling yields is classified MARKET/VALUATION EFFECT. It is not automatically evidence that the underlying AI or business thesis improved.

# AI CAPITAL EFFICIENCY CHECKPOINT Ω

For AI, cloud, datacenter, semiconductor and adjacent infrastructure earnings, evaluate the economic chain:

`AI revenue growth -> incremental CAPEX -> depreciation -> financing cost -> FCF -> incremental ROIC / economic return`

Required checkpoints where disclosed:

- AI-related revenue and growth;
- backlog/RPO/orders and conversion quality;
- CAPEX and CAPEX growth;
- depreciation/amortization trajectory;
- gross and operating margin direction;
- FCF and FCF margin;
- net debt / liquidity / financing cost;
- incremental revenue per incremental CAPEX;
- incremental FCF per incremental CAPEX;
- evidence of utilization, pricing power and customer concentration;
- management guidance and primary-source evidence.

High AI growth with deteriorating capital efficiency must be flagged. High CAPEX is not itself a falsifier; the question is whether reinvestment creates durable economic value.

## Event routing

EVENT PRICING Ω may feed observations into CAPEX Productivity Ω, Risk Ω, Money Rotation Ω and Decision Log Ω, but only validated fundamental evidence may alter their structural conclusions.

## Mobile-first output

Minimum event card:

`TICKER | EVENT | SPOT | IMPLIED MOVE | RANGE | PUT WALL | PIVOT | CALL WALL | HIST AVG | ACTUAL MOVE | ATTRIBUTION | AI CAPEX EFFICIENCY FLAG`

Keep the card compact and suitable for Android/mobile use.
