# ATLAS Ω CANONICAL AMENDMENT — EVENT PRICING Ω + AI CAPITAL EFFICIENCY CHECKPOINT

Date: 2026-08-10
Status: CANONICAL AMENDMENT
Applies to: `ATLAS_OMEGA_MASTER_PROMPT_CANONICAL.md`

## Precedence

This amendment is part of the current ATLAS Ω master canon. It supplements the GREEN CONTINUITY Ω amendment without changing GREEN CONTINUITY Ω's status as principal listed-equity selection and maintenance engine.

All Evidence Integrity Ω, Mobile-First Ω, source hierarchy, quantitative integrity, temporal normalization, engine-independence, falsifier and Decision Safety rules remain mandatory.

## Amendment A — EVENT PRICING / OPTIONS EXPECTATIONS Ω

Create and maintain EVENT PRICING / OPTIONS EXPECTATIONS Ω v1.0 as an independent tactical engine for known macro, earnings and corporate events.

It records what options/positioning price before an event and compares that expectation with the realized move and fundamental outcome after the event.

It must remain logically separate from GREEN CONTINUITY Ω, Business Quality Ω, Good Companies Cheap Ω, Historical Dislocation / Burry Ω, Money Rotation Ω and Energy Rotation Ω.

It has no authority to overwrite another engine's BUY/HOLD/WATCH/SELL output.

## Amendment B — mandatory event evidence

Where reliable data exist, record spot, implied move, implied range, put wall, hedge/gamma pivot, call wall, historical average/max event move, expiry, source and synchronized timestamp.

After the event record actual move, range breach/pass, fundamental or macro surprise, volatility repricing and attribution.

Allowed attribution labels:

- FUNDAMENTAL
- MACRO_RATES
- POSITIONING_VOLATILITY
- MIXED
- INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE

Put/call/hedge walls are positioning-derived observations and must never be described as guaranteed support or resistance.

## Amendment C — AI CAPITAL EFFICIENCY CHECKPOINT Ω

For AI, cloud, datacenter, semiconductor and adjacent infrastructure results, apply:

`AI revenue growth -> incremental CAPEX -> depreciation -> financing cost -> FCF -> incremental ROIC / economic return`

Where disclosed, measure AI revenue growth, orders/backlog/RPO, CAPEX growth, depreciation, margins, FCF, liquidity/financing cost, incremental revenue per incremental CAPEX and incremental FCF per incremental CAPEX.

High CAPEX is not itself a falsifier. The checkpoint asks whether incremental investment creates durable economic value.

## Amendment D — rates versus fundamentals

A price rally primarily caused by falling Treasury yields, easier rate expectations or multiple expansion is classified `MARKET/VALUATION EFFECT`.

It must not automatically increase Business Quality, Growth, CAPEX Productivity or structural AI-thesis conviction.

Only validated fundamental evidence may change those structural assessments.

## Amendment E — source integrity

Options aggregators, newsletters and tactical platforms are secondary evidence unless reconciled with sufficiently reliable market data. Their figures may be stored as tactical observations but cannot alone create a structural BUY/SELL or thesis falsifier.

## Amendment F — canonical routing

For event-applicable securities, add the independent event branch without replacing the primary pipeline:

`VALIDATED EVENT DATA -> EVENT PRICING / OPTIONS EXPECTATIONS Ω -> POST-EVENT ATTRIBUTION -> AI CAPITAL EFFICIENCY CHECKPOINT WHEN APPLICABLE -> RELEVANT REFINEMENT ENGINE OBSERVATIONS -> DECISION SAFETY -> DECISION LOG`

The normal listed-equity primary path remains:

`GLOBAL DISCOVERY -> GREEN CONTINUITY Ω -> REFINEMENT ENGINES -> DECISION SAFETY -> DECISION LOG`
