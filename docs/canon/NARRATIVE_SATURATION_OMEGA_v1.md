# NARRATIVE SATURATION Ω v1.0

Status: canonical higher-layer indicator
Date: 2026-08-09
Scope: Conspiraciones Atlas Ω + MONEY ROTATION Ω + HISTORICAL DISLOCATION Ω

## Mission

Detect when a macro, monetary or market narrative has reached systemic visibility through a major financial/publication cover or lead story, then test whether that narrative is already crowded, already priced, or near a regime-change point.

This indicator is not a prediction engine and never produces BUY, SELL or REDUCE by itself.

## Core Rule

When a major publication places an extreme macro narrative on the cover, ATLAS must ask:

1. How much of this story has already happened?
2. How much is already embedded in price, positioning and flows?
3. Does the cover mark early discovery, contemporaneous stress, or late-stage narrative saturation?
4. What do USD, gold, oil, rates, credit, inflation, breadth and sector flows say at the same time?

## Canonical Pattern

`TREND -> EXTREME -> COVER -> POSSIBLE REGIME RESPONSE`

The cover is treated as a narrative/stress sensor, not proof that the next event is known in advance.

## Evidence Classes

| Class | Meaning | Handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fact | Cover date, title, published article, official macro data | Can be stored as evidence |
| Evidence | Primary/official data from IMF, ECB, Fed, Treasury, EIA, IEA, central banks, regulators | Can activate watch states |
| Interpretation | Symbolic reading or market-regime diagnosis | Preserve with attribution |
| Hypothesis | Cover-saturation or contrarian-cover claim | Test prospectively |
| Speculation | Hidden-code or owner-intent claim without documents | Preserve only as speculation |

## Routing

Relevant evidence must route to:

- `CONSPIRACIONES_ATLAS`
- `MONEY_ROTATION_OMEGA`
- `HISTORICAL_DISLOCATION_OMEGA`

It must also remain available to `RISK_OMEGA` and `CATALYSTS_OMEGA` only when independent macro evidence supports a risk/catalyst state.

## Phoenix 2026 Out-of-Sample Case

The Economist cover dated 2026-08-08, "The Global Currency Beef / Lessons from 40 years of the Big Mac Index", is frozen as the first prospective case.

### Baseline Verdict

- No solid evidence of a secret calendar that predicts concrete events.
- The 1988 Phoenix cover failed literally as a world retail currency by roughly 2018.
- The 1988 Phoenix cover was structurally closer on monetary architecture: euro, SDR expansion, RMB in SDR, CBDC/stablecoin/payment-rail development.
- The 2026 Big Mac cover is initially explained by the 40th anniversary of the Big Mac Index.
- The cover becomes analytically interesting only if later monetary signals match predefined confirmers without retrospective reinterpretation.

### Frozen Confirmers / Falsifiers

| Future signal | Reading |
| --- | --- |
| USD reserve share falls below 55% persistently through real reserve selling | Fragmentation accelerates |
| RMB rises clearly from around 2% of global reserves | China gains reserve relevance |
| BRICS formally announces a common currency or monetary unit | Major regime signal |
| BRICS continues only local-currency/payment-rail work | Fragmentation, not Phoenix |
| Central banks continue massive physical gold accumulation | Reserve diversification continues |
| Repeated USD down + UST yields up + gold up | US reserve-asset confidence stress |
| Major energy contracts migrate away from USD settlement | Petrodollar stress signal |
| Extraordinary new SDR allocation | Phoenix-structural signal |
| SDR obtains private or retail usage | Historic monetary signal |
| Transnational commercial monetary unit appears | Phoenix-near signal |
| None of the above occurs | Cover remains anniversary + FX context |
| 2026-08-12 has no specific monetary event | No reinterpretation of eclipse claims |

## Market Use

NARRATIVE SATURATION Ω can raise review priority in MONEY ROTATION Ω and HISTORICAL DISLOCATION Ω.

It must never:

- treat a cover as primary evidence of future events;
- infer owner intent without documents;
- promote symbolic readings into facts;
- override valuation, quality, thesis falsifiers or source hierarchy;
- trigger portfolio action without independent evidence.
