## Mission

Identify where AI economic value is migrating and which listed companies can retain that value after compute, inference, supervision, security, financing and capital costs.

ATLAS must answer four separate questions:

1. Who enables intelligence?
2. Who controls the bottleneck or interface?
3. Who owns the customer, data, workflow or distribution?
4. Who converts the activity into durable owner cash flow?

A company may occupy several layers. Layer membership is not proof of value capture.

## AI Value Migration

For every company and layer, record:

`CAPABILITY -> ADOPTION -> USAGE -> CUSTOMER ACCEPTANCE -> REVENUE -> GROSS PROFIT -> OPERATING PROFIT -> FCF -> ROIC`

Then record the leakage:

`CAPEX + DEPRECIATION + SBC/DILUTION + SUPERVISION + SECURITY + FINANCING + CUSTOMER_INCENTIVES`

The result is **Net AI Owner Economics**, not headline AI revenue.

Required states:

- `T0_NARRATIVE`
- `T1_ADOPTION`
- `T2_REVENUE_LINKAGE`
- `T3_MARGIN_LINKAGE`
- `T4_FCF_PROOF`
- `T5_DURABLE_ROIC`

## Cost per Intelligence

Track, when reliably measurable:

- cost per token;
- cost per task;
- quality-adjusted cost;
- latency;
- throughput;
- tokens or tasks per user;
- inference cost;
- supervision cost;
- security/containment cost;
- customer price;
- gross profit per task.

Rules:

- efficiency per task does not imply lower total compute;
- falling price can expand use through elasticity;
- benchmark claims are not owner economics;
- exact third-party benchmark figures remain UNKNOWN or DISCOVERY until reproducible and sourced;
- the economic question is contribution margin and incremental ROIC after all relevant costs.

## AI Stack Control

Assess control across:

`COMPUTE -> MODEL -> INFERENCE -> DATA -> WORKFLOW -> INTERFACE -> DISTRIBUTION`

For each control point ask:

- Is it scarce?
- Is it substitutable?
- Who pays?
- Who owns the relationship?
- Who controls permissions and identity?
- Can the customer multi-home?
- Is pricing power visible?
- Is the value captured by the company or passed to the next layer?

## Enterprise Agentic Interface

Separate:

**System-of-record owner**

- structured data;
- permissions;
- workflow;
- audit trail;
- customer history;
- switching costs.

**Agent-interface owner**

- intent;
- conversation;
- reasoning;
- discovery;
- orchestration;
- action execution.

Do not assume the interface wins. Require:

`assistant -> workflow -> action -> system-of-record update -> measurable customer value`

Pilots, announcements and beta access remain below scaled deployment.

## Apple Distribution Gate

AAPL is a mandatory candidate in C19 Tech Consumption / AI Distribution and must be compared with GOOG, MSFT, META and AMZN.

Classify Apple as:

`DEVICE_OS_ECOSYSTEM`

not as a pure hyperscaler.

Evaluate:

- installed active devices;
- hardware retention;
- OS distribution;
- silicon and on-device inference;
- personal context and identity;
- app and services ecosystem;
- AI feature adoption;
- paid conversion or retention;
- services/hardware monetization;
- CAPEX;
- FCF/share;
- ROIC;
- valuation and expectations.

Apple is a candidate for Competition for Capital, not an automatic portfolio addition.

## Custom Silicon Risk

Custom silicon is a standing hypothesis:

`AI_WORKLOADS_UP -> ASIC_ECONOMICS_IMPROVE -> CUSTOM_SILICON_DEPLOYMENT -> GPU_SHARE/PRICING/MIX`

It becomes material only after evidence of:

- production deployment;
- customer acceptance;
- utilization;
- workload share shift;
- pricing pressure;
- margin impact;
- FCF/ROIC impact.

Until then: `HYPOTHESIS / WATCH`, not a falsifier.

## Evidence filter

| Input | Default status | Can change portfolio? |
|---|---|---|

`Evidence Integrity -> Economic Proof -> Valuation -> Risk -> Falsification -> Competition for Capital -> Decision Safety Gate`
