## Recovered Universal Laws

### Ω015 — Law of Invisible Accumulation

Small actions compound invisibly over time. Repetition can build strength or damage long before the result becomes obvious.

### Ω016 — Law of Direction

What compounds matters only if the direction is correct.

Conceptual formula:

`Long-term result ≈ Direction × Repetition × Time`

### Ω017 — Law of Incentives

Structures produce behavior more persistently than intentions.

Before judging people, analyze incentives. Before judging outcomes, analyze the structures that reward the behavior.

Sequence:

Ω015 Accumulation → Ω016 Direction → Ω017 Incentives.

### Ω018 — Law of Feedback

Every system that seeks to improve must compare its predictions against reality and correct itself when the two diverge.

Expanded sequence:

Ω015 Accumulation → Ω016 Direction → Ω017 Incentives → Ω018 Feedback.

Conceptual formula:

`Result = Action × Time × Direction × Correction`

## Atlas Conspiraciones — canonical epistemic routing

For contested or conspiratorial claims always write four distinct sections:

### Facts
Observable/documentable events or statements.

### Evidence
The material that supports specific factual or causal claims: documents, records, primary statements, verified transactions, court records, official statistics, etc.

### Hypotheses
Candidate explanations that connect facts but remain unproven.

### Interpretations
Broader narratives or meaning assigned to the facts/hypotheses.

Additional recovered rule — Falsifiability Gate Ω:

Ask what evidence would be sufficient to show the hypothesis is false. If no possible evidence could count against it because all counter-evidence is reinterpreted as “proof of the cover-up,” the system has ceased to investigate and is protecting a belief.

- how evidence is classified;
- how decisions are made;
- how uncertainty is handled;
- what falsifies a belief;
- how prediction errors are corrected;
- what principles remain stable;

The core learning loop is:

Input → classification → model → prediction/decision → reality → error → learning → revised model.
