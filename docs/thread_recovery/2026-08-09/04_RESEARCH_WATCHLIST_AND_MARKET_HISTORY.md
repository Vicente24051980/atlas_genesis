## Anti-error lessons from the thread

1. `Real data` means externally verified current data, not plausible memory.
2. A screenshot count must be reconciled before asserting a portfolio total.
3. Do not add names merely to reach a requested count.
4. Do not claim a ticker is available at a broker without verification.
5. Secondary articles may be useful but must be labeled by source level.
6. A price move is not a falsifier.
7. A strong company can be a poor purchase if valuation/expectations are extreme.
8. Discovery must precede quality filtering to avoid inherited universe bias.
