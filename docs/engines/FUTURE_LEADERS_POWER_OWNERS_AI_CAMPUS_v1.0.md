# ATLAS Ω — POWER OWNERS / AI CAMPUS CONVERSION Ω v1.0

Status: ACTIVE
Date: 2026-08-16
Parent: Future Leaders / Asymmetric Growth Ω
Purpose: detect companies whose scarce asset is land + interconnection/power + permits + existing infrastructure and which can convert that asset into long-duration AI/HPC data-center cash flows before the transformation is fully priced.

## Canonical law
CONTRACTED REVENUE != ECONOMIC VALUE != FCF/SHARE.
MW available != MW contracted != MW constructed != MW energized != MW billed.
Price action is not fundamental evidence.

## Pipeline
1. Power Access Ω — utility power, interconnection, gas/on-site generation, redundancy.
2. Site Control Ω — land, permits, water/cooling, fiber, construction readiness.
3. Contract Proof Ω — customer identity/quality, MW, term, take-or-pay, escalators, termination clauses.
4. Construction Proof Ω — funded CAPEX, milestones, schedule, contractor/supply-chain risk.
5. Energization Proof Ω — delivered MW and commissioning.
6. Billing Proof Ω — revenue-producing MW and revenue/MW.
7. Unit Economics Ω — EBITDA/NOI per MW, margin, maintenance capex.
8. Funding Transmission Ω — project debt, corporate debt, interest, guarantees/backstops, refinancing, equity issuance/dilution.
9. FCF/Share Conversion Ω — operating cash flow less all attributable capex/financing burden divided by diluted shares.
10. Expectations Gate Ω — compare enterprise value/equity value with risk-adjusted value of contracted + pipeline capacity; penalize transformations already priced.
11. Concentration Ω — customer, geography, utility, technology and financing concentration.
12. Falsifier Gate Ω.

## Scoring (100)
- Contract / Customer Proof: 15
- Power + Site Scarcity: 15
- Construction / Energization Proof: 10
- Billing / Economic Proof: 15
- Unit Economics: 10
- Future Scale Potential: 10
- Funding & Dilution Quality: 10
- Customer/Asset Diversification: 5
- Expectations / Valuation Asymmetry: 10

Mandatory caps:
- No billing proof: Economic Proof component capped at 60/100.
- No committed funding for contracted build: total score capped at 79.
- Material going-concern/refinancing risk: ASYMMETRIC_WATCH maximum.
- Unresolved contract/customer claim without primary evidence: cannot be FUTURE_LEADER.

## States
- FUTURE_LEADER: >=85 and all mandatory gates pass.
- TOP_AUDIT: >=80 but one material funding/construction/valuation gate remains open.
- ASYMMETRIC_WATCH: future-scale potential high but economic conversion/funding not sufficiently proven.
- DISCOVERY: structural fit, insufficient proof.
- REJECT: falsifier or unattractive risk-adjusted FCF/share economics.

## Governance
Only primary evidence can change Conviction Ω. Secondary evidence may trigger research but remains PENDING PRIMARY VALIDATION. Duplicate contractual announcements must be consolidated rather than counted as independent proof. Contract value over many years must never be compared directly with current market capitalization without discounting, capex, financing, operating costs and dilution. All rankings are provisional until contemporaneous valuation and primary-source unit economics are reconciled.
