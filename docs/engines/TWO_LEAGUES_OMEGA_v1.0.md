# ATLAS Ω — Two-League Equity Architecture v1.0

Canonical distinction frozen: 2026-08-16.

## Law
Quality Ω != Expected Return Ω. Mature compounders and emerging future leaders MUST NOT compete in the same ranking.

## Engine A — Quality Compounders Ω
Mission: evaluate already-large, proven companies as long-duration portfolio anchors. Do not award a high expected-return score merely because Business Quality is exceptional.

Primary dimensions: moat durability; ROIC/FCF/share; reinvestment runway; balance sheet; earnings durability; capital allocation; valuation/expectations; structural risk.

Interpretation: a company can score Quality Ω 99 while Forward Return Ω is only 60–75 because much of its economic maturation and rerating has already occurred.

## Engine B — Future Leaders / Asymmetric Growth Ω
Mission: discover companies that are not yet giants but can become several times larger over 3–6 years, while explicitly measuring how much of that future is already priced.

Canonical scoring weights:
- Future Scale Potential: 25%
- Economic Proof: 20%
- Forward Asymmetry: 20%
- TAM / structural tailwind: 10%
- Incremental ROIC / FCF trajectory: 10%
- Moat Formation: 5%
- Balance / Funding: 5%
- Valuation / Expectations: 5%

Mandatory Expectations Gate Ω: extreme growth does not qualify automatically when valuation already requires near-perfect execution.

Pipeline: Discovery -> Economic Proof -> Future Scale -> Expectations -> CAPEX/FCF -> Dilution -> Moat Formation -> Forward Asymmetry -> Falsifiers -> Ranking.

States: FUTURE_LEADER; ASYMMETRIC_WATCH; QUALITY_BUT_PRICED; NARRATIVE.

No arbitrary market-cap ceiling. Size is contextual; the key variable is remaining economic growth relative to expectations embedded in enterprise value.

## Governance
- Scores across the two engines are NOT directly comparable.
- Price action alone is never Economic Proof.
- High historical return is not itself a sell signal, but maturity and expectations must reduce forward-return assumptions where warranted.
- Future Leaders must be audited for dilution, funding dependence and CAPEX-to-FCF/share conversion.
- Quality Compounders remain eligible as portfolio anchors even when they no longer offer multibagger-style asymmetry.
