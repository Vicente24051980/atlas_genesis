# BD-02 — Orontids → Bagratuni — Bridge Destruction Test

**Status:** CLOSED-UNRESOLVED / DOCUMENTARY GAP  
**Date:** 2026-09-06

## Frozen claim
`Orontids → Bagratuni` as a remote genealogical descent claim.

## Survival criterion
The bridge survives only if at least one argument connects the Orontid and Bagratuni endpoints without ultimately depending on Toumanoff or on a derivative chain that returns to the same reconstruction.

## Evidence audit
1. **Toumanoff / Encyclopaedia Iranica (BAGRATIDS)** treats the Bagratuni as an Orontid offshoot and proposes Bagadates, strategos of Tigranes the Great in 83–69 BCE, as the earliest possible member. The same synthesis places an uninterrupted Bagratuni genealogy only from approximately 555 onward. The remote derivation therefore remains reconstructive rather than a continuous ancient pedigree.
2. **Facella / ORONTIDS** independently strengthens Orontid history and the western/Sophene line but does not provide a genealogical bridge from the Orontids to the Christian-period Bagratuni.
3. **Armenia and Iran ii / Encyclopaedia Iranica** explicitly states that the surviving evidence is insufficient to support descent of the leading Armenian families of the Christian period from the Orontids.
4. **Movsēs Khorenatsi is not independent support for an Orontid origin.** His prestigious Bagratuni ancestry is Hebrew/Davidic, not Orontid. His dating is disputed and Garsoïan documents a strong pro-Bagratuni orientation together with chronological/historical distortions and systematic downgrading of the rival Mamikonean house.
5. Therefore, a deep prestigious Bagratuni genealogy in Khorenatsi belongs first in `claims_descent_from` / dynastic-legitimation analysis, not as corroboration of Toumanoff's Orontid reconstruction.

## AUTHOR-REMOVAL TEST
Removing Khorenatsi does **not** destroy or rescue the Orontid hypothesis because the Orontid claim is mainly a modern historiographical reconstruction. It does, however, remove a major source of extraordinary ancestral prestige assigned to Bagratuni.

Result:
`NARRATIVE_CAPTURE_RISK = MATERIAL`
for any aggregate graph that treats prestigious remote ancestry as equivalent to demonstrated descent.
