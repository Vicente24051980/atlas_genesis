# BD-02 — Orontids → Bagratuni — Bridge Destruction Test

**Status:** CLOSED-UNRESOLVED / DOCUMENTARY GAP  
**Date:** 2026-09-06

## Frozen claim
`Orontids → Bagratuni` as remote genealogical descent.

## Destructive result
No independent recoverable pedigree connects the Orontids to the historically visible Bagratuni. Toumanoff's Orontid derivation remains a modern reconstruction rather than a hard filiation chain.

Khorenatsi does not independently corroborate an Orontid origin: his prestigious genealogy for the Bagratuni is a different, Hebrew/Davidic tradition and must itself be treated under `NARRATIVE CAPTURE Ω` because the work is strongly pro-Bagratuni and its dating is disputed.

## What survives
- Historical Bagratuni existence.
- Stronger late-antique/early-medieval Bagratuni genealogy from roughly the 6th century onward.
- Explicit chains such as Smbat `Khosrov Shum` → Varaz-Tirots → Smbat.
- Offices, territories and later kingship.

## What fails
- Orontid → Bagratuni as a hard genealogical edge.
- Prestige traditions as substitute for pedigree.

## Verdict
`CLOSED-UNRESOLVED / DOCUMENTARY GAP`. Retain remote Orontid origin only as a source-qualified historiographical hypothesis. Reopen only for materially new independent evidence.
