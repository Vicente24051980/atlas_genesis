**Status:** CANONICAL HISTORIOGRAPHICAL ARCHITECTURE

## Scope partition
DINASTÍA HISTÓRICA Ω is a historiographical project. It may share graph software and anti-inference rules with ATLAS CAPITAL, but it does not share evidence scales, confidence propagation or investment conclusions.

Hard namespaces: `HISTORICAL_GRAPH`, `CORPORATE_GRAPH`, and explicitly sourced `CROSS_DOMAIN_LINKS` only.

### NO CROSS-DOMAIN CONFIDENCE PROPAGATION Ω
A modern regulatory filing cannot raise the credibility of a medieval reconstruction by graph adjacency. A medieval chronicle cannot raise the credibility of a modern ownership claim.

## Tree vs prosopography
- Genealogical tree: explicit/defensible kinship only.
- Prosopographical map: persons, houses, offices, territories and events without implied kinship.
- Control-transmission graph: office, territory, patrimony, confiscation, grant, marriage-transfer, acquisition and political succession.
- Claim/tradition graph: prestigious descent/affiliation not independently demonstrated.

`member_of ≠ parent_of` · `successor_of ≠ child_of` · `claims_descent_from ≠ descends_from`

## Evidence is multidimensional
A1/A2/B1/B2/C/X may describe relation explicitness/type but MUST NOT be treated as a total confidence score. Store where known: relation explicitness, source class, source temporality, independence, bias, scholarly dispute, ultimate source and dependency root.

## SOURCE PROFILE Ω
Each source requires composition date/range, temporal distance, genre, patronage/political interest, house bias, transmission problems, dependencies and major scholarly disputes.

## HOMONYM FIREWALL Ω
Repeated names are separate persons by default. Identity requires evidence. Name recurrence + same house + compatible chronology is only a clue.

## NARRATIVE CAPTURE Ω
Test whether the macromap reflects historical structure or legitimating genealogy. Frozen models: `H1_HISTORICAL_NETWORK` vs `H0_GENEALOGICAL_PROGRAM`. Use author-removal and source normalization before family centrality claims.

## Bibliographic dependency
Ten secondary works repeating one reconstruction count as one dependency. Record independent support count, ultimate-source count, author dependency, alternative model and dependency loops.

## GENERATIONAL DENSITY GATE Ω
For implicit/reconstructed complete pedigrees use 20–40 years/generation as a plausibility envelope. 451→610 ≈159 years (~4–8 generations); 610→ca.750 ≈140 years (~4–7); 69 BCE→ca.350 CE ≈419 years (~11–21). Long unnamed spans are `GENEALOGICAL_DISCONTINUITY`, not ordinary small gaps.

## Bridge Destruction Protocol Ω
Freeze every structural bridge before research. Outcomes: `SURVIVES | PARTIAL | DOCUMENTARY_GAP | CONTRADICTED | DEPENDENCY_LOOP | CLOSED_UNRESOLVED | CLOSED_PARTIAL | CLOSED_SURVIVES`. Only a surviving claim may enter the hard macromap at the exact tested granularity.

## Primary object: transmission of control
Priority: `holds_office`, `inherits_office`, `holds_territory`, `inherits_territory`, `transfers_patrimony`, `marriage_transfer`, `acquires_territory`, `confiscated_from`, `granted_to`, `political_successor_of`. `parent_of` is one possible mechanism, not the sole objective.

Superior question: **How is control reproduced and transferred among elites through kinship, office, territory, marriage, confiscation, purchase and institutional succession?**

## Extinction rule
`POLITICAL_EXTINCTION ≠ HOUSE_NAME_EXTINCTION ≠ BIOLOGICAL_EXTINCTION`. Biological survival is never presumed; unsupported later descent = `UNKNOWN`.
