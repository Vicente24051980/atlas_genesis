# DINASTÍA HISTÓRICA Ω v2

**Status:** CANONICAL HISTORIOGRAPHICAL ARCHITECTURE  
**Date:** 2026-09-06

## 1. Scope partition
DINASTÍA HISTÓRICA Ω is a historiographical project. It may share graph software and anti-inference rules with ATLAS CAPITAL, but it does not share evidence scales, confidence propagation or investment conclusions.

Hard namespaces:
- `HISTORICAL_GRAPH`
- `CORPORATE_GRAPH`
- `CROSS_DOMAIN_LINKS` only when explicitly sourced

### NO CROSS-DOMAIN CONFIDENCE PROPAGATION Ω
A modern regulatory filing cannot raise the credibility of a medieval reconstruction by graph adjacency. A medieval chronicle cannot raise the credibility of a modern ownership claim.

## 2. Tree vs prosopography
- **Genealogical tree:** explicit or defensible kinship only.
- **Prosopographical map:** persons, houses, offices, territories and events may coexist without implied kinship.
- **Control-transmission graph:** office, territory, patrimony, confiscation, grant, marriage-transfer, acquisition and political succession.
- **Claim/tradition graph:** asserted prestigious descent or affiliation that is not independently demonstrated.

`member_of ≠ parent_of`  
`successor_of ≠ child_of`  
`claims_descent_from ≠ descends_from`

## 3. Evidence is multidimensional
A1/A2/B1/B2/C/X may describe relation explicitness/type but MUST NOT be treated as a total confidence score.

Every historical claim should carry, where known:
- `relation_explicitness`
- `source_class`
- `source_temporality`
- `source_independence`
- `source_bias`
- `scholarly_dispute`
- `ultimate_source`
- `dependency_root`

An explicit father/son statement in a contemporary inscription is not epistemically equivalent to an explicit father/son statement in a later chronicle of disputed dating.

## 4. SOURCE PROFILE Ω
Each source/author requires a profile containing:
- claimed composition date;
- critical dating range;
- distance from narrated event;
- genre;
- patronage or political interest;
- known house bias;
- manuscript/transmission problems;
- dependence on earlier sources;
- major scholarly disputes.

Khorenatsi is a mandatory high-risk source profile because dating and political orientation are disputed. Explicitness in Khorenatsi is therefore separate from contemporaneity/reliability.

## 5. HOMONYM FIREWALL Ω
Repeated names are separate persons by default. Identity requires evidence.

Name recurrence, same house, territorial continuity and chronological compatibility are clues only.

## 6. NARRATIVE CAPTURE Ω
DINASTÍA must test whether a reconstructed macromap reflects historical structure or later legitimating genealogy.

Frozen rival models:
- `H1_HISTORICAL_NETWORK`
- `H0_GENEALOGICAL_PROGRAM`

Bagratuni ancestral depth/connectivity must be compared with Mamikonean, Artsruni, Siwni and Kamsarakan while controlling for prominence, documentary survival and number of mentions.

### AUTHOR-REMOVAL TEST
Recompute a claim/network after removing a potentially interested author. A structure that disappears after author removal is evidence for source-specific narrative capture, not independent historical identification.

## 7. Bibliographic dependency
Ten secondary works repeating Toumanoff do not equal ten independent lines.

For structural claims record:
- `independent_support_count`
- `ultimate_source_count`
- `depends_on_author`
- `alternative_model`
- `dependency_loop`

The Bridge Destruction Register is the canonical place for the five initial structural tests.

## 8. GENERATIONAL DENSITY GATE Ω
For implicit or reconstructed complete pedigrees use a 20–40 year/generation plausibility envelope.

This gate flags or rejects reconstructed complete chains outside the envelope. It does not mechanically reject an explicit extraordinary primary-source filiation.

Key current examples:
- 451→610 ≈159 years: roughly 4–8 generations.
- 610→ca.750 ≈140 years: roughly 4–7 generations.
- 69 BCE→ca.350 CE ≈419 years: roughly 11–21 generations.

Long unnamed spans of this magnitude are `GENEALOGICAL_DISCONTINUITY`, not ordinary small gaps.

## 9. Bridge Destruction Protocol Ω
Every structural bridge must be frozen before research.

Allowed outcomes:
`SURVIVES | PARTIAL | DOCUMENTARY_GAP | CONTRADICTED | DEPENDENCY_LOOP | CLOSED_UNRESOLVED`

A bridge enters the hard macromap only if it `SURVIVES` the independent-evidence test.

## 10. Primary object: transmission of control
DINASTÍA HISTÓRICA prioritizes:
1. `holds_office`
2. `inherits_office`
3. `holds_territory`
4. `inherits_territory`
5. `transfers_patrimony`
6. `marriage_transfer`
7. `acquires_territory`
8. `confiscated_from`
9. `granted_to`
10. `political_successor_of`
11. `parent_of` as one possible mechanism, not the sole research objective

The superior question is:
**How is control reproduced and transferred among elites through kinship, office, territory, marriage, confiscation, purchase and institutional succession?**

## 11. Extinction rule
`POLITICAL_EXTINCTION ≠ HOUSE_NAME_EXTINCTION ≠ BIOLOGICAL_EXTINCTION`

But biological survival is never presumed. If later descent cannot be demonstrated, status is `UNKNOWN`.
