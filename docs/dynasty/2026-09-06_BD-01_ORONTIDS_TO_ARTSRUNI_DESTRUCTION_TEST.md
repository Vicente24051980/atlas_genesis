# BD-01 — Orontids → Artsruni — Bridge Destruction Test

**Status:** CLOSED-UNRESOLVED / DOCUMENTARY GAP
**Date:** 2026-09-06

## Frozen claim
`Orontids / Sophene → Artsruni` as a genealogical descent claim.

## Survival criterion
The bridge survives only if at least one line of argument connects the two endpoints without depending on Toumanoff's reconstruction. Repetitions of Toumanoff do not count as independent corroboration.

## Evidence audit
1. **Toumanoff / Iranica (ARTSRUNI)** explicitly calls the Artsruni an Orontid offshoot, but says Mithrobuzanes of 69 BCE **"may well be"** the first historically known member. This is a reconstructive claim, not a demonstrated pedigree.
2. **Facella / Iranica (ORONTIDS, 2021)** independently strengthens the existence of an Orontid royal line in Sophene and Commagene but warns that the surviving evidence is insufficient for complete ruler lists. Facella does not supply a bridge from that line to the late-antique Artsruni.
3. **Iranica, Armenia and Iran ii** states explicitly that insufficient evidence does not support the claim that the leading Armenian families of the Christian period descended from the Orontids; it also notes uncertainty over whether the Sophene dynasts were themselves Orontids.
4. The apparent independent lineage behind Toumanoff runs through **Markwart and Adontz**. This is therefore not a pure Toumanoff single-source claim, but the surviving argument remains reconstructive and rests on onomastics, geography, titulature and continuity assumptions rather than a recoverable genealogy.
5. Alternative models exist: local/Urartian-origin hypotheses for the Artsruni have been proposed in modern Armenian scholarship, showing that Orontid descent is not the only explanatory model.

## Generational density
From Mithrobuzanes in 69 BCE to securely visible late-antique Artsruni in the 4th century is roughly 400+ years, implying approximately **11–21 generations** under the 20–40-year heuristic, with no continuous sequence of named ancestors.

This is therefore classified as:

`GENEALOGICAL_DISCONTINUITY`

not as an ordinary short documentary hiatus.

## Result matrix
- **Genealogical bridge:** DOCUMENTARY GAP / NOT DEMONSTRATED
- **Orontid line in Sophene:** SUPPORTED independently
- **Artsruni historical existence:** SUPPORTED
- **Mithrobuzanes = Artsruni:** B2 reconstructive hypothesis
- **Artsruni = Orontid offshoot:** historiographical hypothesis, not hard graph edge
- **Contradicted?** NO — lack of proof is not positive disproof

## Canonical consequence
Remove `Orontid → Artsruni` from the hard historical macromap. Retain only:

`claims_descent_from / reconstructed_as_offshoot_of`

with explicit dependency metadata.

Do not reopen BD-01 because another secondary handbook repeats the conclusion. Reopen only for new primary evidence, new critical edition, new epigraphic/numismatic evidence, or a genuinely independent argument that directly bridges the families.

## What survives intact
- Orontid/Sophene dynastic history.
- Artsruni prosopography from Late Antiquity onward.
- Secure internal Artsruni kinships.
- Territory, office and patrimonial transmission inside the documented Artsruni period.

## Verdict
**BD-01 does not survive as genealogy.**

The appropriate result is **CLOSED-UNRESOLVED / DOCUMENTARY GAP**, not FALSE.
