# BD-05A — Armenian Bagratuni ↔ Georgian Bagrationi

**Status:** CLOSED-SURVIVES  
**Date:** 2026-09-06

## Frozen claim
A historical dynastic relationship exists between the Armenian Bagratuni and Georgian Bagrationi houses.

## Destructive result
The broad relationship survives removal of Toumanoff as a single point of failure. Georgian and Armenian traditions plus pre-Toumanoff scholarship preserve a Bagratid relationship; Georgian material calls Ashot Bagratuni and connects the early branch to an Armenian/Bagratid context.

This does **not** prove the exact founder pedigree or every intervening father→son link.

## Hard-graph consequence
Allow a source-qualified hard edge:
`dynastically_related_to(Bagratuni, Bagrationi)`

Do not infer:
`Vasak → Adarnase → Ashot`
without separately passing BD-05B.

## Verdict
`CLOSED-SURVIVES` at the broad dynastic-relationship level only.
