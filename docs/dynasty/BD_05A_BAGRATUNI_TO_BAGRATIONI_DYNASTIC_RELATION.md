# BD-05A — Armenian Bagratuni ↔ Georgian Bagrationi dynastic relationship

**Status:** CLOSED-SURVIVES  
**Date:** 2026-09-06

## Frozen claim
The Georgian Bagrationi dynasty is historically/dynastically related to the Armenian Bagratuni house.

## Destructive test
This claim does not depend exclusively on Toumanoff.

Independent/earlier evidence includes:
- Georgian tradition in Juansher associates Adarnase, father of Ashot, with the Bagratid house and with a father established as a prince in Armenian lands.
- Giorgi Merchule's *Life of Grigol Khandzteli* identifies Ashot as Bagratuni.
- Armenian Vardan preserves a tradition of the migration of Adarnase/Atrnerseh and his line from Armenia into Tao-Klarjeti.
- Pre-Toumanoff scholarship, notably Markwart, analyzed the Armenian/Iberian Bagratid relationship independently.

The Georgian historiographical tradition also contains an alternative Davidic/localized genealogy and modern Georgian scholarship has challenged an Armenian-origin reading. Those disputes prevent using the broad relationship as proof of any specific father-to-son bridge.

## Result
`SURVIVES` for the frozen broad dynastic-relationship claim.

This does **not** imply that every specific founder genealogy survives. BD-05B is tested separately.

## Hard-graph consequence
Allowed:

Not allowed from BD-05A alone:

## Narrative-capture note
The Davidic origin legend is evidence of dynastic legitimation, not an independent proof of Armenian or biblical descent.
