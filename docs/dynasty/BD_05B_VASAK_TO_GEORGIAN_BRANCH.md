# BD-05B — Vasak → Atrnerseh / Adarnase → Georgian branch

**Status:** CLOSED-PARTIAL  
**Date:** 2026-09-06

## Frozen claim
Vasak, brother of Smbat VII, was father/ancestor of Atrnerseh/Adarnase and founded the Georgian Bagratid branch.

## Destructive result
The founder tradition predates Toumanoff: Vardan preserves Vasak→Atrnerseh and Markwart used this material before Toumanoff. Therefore it is not a pure Toumanoff dependency loop.

However:
- Vardan is a late source for the early branch;
- Georgian sources do not independently name Vasak as Adarnase's father;
- identification of the relevant Adarnase/Atrnerseh and links to 'Ashot the Blind' are reconstructive;
- the broad Bagratuni↔Bagrationi relationship survives independently, but the exact founder pedigree does not reach the same evidentiary level.

## Hard-graph consequence
Allow a source-qualified historiographical parentage/founder claim. Prohibit an unqualified hard `parent_of(Vasak, Adarnase)` edge.

## Verdict
`CLOSED-PARTIAL`.
