# BD-05B — Vasak, brother of Smbat VII → founder of Georgian branch

**Status:** CLOSED-PARTIAL  
**Date:** 2026-09-06

## Frozen claim
Vasak Bagratuni, brother of Smbat VII and son of Ashot the Blind, founded the Georgian Bagratid/Bagrationi branch through his son Atrnerseh/Adarnase.

## Destructive test
The claim is not solely Toumanoffian: Vardan's later Armenian history preserves Atrnerseh as son of Vasak, and Markwart used that genealogy before Toumanoff. This provides an independent historiographical root.

However:
- Vardan is many centuries later than the events.
- Georgian sources preserve Adarnase and his Bagratid association but do not name Vasak as his father.
- Georgian traditions contain competing genealogical constructions.
- The exact identification of Juansher's 'Adarnase the Blind' with Ashot the Blind is reconstructive.

## Result
`CLOSED-PARTIAL`.

Survives:
- a pre-Toumanoff late-medieval Armenian tradition connecting Vasak→Atrnerseh;
- consistency with an Armenian Bagratid migration into Tao-Klarjeti.

Does not survive as hard genealogy:
- Vasak→Atrnerseh/Adarnase as contemporaneously attested father→son;
- exact founder chain Ashot the Blind→Vasak→Adarnase→Ashot I at A1/A2 level.

## Hard-graph consequence
Allow only source-qualified `claims_parentage` / `historiographically_derived_from` for Vasak→Atrnerseh unless stronger primary evidence is found.

Do not create an unqualified `parent_of` hard edge from this test.

## Reopen rule
Reopen only for earlier Armenian/Georgian documentary, epigraphic, sigillographic, or manuscript evidence that independently names Vasak as Adarnase/Atrnerseh's father.
