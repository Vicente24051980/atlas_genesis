# CONTROL TRANSFER Ω — Armenia 428→775→884

**Status:** FIRST HARD-MAP SYNTHESIS  
**Date:** 2026-09-06

## Objective
Reconstruct transfer of control using offices, territory, patrimony, marriage, confiscation, purchase and political recognition rather than speculative remote pedigree.

## 428 — abolition of Arsacid monarchy
Sasanian abolition of the Armenian monarchy shifts the system toward hereditary princely houses under imperial oversight. Structural regime change, not genealogy.

## Mamikonean control package
Territorial base: Tayk', Tarawn. Patriarchal estates: Bagrewand, Daranalik', Ekeleac'. Hamazasp Mamikonean marries Sahak the Great's only daughter; after Sahak's death in 438 patriarchal estates pass into Mamikonean control. The house also holds the hereditary `sparapet` office. Vahan's marzpan dignity later passes to brother Vard Patrik: office succession ≠ parent-child succession.

## 732 — office displacement
Prince of Armenia passes to Ashot Bagratuni; Bagratuni also take over the sparapet function traditionally associated with Mamikonean. This is function-of-control transfer without biological descent.

## 748–775 — failed resistance
Further Mamikonean revolts fail. At Bagrewand in 775 Musel, head of the house, dies and homeland preponderance collapses. Political extinction is not biological extinction.

## Post-775 — territorial reallocation
Bagratuni acquire/possess former Mamikonean principalities including Tarawn, southern Tayk', Bznunik' and later Bagrewand. Bagratuni also purchase Arsharunik' and Shirak, including Bagavan and Ani, from Kamsarakan. This latter macro-transfer remains provisional pending independent source-root audit.

## Bagrewand case study
Early royal context → Gregorid ecclesiastical/patriarchal control → Gregorid→Mamikonean via marriage/inheritance after 438 → Mamikonean→Bagratuni by the 9th c. A single territory records changing control architecture while pedigree remains secondary.

## Parallel survivor states after 775
Bagratuni west/center; Artsruni south/Vaspurakan; Siwni east.

## 884 — control converted into kingship
Bagratuni monopolization of the presiding-prince function is converted into kingship, recognized externally. State-function conversion, not demonstrated direct descent from Arsacid kings.

## Core finding
The strongest architecture from 428 to 884 is a sequence of transferable control assets:
`territory + hereditary office + marriage/patrimony + imperial recognition + military capacity + fiscal resources`.

## Canonical implications
1. `parent_of` is subordinate to control-transfer edges.
2. Political loss does not prove biological extinction.
3. A house can inherit/purchase control assets without descending from the previous holder.
4. Dynastic prestige claims cannot override documented office/territory transfers.
5. 428→732→775→884 is a primary hard-map spine.
