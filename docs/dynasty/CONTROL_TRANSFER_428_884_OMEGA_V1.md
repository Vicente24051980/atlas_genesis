# CONTROL TRANSFER Ω — Armenia 428→775→884

**Status:** FIRST HARD-MAP SYNTHESIS  
**Date:** 2026-09-06

## Objective
Reconstruct transfer of control using offices, territory, patrimony, marriage, confiscation, purchase and political recognition rather than speculative remote pedigree.

## 428 — abolition of Arsacid monarchy
The Sasanian abolition of the Armenian monarchy shifts the political system toward a network of hereditary princely houses under distant imperial oversight. This is a structural regime change, not a genealogical event.

## Mamikonean control package
### Territorial base
- Tayk'
- Tarawn
- later patriarchal estates Bagrewand, Daranalik', Ekeleac'

### Acquisition channel
Hamazasp Mamikonean married the only daughter of Sahak the Great, last male Gregorid; after Sahak's death in 438, patriarchal estates passed into Mamikonean control.

### Office channel
The Mamikonean house held the hereditary office of sparapet / commander-in-chief.

### Political amplification
Vahan Mamikonean's revolt ended with recognition as marzpan; that dignity then passed to his brother Vard Patrik. This is a documented office-transfer block and should not be represented as parent→child succession.

## Late 6th–8th c. shift
Mamikonean power begins to wane as Bagratuni political weight rises. This shift is multi-causal: office transfer, dynastic survival, imperial/caliphal recognition, fiscal/territorial resources and strategic positioning.

## 732 — office displacement
The dignity of prince of Armenia was transferred to Ashot Bagratuni. The Bagratuni also took over the sparapet function traditionally associated with the Mamikonean house. This is a direct example of **function-of-control transfer without biological descent**.

Hard edges:
- `office_transferred_from(Mamikonean, Bagratuni, prince_of_armenia, 732)`
- `office_displaced(Mamikonean, Bagratuni, sparapet, 8th_c)`

## 748–775 — failed Mamikonean resistance
Further Mamikonean revolts fail. In 775, the Armenian defeat at Bagrewand kills Musel, head of the Mamikonean house, and shatters its remaining homeland power.

Result:
- political extinction of Mamikonean preponderance;
- not biological extinction;
- most homeland domains become available for redistribution/absorption.

## Post-775 — territorial reallocation
Bagratuni acquire/possess former Mamikonean principalities including:
- Tarawn
- southern Tayk'
- Bznunik'
- subsequently Bagrewand

Bagratuni also purchase from Kamsarakan:
- Arsharunik'
- Shirak
- Bagavan
- Ani

This yields two distinct control mechanisms:
1. `absorbs_former_domain`
2. `purchases_territory`

Neither is genealogical inheritance unless separately proven.

## Bagrewand as a control-chain case study
Ayrarat evidence allows the following non-genealogical sequence:
1. royal/Orontid-era domain context;
2. later Gregorid ecclesiastical/patriarchal control after Christianization;
3. Gregorid → Mamikonean via marriage/inheritance after Sahak's death in 438;
4. Mamikonean → Bagratuni by 855/862.

This is one of the strongest DINASTÍA Ω examples because a single territory documents changing control architecture across houses while pedigree remains secondary.

## Parallel survivor states after 775
The collapse/weakening of many houses concentrates Armenia into three major political formations:
- Bagratuni — west/center
- Artsruni — south/Vaspurakan
- Siwni — east

Artsruni expansion over Greater/Lesser Albag into the wider region later called Vaspurakan represents a separate control-consolidation path.

## 884 — control converted into kingship
Bagratuni monopolization of the presiding-prince function is converted into kingship in 884, with recognition from both caliph and emperor. The monarchy abolished in 428 is thereby restored under a different house.

This is a **state-function conversion**:
`presiding_prince_control → recognized_kingship`
not a return by direct descent from the Arsacid kings.

## Core finding
The strongest historical architecture from 428 to 884 is not a remote family tree. It is a sequence of transferable control assets:

`territory + hereditary office + marriage/patrimony + imperial recognition + military capacity + fiscal resources`

These assets move across houses by several mechanisms. Biological kinship is only one.

## Canonical implications
1. `parent_of` is subordinate to control-transfer edges in DINASTÍA HISTÓRICA Ω.
2. A house can lose political control while biological descendants survive.
3. A house can inherit or purchase another house's control assets without descending from it.
4. A source's dynastic prestige claims cannot override documented office/territory transfers.
5. The 428→732→775→884 sequence becomes a primary hard-map spine for the project.
