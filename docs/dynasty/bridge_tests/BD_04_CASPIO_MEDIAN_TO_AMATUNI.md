# BD-04 — Caspio-Median / Matianian-Mannaean → Amatuni

**Date:** 2026-09-06  
**Protocol:** BRIDGE DESTRUCTION Ω  
**Frozen claim:** the historical Amatuni house derives from a Caspio-Median / Matianian-Mannaean aristocratic stratum. This is an origin hypothesis, not a continuous father→son pedigree.

## 1. Historical anchor
The Amatuni house is independently visible in Armenian evidence from Late Antiquity, including Pʿawstos/Faustus and later fifth-century sources. This secures the historical house but says nothing by itself about a first-millennium-BCE ancestry.

## 2. Khorenatsi layer
Movsēs Xorenacʿi 2.57 gives an eastern/Iranian setting: the family is associated with the land of the Aryans and the region of Hamadan. But the same passage supplies a Jewish genealogy through Manue/Samson. Modern scholarship treats that biblical pedigree as legendary/spurious.

Therefore Khorenatsi supports the existence of a remote-origin tradition but cannot be converted into a verified Caspio-Median pedigree.

## 3. Modern reconstruction and dependency audit
Toumanoff's Iranica synthesis calls the Amatuni Caspio-Median and groups them with Mardpet, Mandakuni and Muracʿan. His earlier Traditio treatment does not present this as a newly discovered explicit genealogy: it cites Adontz, Łapʿancʿyan, Markwart, Herzfeld and scholarship on Median/Mannaean strata.

This matters in both directions:
- the proposition is not merely a one-line assertion invented in a late handbook;
- but multiple scholarly antecedents do not automatically become independent genealogical witnesses if they infer the same remote origin from geography, ethnonymy, onomastics or historical plausibility.

No audited ancient source supplies a named Caspio-Median/Mannaean ancestor → named Amatuni bridge.

## 4. External control
Independent evidence confirms Caspian populations around the southwest Caspian and substantial Iranian cultural/onomastic influence. It does not identify the Amatuni as descendants of those populations. Likewise, Armenia's deep political and cultural ties with Median/Persian worlds make an Iranian-stratum origin plausible but are not discriminating evidence for this specific house.

| Layer | Verdict |
