## Frozen survival rule
A bridge survives only if at least one argument connects the endpoints without ultimately depending on Toumanoff or on a bibliographic chain returning to the same reconstruction. Derivative repetition is not independent corroboration.

## Allowed outcomes
`SURVIVES`, `PARTIAL`, `DOCUMENTARY GAP`, `CONTRADICTED`, `DEPENDENCY LOOP`, `CLOSED-UNRESOLVED`, `CLOSED-PARTIAL`, `CLOSED-SURVIVES`.

## Stop Rule Ω
Close only after relevant known primary evidence, materially independent secondary arguments where available, dependency roots, surviving/failing layers and pending evidence classes have been audited. Reopen only for materially new evidence or genuinely independent argument.
