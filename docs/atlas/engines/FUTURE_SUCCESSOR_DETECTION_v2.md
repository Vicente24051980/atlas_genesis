# ATLAS Ω — FUTURE / SUCCESSOR DETECTION Ω v2

**Status:** CANONICAL / ACTIVE
**Effective date:** 2026-08-17
**Scope:** Independent discovery engine. Its signal MUST NOT overwrite Principal Ω, Good Companies Cheap Ω, Historical Dislocation/Burry Ω, Money Rotation Ω, or specialized engines.

## Objective
Find companies capable of moving up category over 3–6 years before becoming obvious leaders.

Does NOT seek the best current companies, largest companies, simple high growth, stocks merely because they already multiplied, or pure momentum.

Targets: SMALL/MID→LARGE; LARGE→FUTURE GIANT; NICHE→CATEGORY LEADER; SUPPLIER→BOTTLENECK; LOSS-MAKING→PROFITABLE SCALE; LOCAL→GLOBAL; PRODUCT→PLATFORM.

## Phase 0 — Universe Discovery
Global search. Preferred market cap $0.5B–$30B; primary zone $2B–$15B; secondary $15B–$50B. Above $50B apply Successor Maturity Penalty. Above $100B requires exceptional evidence of a new S-curve. Exclude shells, pre-operating SPACs, fraud/dubious evidence, illiquid microcaps, structurally insolvent businesses.

## Phase 1 — Category Ascension Ω
Measure revenue rank velocity, market-cap rank velocity, industry-share gains, customer-tier migration, geographic expansion, product→platform transition, installed-base expansion, contract-size expansion, backlog acceleration, TAM expansion.

SUCCESSOR_SIGNATURE = accelerating revenue growth AND accelerating market share AND earnings/FCF inflection.

## Phase 2 — Economic Proof Gate
E0 narrative; E1 orders/pilots; E2 contracts/backlog; E3 material revenue; E4 demonstrated OCF/FCF/ROIC. Future may advance at E2–E3 earlier than Principal Ω. E0 = NO PASS. E1 = DISCOVERY ONLY.

## Phase 3 — Growth Quality Ω
Separate organic growth, acquisition growth, price, volume, FX, accounting/reclassification. Reward revenue, volume, customers, backlog, retention, gross profit and operating income expansion. Prefer economically productive growth (e.g. revenue +25%, operating income +50%, FCF +70%) over high revenue growth accompanied by collapsing FCF.

## Phase 4 — Inflection Ω
Measure derivatives: Δ revenue growth, gross margin, operating margin, FCF margin, ROIC, backlog, retention, customer quality. Key signature: negative FCF → breakeven → positive FCF → accelerating FCF.

## Phase 5 — Bottleneck / CAPEX Position Ω
Classify Commodity / Component / Critical component / System / Platform / Infrastructure bottleneck / Toll-road. Reward switching costs, scarce capacity, certification, regulation, IP, network effects, installed base, long qualification cycles and mission-critical status. High Bottleneck Persistence required for maximum scores.

## Phase 6 — TAM Migration Ω
Use demonstrated TAM expansion rather than management TAM claims: new product, customer, country, vertical, workload, regulation driver or infrastructure cycle. Core question: can the company sell 5× more without inventing another business?

## Phase 7 — Customer Ladder Ω
Track SMB→enterprise→Fortune 500→hyperscaler→government→sovereign/strategic customer. Reward larger, longer, more recurrent contracts with stronger counterparties.

## Phase 8 — Capital Funding Quality Ω
CFQ0 self-funded; CFQ1 customer/prepayment; CFQ2 project finance/non-recourse; CFQ3 reasonable corporate debt; CFQ4 recurring equity/dilution; CFQ5 fragile financing. A Future may burn FCF only when evidence supports CAPEX→capacity→utilization→revenue→OCF→FCF, not CAPEX→promise→new equity raise.

## Phase 9 — Management Execution Ω
Measure guidance hit-rate, capital allocation, insider ownership, dilution, M&A discipline, acquired-business ROIC and execution against capacity plans. Do not reward storytelling.

## Phase 10 — Forward Asymmetry Ω
Build Bear/Base/Bull scenarios over 3–6 years. Ask what must happen for 3×/5× and whether it is economically plausible. Current market cap is critical: $4B→$20B can be plausible; $150B→$750B requires much more value creation.

## Phase 11 — Discovery Penalty / Maturity Ω
Successor_Maturity_Penalty: < $5B = 0; $5–15B = -2; $15–30B = -5; $30–50B = -8; $50–100B = -12; $100–250B = -18; >$250B = -25. Applies ONLY to Future Ω and does NOT reduce Business Quality.

Institutional saturation, analyst coverage, index saturation and mega-cap status generate a separate DISCOVERY_SATURATION_PENALTY. A company may therefore have Business Quality 95 while Future Discovery is 75–82 because it is already widely discovered.

## Phase 12 — Hidden Concentration Ω
Map causal exposure (AI compute, networking, optics, power, grid, gas, construction, defense, healthcare, financial infrastructure, etc.). An exceptional Future may pass regardless, but portfolio marginal contribution is calculated separately and MUST NOT alter Future Score.

## Phase 13 — Falsifiers
Every Future requires explicit falsifiers: backlog decline, rising customer concentration, utilization decline, gross-margin deterioration, failed FCF inflection, repeated dilution, debt/refinancing stress, project delays, loss of critical customer, technological displacement.
