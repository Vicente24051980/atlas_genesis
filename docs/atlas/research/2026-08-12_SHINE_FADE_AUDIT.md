# ATLAS Ω — SHINE / FADE AUDIT — 2026-08-12

## Scope
Audit of THC, CAH, GEHC, COR, APH, MRVL, HIMS and INTC using current operating data, guidance, cash flow, orders/backlog, capital allocation and financing risk.

## Audited conclusions

### APH — BRIGHTEST / upgrade
Q2 2026: sales $8.8B (+55% reported, +30% organic), orders $10.7B, book-to-bill 1.23x, adjusted EPS $1.35 (+67%), adjusted operating margin 29.8%, operating cash flow $1.6B and free cash flow $1.2B. Result exceeded prior guidance. ATLAS interpretation: fundamental acceleration remains extremely strong; valuation/entry timing must remain a separate gate.

### THC — BRIGHT / confirmed, with normalization caveat
Q2 2026 adjusted EPS $6.12 (+52.2%), adjusted EBITDA $1.304B (+16.3%), hospital adjusted EBITDA margin 18.0% vs 15.6%, FY26 adjusted EBITDA guidance raised to $4.83-$5.03B and adjusted FCF guidance to $2.725-$3.025B; board added $2.0B to buyback authorization. Caveat: GAAP earnings include material contract-termination revenue; H1 adjusted FCF $1.422B was slightly below $1.466B prior year. Strong, but use adjusted operating metrics.

### GEHC — INFLECTION / confirmed, not yet cleanest
Q2 2026 revenue +5.7%, organic revenue +3.5%, organic orders +11.1%, book-to-bill 1.15x, backlog $23.9B, diluted EPS +16.5%. FCF only $68M in quarter and results benefited from IEEPA tariff refunds; company reaffirmed rather than raised full-year guidance. Strong commercial inflection, weaker cash confirmation.

### COR — BRIGHT / confirmed on latest primary data available
Q2 FY26 revenue $78.4B (+3.8%), adjusted EPS $4.75 (+7.5%), FY26 adjusted EPS guide raised to $17.65-$17.90 and later narrowed upward to $17.70-$17.90; board authorized an additional $2.0B repurchase program. Primary-source Q3 release was not retrievable in the audit session, so no unsupported Q3 claim is canonized.

### CAH — BRIGHT / provisional current-quarter validation
Current Q4 FY26 reporting shows adjusted EPS $2.91 and FY27 adjusted EPS guidance $12.40-$12.60 (about +13%-15%), but the fresh company primary release was not retrievable from the indexed company site during this audit. Prior Q3 primary release had revenue +11% to $60.9B and raised FY26 outlook. Keep BRIGHT but below APH/THC until primary Q4 is captured.

### MRVL — FUNDAMENTALS BRIGHT, equity quality more mixed
Q1 FY27 revenue $2.418B (+28%), operating cash flow $638.8M (record), Q2 revenue guide $2.7B midpoint (+35% YoY), management raised FY27/FY28 revenue outlook on AI bookings. Caveats: GAAP EPS only $0.04, SBC $207.6M in Q1, acquisitions consumed $1.271B cash, $2.0B preferred stock issued and debt increased. Business momentum is strong; capital structure/dilution prevents top-tier score.

### HIMS — FADE / confirmed
Q1 2026 already showed revenue +4% but gross margin down to 65% from 73%, net loss $92.1M vs prior-year profit, adjusted EBITDA $44.3M vs $91.1M. Current Q2 reporting shows revenue acceleration but continued gross-margin compression, loss-making economics and reduced upper end / midpoint of EBITDA expectations. Growth is real; quality of growth has deteriorated. FADE remains appropriate.

### INTC — PRICE BRIGHT, EQUITY DILUTION RISK / confirmed
Intel upsized a 2026 equity offering from $15B to $20B, priced at $95/share, after a very large YTD rally. Capital raise supports capex/foundry expansion but is materially dilutive. ATLAS classification: not a fundamental 'dead company' call; rather FINANCING/DILUTION RISK and valuation/execution risk after price rerating.

## Rules
- SHINE/FADE is not BUY/SELL.
- BUY requires QUALITY Ω × IMPLIED RETURN Ω × GREEN CONTINUITY Ω × ENTRY TIMING Ω / NO-CHASE × EVENT_RISK Ω.
- Never treat reported growth alone as quality; cross-check margins, FCF, SBC/dilution, leverage and capital intensity.
- Never canonize an unavailable fresh quarter as verified primary evidence.
