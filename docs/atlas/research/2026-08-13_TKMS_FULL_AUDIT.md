## Valuation snapshot
Reference market price supplied/checked around €94.20 (12-Aug-2026).
Implied equity value ≈ €5.98bn (63.524m shares × €94.20).
At FY2024/25 net profit €108m this is ~55x trailing earnings; therefore TKMS is not statistically cheap on trailing earnings.

Using updated sales guidance on FY2024/25 €2.2bn gives indicative FY2025/26 sales around €2.42–2.46bn. At 6.5% margin, indicative adjusted EBIT is around €157–160m. This is a scenario calculation, not company guidance for absolute EBIT.

## FCF normalization
Reported FY2024/25 FCF (€784m) is unsuitable as a steady-state valuation anchor because large customer prepayments drive working-capital timing. H1 FY2025/26 FCF (-€72m) demonstrates the reverse effect during execution. ATLAS must value TKMS using multi-year through-cycle cash conversion / project cash profiles, not one-year FCF yield.

## Moat
High structural moat: submarine design/integration capability, long certification and procurement cycles, sovereign customer relationships, specialized shipyards, combat/sensor integration, installed program base and high switching/execution costs. Score preliminarily 9/10, subject to peer and contract-economics validation.

## Growth drivers 2026–2030
1. Conversion of >€20bn backlog.
2. Type 212CD submarine program and follow-ons.
3. Surface vessel/frigate demand.
4. Atlas Elektronik sensors, sonar, mine countermeasure and torpedo systems.
5. European/NATO maritime rearmament.
6. Canada opportunity can materially expand backlog but must be probability-weighted until binding economics/order recognition are verified.

## Principal risks
1. Project execution/cost overruns on long-duration fixed/structured contracts.
2. Working-capital and customer-prepayment volatility makes FCF noisy.
3. Margin recovery at Atlas Electronics must occur; recent investment pressure is a watch item.
4. Procurement timing produces lumpy order intake.
5. Political/export/customer concentration risk.
6. Valuation already prices substantial earnings growth.
7. Majority ownership / relatively limited free float.

## Scenario framework (ATLAS estimates, not price targets from management)
Bear: €65–75 — execution delays, margin near ~6%, contract timing disappoints.
Base: €105–125 — double-digit near-term growth, backlog conversion, margin progresses toward 7–8%.
Bull: €145–175 — major international wins including successful Canada conversion plus sustained margin expansion.

At €94.20 the stock sits below the midpoint of the base range, but the margin of safety is not sufficient to call an automatic BUY given trailing valuation and cash-flow lumpiness.
