## Epistemic red flags

1. **Authority theater** — autobiographical credentials are used in place of auditable process.
2. **Deterministic language** — "mandatory buy", "life-changing", "generational wealth", "will soon be".
3. **Cherry-picked winners** — historical winning calls are highlighted without a complete denominator of all calls.
4. **No falsifier framework** — recommendations change without clearly stated conditions.
5. **Narrative compression** — complex theses are reduced to slogans such as "power itself", "memory bottleneck", "landlord of compute".
6. **Price target without valuation bridge** — targets are asserted without DCF, multiples, unit economics, FCF, dilution or probability ranges.
7. **Possible misattribution** — public/company figures are presented as recommending specific equities without attached primary evidence.
