## 13. Hidden concentration

Future Watchlist has substantial second-order exposure to one economic chain:

`AI model demand → GPU/compute → datacenter construction → cooling/electrical → grid → gas/power → project finance`.

Names can appear diversified by sector while depending on the same CAPEX regime. Therefore portfolio promotion must evaluate marginal driver contribution, not ticker count.

The most correlated clusters are:
- AI capacity/funding: `NBIS, CORZ, APLD, IREN, HUT, CIFR, RIOT, CLSK, WYFI, CRWV`.
- electrical/data-center supply: `FIX, VRT, ETN, NVT, HUBB, BE, DLR, EQIX`.
- power/gas enabling layer: `NEE, BKR, WMB, EQT, EXE, AR, RRC, CRK, FRVO`.

This correlation is not a rejection; it is a sizing and portfolio-construction constraint.

## 14. Promotion law

A Future ticker does not enter active portfolio because it is exciting, has a large TAM, signs one giant contract or rallies after earnings.

Promotion requires:

`Identity/Data PASS → Economic Proof → Forward Asymmetry → Funding Sustainability → Demand Independence/quality → Valuation → Hidden Concentration contribution → Entry Timing → falsifier-defined position sizing`.

If promoted into active portfolio, it must be removed from Future Watchlist in the same canonical reconciliation.
