## Audit of “sell U.S. stocks / buy gold” thesis
- REJECT the claim that small investors hold 60% of all wealth in stocks. Fed/FRED Q1 2026: equities are 45.76% of household+nonprofit financial assets and 31.68% of total assets.
- The foreign-investor 63% claim is not reproduced by the latest complete annual Treasury survey. At 30 Jun 2025, foreign holdings of U.S. securities were $35.349T, of which $19.860T were equities (~56.2%). May 2026 foreign U.S.-equity holdings were $24.4645T and net foreign U.S.-equity transactions were positive.
- U.S. valuation risk is real but “completely disconnected” is too categorical: FactSet on 24 Jul 2026 showed S&P 500 forward P/E 20.1x vs 19.9x 5y average and 19.0x 10y average, while Shiller CAPE was near 40-41x, historically extreme.
- Mathematical correction: if commodities are cheap vs stocks, GSCI/S&P 500 is low, equivalently S&P 500/GSCI is high. Saying S&P 500/GSCI is low while saying commodities are cheap is internally inconsistent.
- Commodities are not starting from zero: S&P GSCI spot was +27.78% YTD as of 24 Jul 2026.
- Gold has structural support from central-bank diversification: WGC 2026 survey says 89% expect global gold reserves to rise and 45% expect their own reserves to rise. But gold already experienced record highs and a >20% correction in 2026; it is a regime/hedge asset, not proof of an imminent equity crash.
- Silver Institute expects a sixth consecutive deficit in 2026 (~67 Moz) but also industrial fabrication -2% and recycling +7%; not a one-way trade.
- Copper has the cleanest structural hard-asset case: IEA 2026 projects about 7 Mt additional demand to 2040 and an approximately 25% 2035 supply gap even with announced projects.
- Oil must stay separate from gold/copper because geopolitical supply shocks, inventories, OPEC+ and inflation dominate its regime signal.

Decision: retain U.S. quality equities unless falsifiers trigger; avoid increasing concentration blindly; treat gold, copper, oil, credit, rates, dollar and breadth as macro-regime confirmation layers. Do not replace one concentration (AI/mega-cap) with another concentration (gold/commodities).
