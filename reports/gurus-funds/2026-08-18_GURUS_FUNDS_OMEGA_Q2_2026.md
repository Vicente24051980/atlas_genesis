## Method

This run applies GURUS & FUNDS Ω v1.0 to currently verifiable manager disclosures.

Score weights:

- conviction / concentration: 25%
- new position / accumulation: 20%
- independent cross-style convergence: 15%
- persistence: 10%
- exceptionality: 10%
- contrarian context: 10%
- evidence quality + freshness: 10%

For this first run, **contrarian = neutral 50 unless separately established** and **persistence = neutral 50 where holding-age evidence was not explicitly verified**. Therefore the ranking is intentionally conservative and provisional.

Alphabet share classes are normalized at company level as `GOOG/GOOGL` for convergence analysis. Berkshire and Himalaya Class A/Class C exposures are combined only for company-level conviction; this is not an assertion of identical securities or exact cost basis.
