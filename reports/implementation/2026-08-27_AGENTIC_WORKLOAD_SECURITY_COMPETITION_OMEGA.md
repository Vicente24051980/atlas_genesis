## 2. Agentic Permission / Security Demand Ω

Agent autonomy and tool/file access are treated as a dual variable: they can increase productivity and increase attack surface.

Gross attack surface uses autonomy, privileged-action share, sensitive-data share and external-tool access. Control coverage uses identity controls, least privilege, auditability and runtime security. The engine outputs residual deployment risk separately from security-demand intensity.

Laws:
- `AGENTIC ATTACK SURFACE UP != CYBER MONETIZATION`
- `IDENTITY + LEAST PRIVILEGE + AUDITABILITY ARE PRODUCTION INFRASTRUCTURE FOR AUTONOMOUS AGENTS`

This creates explicit economic pathways for OKTA and the cyber cohort, but requires ARR/billings/revenue/FCF proof before investment promotion.
