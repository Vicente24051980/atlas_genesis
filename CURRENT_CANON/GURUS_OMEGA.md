`GURUS DIRECT COMPANY SCORE CONTRIBUTION = 0`.

GURÚS Ω may create research priority, convergence/divergence observations and hypotheses only. It never changes company quality, valuation, expected return or portfolio sizing.

## Core rule
A guru holding, filing or public statement is a **research observation**, never a BUY signal and never proof of portfolio conviction by itself.

13F data are delayed snapshots and incomplete: foreign holdings, cash, shorts and some derivatives may be absent. Every observation must carry an as-of date and filing/source date. For options, 13F reported value is not option premium/capital at risk; strike, expiry and premium must not be invented.

## Position states
- NEW — initiated in the latest reporting period.
- ADD — increased.
- HOLD — materially maintained.
- TRIM — reduced.
- EXIT — fully exited.
- UNKNOWN — insufficient comparable evidence.

Position state describes the filing comparison only; it does not establish total economic direction when hedge visibility is incomplete.

## Evidence schema
For every manager/security observation record:
1. manager/fund
2. ticker/security
3. reporting period / as-of date
4. filing/publication date and timestamp when available
5. position state
6. instrument: shares / put / call / other
7. shares/value when available
8. semantics of reported value
9. visible-portfolio weight only when explicitly labeled as such
10. total-portfolio weight only when an independently valid denominator exists
11. prior-period comparable
12. source quality
13. hedge visibility
14. limitations (13F delay, options, foreign security, incomplete portfolio, etc.)
15. unique provenance/evidence ID

## Convergence logic
Raw holder count is insufficient. Before any convergence label, evaluate:
- NEW/ADD/HOLD/TRIM/EXIT as filing states, not proof of hidden exposure;
- persistence across quarters;
- instrument and directionality;
- denominator quality;
- hedge visibility;
- independence of managers and strategies;
- freshness and primary-source quality;
- source/provenance independence.

A ticker held by five managers who are trimming is not equivalent to a ticker held by three managers with two material ADD/NEW actions. Neither configuration is a BUY signal.

## Contradictions
Contradictions are preserved, not averaged away. Example: one manager adding a semiconductor while another exits it must appear as DIVERGENCE and be routed to Adversarial Debate Ω / TRADER INTELLIGENCE Ω.

No majority voting. Falsifiers Ω retains absolute independent veto.
