# ATLAS Ω — RESEARCH PRIORITY ≠ SELECTION SCORE

Signals from Capital Intelligence, Trader Intelligence, news, tape, sponsorship, unusual flows, expert nomination or thematic discovery may increase the priority with which an entity is researched.

They do not automatically increase its clean selection score or portfolio membership probability.

`DISCOVERY_PRIORITY ≠ CONVICTION`
`SPONSORSHIP ≠ FUNDAMENTAL_PROOF`
`ATTENTION ≠ EXPECTED_RETURN`
