# ATLAS Ω — COMPARABLE-COMPANY DUEL RULE

Where economically comparable firms compete for the same profit pool, customer budget, capital cycle or portfolio role, ATLAS must compare them directly rather than independently awarding high scores to all.

The duel should identify which company has the superior combination of moat, growth/revisions, FCF/ROIC, valuation/Expected Return, financing quality, downside risk and falsifier profile.

The losing company can remain a challenger; peer victory does not create an automatic portfolio slot until cross-sector Competition for Capital is completed.
