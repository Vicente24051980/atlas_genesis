# ATLAS Ω — ANTI-MEGACAP NEUTRALITY

T0 exists to neutralize discovery bias, not to penalize large companies.

Market capitalization produces neither positive nor negative selection points.

A megacap may rank #1 if evidence, Expected Return and risk merit it. A small/mid-cap may rank #1 under the same standard. The process must ensure both had a fair opportunity to be discovered and evaluated.

`ANTI_MEGACAP_BIAS_CONTROL ≠ ANTI_MEGACAP_PORTFOLIO`
