## Purpose
Distinguish why sovereign long yields are moving before translating rates into precious-metals, copper, mining, grid, industrial or technology equity signals.

## Core law
A rise in long yields is not intrinsically bearish for hard assets. The causal source of the yield move must be classified first.

### Regime A — Restrictive / real-yield shock
LONG_YIELD_UP + REAL_YIELD_UP + USD_UP + GOLD_DOWN => HARD_ASSETS_HEADWIND

Expected equity transmission:
- Gold/silver miners: negative.
- Copper miners: negative/neutral unless copper breadth independently confirms demand.
- Long-duration technology: negative valuation pressure.

### Regime B — Sovereign credibility divergence
LONG_YIELD_UP + USD_DOWN + GOLD_UP => SOVEREIGN_CREDIBILITY_DIVERGENCE

Interpretation: yields may be rising because of fiscal supply, term premium or sovereign credibility stress rather than simply tighter monetary policy.

Expected equity transmission:
- Gold exposure: positive.
- Silver: positive but require industrial-demand confirmation because silver is monetary + industrial.
- Copper: no automatic promotion; require copper price, breadth and FCF transmission.

### Regime C — Liquidity/intervention response
SOVEREIGN_STRESS + LIQUIDITY_SUPPORT + LONG_YIELD_DOWN + USD_DOWN + GOLD_UP => HARD_ASSETS_STRONG_CONFIRMATION

Do not label Treasury buybacks, liquidity operations or market-functioning interventions as Yield Curve Control unless an explicit yield target/cap is evidenced.

GOLD_UP + SILVER_UP + COPPER_UP + MATERIALS_BREADTH_UP => HARD_ASSETS_ROTATION_CONFIRMED

COPPER_UP without gold/silver confirmation => INDUSTRIAL_COMMODITY_ROTATION; do not infer sovereign credibility stress.

## Integration with AI Layer Flow Confirmation Ω
Hard-assets leadership is not proof of AI CAPEX transmission. Copper/materials may be driven by USD, fiscal stress, geopolitics, supply constraints or trend/systematic flows. AI attribution requires independent downstream confirmation across the canonical layer-flow gates.

## Falsifiers
Downgrade/kill sovereign-credibility interpretation if:
- USD and real yields rise persistently while gold/silver lose relative strength;
- copper leadership disappears and Materials breadth collapses;
- observed moves are explained more parsimoniously by commodity-specific supply shocks;
- sovereign-market stress indicators normalize without continued hard-asset relative strength.
