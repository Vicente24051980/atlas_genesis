# ATLAS Ω — CLEAN SELECTION / EXECUTION SEPARATION

## Phase A — clean selection

Uses only canonical universe, evidence, fundamentals, valuation, expected return, risk, falsifiers and Competition for Capital.

Forbidden inputs: current holding status, invested capital, current weight, personal P/L, average cost, prior ranking, tax, turnover cost, brokerage friction, entry timing and replacement hysteresis.

Output: clean ranking, clean selected portfolio, OPTIMAL_N, challengers, decisive duels, falsifiers, confidence and data cutoff.

## Phase B — transition plan

May consume current operational portfolio plus the clean selection output.

Allowed questions: what to sell first, what to buy first, tax/turnover impact, liquidity, order sizing, timing, broker availability and hysteresis against unnecessary churn.

Phase B may delay or stage a clean-selection transition. It may not rewrite Phase A's result.
