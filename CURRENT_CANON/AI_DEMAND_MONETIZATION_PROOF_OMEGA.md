# AI DEMAND & MONETIZATION PROOF Ω v1.0

**Status:** CANONICAL SPECIALIZED ENGINE  
**Date:** 2026-08-18  
**Scope:** ATLAS Ω ENTERPRISE v3.1+

## Mission
Measure whether AI demand is real, broadening and converting into durable economic activity before AI CAPEX Payback Ω judges the return on capital.

This engine exists to prevent two opposite errors:
- assuming large AI CAPEX proves end demand;
- assuming falling token/API prices prove weak demand.

## Canonical chain
`Users / enterprises → workloads / tokens / API calls → retention / expansion → realized price → revenue → gross profit → OCF/FCF contribution → durable monetization`

## Core laws
- RUN RATE != TTM REVENUE != CASH COLLECTED.
- MODEL USAGE != ECONOMIC DEMAND unless a paying/valuable workload can be demonstrated.
- TOKEN PRICE DOWN != DEMAND DOWN.
- REVENUE GROWTH != OWNER ECONOMICS without gross-profit and cash conversion.
- Secondary newsletters/media can trigger research; only primary filings, company disclosures, audited/credible datasets or strongly corroborated independent evidence can promote state.
- Do not double-count the same demand evidence inside AI CAPEX Payback Ω.

## Required inputs
1. Reported revenue and growth.
2. TTM revenue versus annualized/run-rate revenue, clearly separated.
3. Enterprise/customer count and paying-customer growth where disclosed.
4. Net revenue retention, expansion, churn or credible proxy.
5. Workload/token/API/request volume or utilization proxy.
6. Realized unit pricing and price changes.
7. Gross margin / contribution margin.
8. Cost per inference/workload where supportable.
9. Backlog/RPO/TCV/contracted demand.
10. Application-layer monetization: paid seats, enterprise deployments, usage intensity, revenue per customer.
11. Indirect monetization: advertising uplift, conversion, cloud attach, productivity or other measurable economic benefit.
12. OCF/FCF contribution or company-level cash conversion.
13. Customer concentration and subsidy/credit dependence.
14. Geographic/vertical breadth of adoption.
15. Source level and confidence.

Never fabricate undisclosed token volumes, AI revenue splits, utilization, customer counts or unit economics.

## Run-Rate Normalization Ω
Every annualized figure must be tagged:
- `REALIZED_TTM` — trailing twelve-month recognized revenue;
- `ANNUALIZED_CURRENT_PERIOD` — recent monthly/quarterly pace multiplied to one year;
- `MANAGEMENT_GUIDANCE` — forward company guidance;
- `MEDIA_ESTIMATE` — third-party estimate.

Rules:
- Annualized current-period revenue cannot be compared as if equivalent to another company's TTM revenue.
- Extremely fast run-rate growth raises Demand Proof priority but not Economic Scale state by itself.
- Promotion requires corroboration from realized revenue, paying usage, retention/backlog and/or cash conversion.

## Demand Breadth Ω
Measure whether demand is concentrated or broadening across:
- model providers;
- hyperscalers/clouds;
- enterprise agents/coding/productivity;
- media/video/voice applications;
- vertical applications;
- inference infrastructure.

A single vendor's growth is not industry-wide demand proof.

## Price–Volume Demand Law
Core diagnostic:
`Demand Throughput Proxy = Workload Volume × Realized Revenue per Workload`

Interpretation:
- price ↓ + volume ↑↑ enough → demand can strengthen;
- price ↓ + volume flat/down → demand warning;
- volume ↑ but gross profit/FCF deteriorates → demand exists but monetization quality is weak;
- price ↑ + volume ↑ → strong pricing/demand, subject to sustainability and concentration checks.

## Score 0–100
- Realized revenue growth / normalization quality: 20
- Paying demand / enterprise adoption: 15
- Workload/utilization evidence: 15
- Retention / expansion / backlog: 15
- Price-volume economics: 10
- Gross-profit conversion: 10
- Cash conversion / owner economics: 10
- Breadth / concentration quality: 5

## States
### D0 — UNPROVEN
Narrative/usage claims without sufficient paying-demand evidence.

### D1 — USAGE ACCELERATION
Usage or adoption is rising, but realized monetization is incomplete.

### D2 — DEMAND CONFIRMED
Multiple independent indicators confirm growing paying demand.

### D3 — MONETIZATION CONFIRMED
Demand growth is converting into realized revenue and gross profit with credible retention/backlog.

### D4 — ECONOMIC SCALE CONFIRMED
Demand, gross profit and cash conversion scale together across multiple periods with durable unit economics.

### DX — DEMAND DETERIORATION REVIEW
Usage, paying demand, retention/backlog or monetization weakens materially enough to force extraordinary review.

## Standard output
`Entity → revenue basis → paying demand → workload/utilization → price → volume → retention/backlog → gross profit → cash conversion → breadth/concentration → DEMAND STATE → confidence → Payback Ω feed → falsifiers`

## Falsifiers / downgrade triggers
- run-rate growth fails to appear in realized revenue;
- paying usage or retention deteriorates despite headline usage growth;
- price compression is not offset by volume;
- gross-profit conversion deteriorates persistently;
- demand is predominantly subsidized, circular or concentrated in financially fragile counterparties;
- multi-period cash conversion fails despite claimed scale.

## Decision authority
This engine can raise/lower AI economic-proof confidence and audit priority. It cannot issue an automatic trade. Falsifiers Ω retains independent absolute veto only on confirmed structural evidence.

## Canon amendment — 2026-08-20 — Neocloud Customer Acceptance Gate Ω

For neoclouds and material AI-infrastructure deployments, the generic demand-to-monetization chain is refined as follows:

`Contract → Capacity / Financing → Hardware Ready → Deployment / Commissioning → Customer Acceptance → Revenue Recognition → Gross Margin → OCF/FCF → ROIC`

Mandatory law: **CONTRACT != DEPLOYMENT != CUSTOMER ACCEPTANCE != REVENUE != OWNER ECONOMICS.**

- Contracted demand, take-or-pay commitments, hardware delivery and physical deployment remain maximum E2 evidence.
- Formal/equivalent customer acceptance is mandatory execution evidence between deployment and monetization where acceptance/go-live validation is economically material.
- Customer acceptance alone remains E2; it cannot be treated as revenue or Economic Scale.
- E3 requires attributable recognized revenue plus credible margin from the accepted deployment.
- E4 requires multi-period cash conversion / ROIC after E3.
- Escrow release may corroborate acceptance but is not revenue recognition.
- Rejection, disputed acceptance, SLA failure or material acceptance delay triggers `NX_EXECUTION_REVIEW`.
