# ATLAS Ω — RISK TREATMENT

Risk inputs may include permanent-loss risk, volatility, tail exposure, financing fragility, regulatory/geopolitical dependence and common-factor correlation when economically grounded.

Risk correlation may reduce portfolio utility even when names belong to different sectors. Conversely, sector overlap alone is not sufficient to impose a penalty unless it maps to common economic risk.
