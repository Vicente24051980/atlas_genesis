# ATLAS Ω — REPLACEMENT FIREWALL AUTHORITY BOUNDARY

Replacement Firewall / hysteresis is downstream execution governance.

Correct sequence:

`CLEAN_POINT_ZERO_SELECTION → DIFF_VS_CURRENT_OPERATIONAL → REPLACEMENT/CHURN/TAX/EXECUTION_REVIEW → IMPLEMENTATION`

Incorrect sequence:

`CURRENT_INCUMBENTS → REQUIRE_CHALLENGER_HURDLE → CLEAN_SELECTION`.

The firewall may prevent unnecessary turnover or stage execution. It may not grant a current holding a clean-selection bonus, win ties for incumbents, or require a challenger to overcome an incumbent-specific hurdle before the clean ranking exists.
