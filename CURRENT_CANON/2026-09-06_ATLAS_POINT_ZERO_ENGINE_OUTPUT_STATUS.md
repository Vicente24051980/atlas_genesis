# ATLAS Ω — SUBORDINATE ENGINE OUTPUT STATUS

Engine outputs should carry an authority label such as:

`DISCOVERY_ONLY`
`EVIDENCE_COMPONENT`
`RISK_DIAGNOSTIC`
`FALSIFICATION_INPUT`
`MONITORING_ONLY`
`EXECUTION_ONLY`
`SHADOW_ONLY`

Avoid unqualified `BUY`, `SELL`, `IN`, `OUT` or `CANONICAL_PORTFOLIO` labels from subordinate engines unless they explicitly refer to a post-master execution state.
