# SOVEREIGN GOLD ACCUMULATION Ω v1.0

Date: 2026-08-15
Status: ACTIVE CANONICAL REFINEMENT ENGINE

## Mission
Detect strategic sovereign accumulation of gold without confusing official central-bank demand with private Chinese demand, jewellery demand, ETF flows, price momentum, or unverifiable claims of hidden state purchases.

## Canonical laws
- REPORTED OFFICIAL PURCHASES != ESTIMATED HIDDEN PURCHASES.
- CENTRAL BANK DEMAND != PRIVATE CHINA DEMAND.
- GOLD PRICE RISE != PROOF OF SOVEREIGN FLOW.
- Buying during material price corrections strengthens evidence of strategic accumulation.
- Unverified hidden buying cannot increase Conviction Ω.
- Sovereign accumulation may support the gold macro regime but never creates an automatic BUY in miners or gold-linked equities.

## Inputs
1. Reported central-bank net purchases.
2. Persistence in consecutive buying months.
3. Whether purchases continue during gold corrections.
4. Trend in gold share of FX reserves.
5. Private investment demand: bars/coins, ETFs, imports/physical demand.
6. Jewellery demand, tracked separately.
7. Real-yield and USD regime.
8. Hidden/undeclared purchases only when independently verified.
