# ATLAS Ω — FINALIST PROMOTION GATE

Shortlist membership is not finalist status.

Promotion to final capital competition requires enough evidence to evaluate:

- thesis durability;
- valuation;
- 3–6 year Expected Return;
- permanent-loss/fragility risk;
- material catalysts;
- explicit falsifier;
- credible slot competitor;
- confidence/data cutoff.

Candidates with decisive UNKNOWN fields may remain research priorities but should not receive false precision at the final cut.
