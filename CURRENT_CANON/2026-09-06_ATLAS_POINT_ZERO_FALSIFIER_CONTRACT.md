# ATLAS Ω — POINT ZERO FALSIFIER CONTRACT

Every final candidate must have an explicit, observable and economically relevant falsifier.

A falsifier must identify evidence that would materially weaken or destroy the thesis, not merely a generic price decline.

Examples of valid falsifier classes: moat erosion, sustained market-share loss, negative revision regime, FCF/share deterioration, incremental ROIC collapse, financing fragility, dilution, regulatory impairment, customer-concentration break, valuation/expected-return compression or structural disruption.

A thesis without a credible falsifier cannot receive maximum confidence.

Falsifier Veto remains independent of current holdings and prior conviction.
