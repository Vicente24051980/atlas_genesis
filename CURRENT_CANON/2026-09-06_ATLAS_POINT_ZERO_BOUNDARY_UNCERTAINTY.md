# ATLAS Ω — CUT BOUNDARY UNCERTAINTY

When uncertainty could plausibly reverse the membership decision between the last selected and first challengers, surface that explicitly.

Possible states:

- `ROBUSTLY_SELECTED`
- `SELECTED_CLOSE_BOUNDARY`
- `CHALLENGER_CLOSE_BOUNDARY`
- `DATA_PENDING_BOUNDARY`

Do not force high confidence merely because the engine must output an ordering.
