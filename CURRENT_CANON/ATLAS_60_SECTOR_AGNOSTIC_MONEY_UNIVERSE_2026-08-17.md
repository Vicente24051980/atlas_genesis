## Cross-sector decision law
No sector quota. No minimum or maximum number from technology, defense, consumer, healthcare, financials or energy during discovery. Portfolio construction may later impose concentration controls, but discovery itself remains sector blind.

## Anti-chasing law
Recent price leadership is evidence of sponsorship, not automatic evidence of value. A company can rank high in Money Rotation Ω and low in Implied Return Ω. NO_CHASE can coexist with ELITE_CAPTOR.

## Funding-pool deduplication
Multiple suppliers paid from the same hyperscaler, sovereign program, utility budget or pharmaceutical factory program are not independent money flows. CAPEX Convergence Ω must identify shared funding pools before portfolio construction.
