# ATLAS Ω — POINT ZERO DUEL CONTRACT

Final selection is not satisfied by standalone company scores.

Mandatory competition layers:

1. economically comparable peer/substitute duels;
2. adjacent-beneficiary/capital-captor duels where economically relevant;
3. cross-sector Competition for Capital among finalists;
4. portfolio-set marginal contribution test.

Every selected finalist should identify at least one credible `SLOT_COMPETITOR` capable of taking its place if evidence, valuation or expected return changes.

A company that is excellent in isolation can still be excluded if another candidate offers superior scarce-capital utility.
