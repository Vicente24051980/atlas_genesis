# BOTTLENECK OWNERSHIP Ω v2.0

**Status:** ACTIVE / TRANSVERSAL RESEARCH ENGINE  
**Effective date:** 2026-09-17  
**Implementation:** `src/atlas/algorithm/bottleneck-ownership-omega.ts`  
**Tests:** `src/atlas/algorithm/bottleneck-ownership-omega.test.ts`

## Mission

Identify companies that control scarce, economically necessary nodes in a CAPEX chain, prove that scarcity converts into revenue/margin/free cash flow, and detect when the bottleneck is being destroyed by new capacity, substitution, concentration or cyclicality.

The engine answers:

**What resource or capability must the next dollar of funded CAPEX buy, who controls it, and does that control survive long enough to convert into cash economics?**

It is not a BUY engine and has no portfolio authority.

## Canonical causal chain

**FUNDED DEMAND → PHYSICAL/TECHNICAL NECESSITY → SCARCITY → CONTROL POINT → ECONOMIC CAPTURE → FCF CONVERSION → DURATION.**

ATLAS must not reverse this chain from price performance or thematic popularity.

## Relationship to existing canon

Bottleneck Ownership Ω does not replace:

- `CAPEX_HUNTERS_OMEGA.md` — discovers captors of external CAPEX and classifies H1–H7 roles;
- `AI_INFRA_BOTTLENECK_UNIVERSE_OMEGA.md` — defines the AI infrastructure bottleneck universe and Technology–Capacity Lag Ω;
- `GLOBAL_CAPEX_CHAIN_OMEGA.md` — maps funding pools and transmission chains;
- `AI_CAPEX_PAYBACK_OMEGA` — tests customer-side economic payback;
- valuation, Money Rotation Ω, Principal Ω, Falsifiers Ω or Portfolio Risk & Capital Allocation Ω.

It deepens the `H2_BOTTLENECK_OWNER` path by making bottleneck durability and destruction explicitly measurable.

## Evidence gate

Confirmed ownership requires all of the following:

1. traceable evidence;
2. Economic Proof **E2 or higher**;
3. at least two evidence records.

E0 narrative and E1 management claims cannot confirm a bottleneck owner.

**SCARCITY ≠ MOAT. BACKLOG ≠ REVENUE ≠ FCF. BOTTLENECK OWNER ≠ BUY.**

## Base Ownership Score Ω — 0 to 100

Weighted research score:

- Economic necessity: **15%**
- Scarcity: **15%**
- Control point / qualification / switching cost / IP: **15%**
- Pricing power: **10%**
- Economic capture: **15%**
- FCF conversion: **15%**
- Duration / persistence: **15%**

Valuation is deliberately excluded.

## Bottleneck Destruction Risk Ω — 0 to 100

A separate adversarial score measures how quickly scarcity rents can disappear:

- Capacity expansion risk: **30%**
- Substitution risk: **30%**
- Customer concentration risk: **20%**
- Cyclicality risk: **20%**

States:

- **LOW:** <30
- **MODERATE:** 30–54.9
- **HIGH:** 55–74.9
- **CRITICAL:** ≥75

## Adjusted Ownership Score Ω

`Adjusted Ownership Score = Base Ownership Score - 0.40 × Bottleneck Destruction Risk`

This is a research-priority score, not expected return and not a portfolio score.

## States

- **ELITE_OWNER:** adjusted score ≥85, confirmed evidence, economic capture/FCF proven, destruction risk below HIGH.
- **STRONG_OWNER:** adjusted score ≥75.
- **EMERGING_OWNER:** adjusted score ≥65.
- **WATCH:** adjusted score ≥55 or material unresolved durability issue.
- **BOTTLENECK_ONLY:** physical scarcity exists but economic capture <50 or FCF conversion <45.
- **SCARCITY_AT_RISK:** destruction risk is CRITICAL despite current bottleneck evidence.
- **INSUFFICIENT:** no durable structural edge supported.
- **EVIDENCE_PENDING:** evidence gate not confirmed.

## Mandatory falsifiers

Escalate or downgrade when evidence shows any of the following:

- usable capacity expands faster than demand;
- lead times and realized pricing normalize without offsetting volume economics;
- qualified substitutes reduce control-point power;
- backlog/contracts are cancelled or deferred;
- revenue growth fails to convert into margin and FCF;
- customer or funding-pool concentration breaks demand durability;
- technology migration moves the constraint before capacity pays back;
- synchronized industry CAPEX creates oversupply and destroys scarcity rents.

## Integration with Technology–Capacity Lag Ω

For long-lead capacity, Bottleneck Ownership Ω consumes the distinction:

- `SCARCITY_NOW`
- `CAPACITY_PAYBACK_LATER`

Current scarcity can strengthen near-term economics while simultaneously increasing future overshoot risk. Never infer future scarcity from present scarcity.

## Mandatory output

`Ticker | Layer | Economic Proof E0-E4 | Evidence Gate | Base Ownership Score | Destruction Risk Score/State | Adjusted Ownership Score | Economic Capture | FCF Conversion | Valuation Risk (separate) | State | Falsifiers | Next Action`

## Anti-error laws

- `CAPACITY_ADDED != THROUGHPUT_CREATED`
- `SCARCITY != PERMANENT_MOAT`
- `BACKLOG != REVENUE != FCF`
- `BOTTLENECK_OWNER != AUTOMATIC_BUY`
- `VALUATION != BOTTLENECK_OWNERSHIP_SCORE`
- `PRICE_ACTION != FUNDAMENTAL_PROOF`
- Falsifiers Ω retains independent absolute veto.

## Final law

**Do not search for the next famous winner. Search for the unavoidable purchase, prove who gets paid, and measure how quickly the scarcity can disappear.**
