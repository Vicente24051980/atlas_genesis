# ATLAS Ω — TAPE / RS AUTHORITY BOUNDARY

Tape, relative strength, volume, options and price reaction may provide complementary confirmation, timing context, crowding information or evidence of expectation changes.

They may not substitute for fundamentals, valuation, Expected Return, balance-sheet quality or falsifier analysis.

A technically strong company with insufficient fundamental/valuation evidence cannot enter solely because of Tape/RS.

A technically weak but fundamentally superior candidate is not automatically excluded; the technical state must be interpreted in its declared complementary role.
