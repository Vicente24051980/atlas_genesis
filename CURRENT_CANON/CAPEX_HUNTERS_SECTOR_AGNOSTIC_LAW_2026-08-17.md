# CAPEX HUNTERS Ω — SECTOR-AGNOSTIC LAW

**Status:** ACTIVE
**Effective date:** 2026-08-17

## Law

CAPEX Hunters Ω is prohibited from privileging technology, AI, semiconductors, utilities, industrials, defense, healthcare, consumer staples or any other sector merely because that sector is fashionable or already represented in ATLAS.

The engine follows the economic flow wherever it is strongest. If the best verified capital capture is in PepsiCo, Coca-Cola, a bottler, a packaging supplier, a pharmaceutical plant, an insurer, an aerospace supplier, a grid contractor, a turbine maker or a software platform, the sector label is irrelevant.

## Mandatory sequence

1. Identify the source of economic spending.
2. Identify the independent funding pool.
3. Map the transmission chain.
4. Identify the bottleneck/toll road.
5. Require Economic Proof E2+.
6. Test orders/backlog -> revenue -> margin -> OCF/FCF -> incremental ROIC.
7. Test funding quality and financed-demand dependence.
8. Compare valuation and implied return.
9. Only after these steps may Portfolio Construction Ω act.

## Anti-bias rules

- SECTOR ≠ THESIS.
- AI LABEL ≠ PRIORITY.
- CONSUMER STAPLES ≠ AUTOMATIC DEFENSIVE BUY.
- DEFENSE BUDGET ≠ AUTOMATIC CONTRACTOR BUY.
- PHARMA CAPEX ≠ AUTOMATIC PHARMA BUY.
- BIG BACKLOG ≠ CASH FLOW.
- HIGH CAPEX CAPTURE ≠ ATTRACTIVE VALUATION.
- PORTFOLIO FAMILIARITY ≠ RESEARCH PRIORITY.

**ATLAS follows verified economic money, not sectors.**

**Where the money originates -> where it must pass -> who has scarcity -> who converts it to FCF/ROIC -> what price the market asks.**
