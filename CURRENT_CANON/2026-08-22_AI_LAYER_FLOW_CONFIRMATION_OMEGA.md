## Purpose
Extend AI Layer Rotation Ω so layer transitions are not inferred from price alone. Separate AI CAPEX transmission from commodity-cycle, USD, geopolitical and systematic trend-following effects.

## Core state

`C1 🟢🟢🟢 → C2 🟢🟢 → C3 🟡 | BARRIER | C4–C6 🔴`
Verdict: `SALTO EN VIGILANCIA C1→C2. NO ROTAR.`

## FLOW CONFIRMATION Ω
For every layer C1–C19 record independently:
- price trend and acceleration
- breadth: winners/eligible names and participation dispersion
- relative strength versus market and adjacent layers
- persistence across intraday, 1D, 5D and 1M windows when verified
- systematic-flow/CTA positioning when evidenced
- macro confounders: USD, rates, commodity beta, geopolitics and broad risk-on/off
- Economic Proof versus Equity Monetization

No single input is sufficient to confirm a layer jump.

## Transition logic
1. `C1/C2 leadership ↓ + C3 RS/breadth ↑` => WATCH.
2. `C3 confirms + C4 begins ↑` => PARTIAL TRANSITION.
3. `C3+C4+C5 confirm persistently` => `SALTO CONFIRMADO` and capital may begin moving up-chain.
4. `C1/C2 remain strong + C3–C5 strengthen` => `BROADENING`, not substitution. Do not require selling upstream winners merely because downstream layers activate.
5. `C1 strong + copper CTA stretched + C2/C3 fail to broaden` => `C1 SATURATION RISK`.
6. `C1 strong + C2 strong + C3 begins ↑` => `BROADENING WATCH`.

## CTA / systematic-flow interpretation
CTA positioning is confirmation/context, never Economic Proof and never a standalone BUY/SELL trigger.

- Stretched long positioning can support momentum while increasing reversal/unwind risk.
- Under-owned assets with improving fast-trend signals may possess additional systematic buying capacity, but this remains a hypothesis until price/breadth/persistence confirm.
- Equity CTA exposure creates convexity risk: broad-market drawdowns can force deleveraging even when an individual layer thesis remains fundamentally intact.

## Causal attribution gate
Before attributing a layer move to AI CAPEX, test competing explanations:
`AI physical demand | commodity cycle | USD | rates | geopolitics | CTA/trend following | broad equity beta`.

A layer transition gains confidence when downstream manufactured/infrastructure beneficiaries strengthen while the explanation cannot be reduced to the underlying commodity alone.

Example diagnostic:
If copper miners are strong but cable/electrical/grid names remain weak, classify as upstream/resource leadership.
If cable/connectors strengthen broadly while C1/C2 remain strong, classify as transmission/broadening evidence.

## Rotation guardrail
Never rotate solely because the clock reaches a fixed interval after market open or because an upstream ticker is red/green. Rotation requires state transition evidence. Opening gaps are observations, not automatic entry/exit signals.

## Performance / Decision Ledger Ω
Persist one observation per evaluation timestamp with:
- timestamp and market session

This ledger must permit retrospective testing of whether detected C1→C2→C3 transitions led future equity leadership or merely followed it.
