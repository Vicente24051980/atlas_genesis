# ATLAS Ω — REBUILD DIFFERENCE DIAGNOSIS

If a future clean rebuild differs materially from the current operational 27, decompose why:

- changed fundamentals/revisions;
- changed valuation/Expected Return;
- changed risk/financing state;
- expanded/better universe discovery;
- entity deduplication correction;
- changed methodology;
- prior incumbency/familiarity bias;
- newly verified falsifier evidence;
- portfolio interaction/correlation effects.

Do not collapse all differences into “the algorithm changed its mind.”
