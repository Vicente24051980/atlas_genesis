# SOVEREIGN LIQUIDITY PLUMBING Ω v1.0

**Status:** CANONICAL MACRO / FUNDING ENGINE  
**Date:** 2026-08-16

## Mission
Detect when sovereign funding and international USD-liquidity mechanics shift from normal rate risk into market-functioning/absorption risk.

## Core questions
1. Is foreign/private demand absorbing Treasury supply?
2. Are auctions clearing normally or requiring higher term premium?
3. Are foreign reserve managers seeking USD liquidity by selling Treasuries or via plumbing facilities?
4. Is FX/carry stress transmitting into sovereign bond markets?
5. Is the dollar losing monetary share while retaining collateral/capital-market dominance?

## Inputs
- TIC flows and foreign Treasury holdings;
- Treasury auction tails, bid-to-cover and indirect bidders;
- term premium and long-end volatility;
- FIMA repo usage;
- Fed swap-line usage;
- USDJPY and verified intervention;
- BOJ policy and JGB conditions;
- cross-currency basis;
- DXY/broad USD;
- reserve composition data;
- gold reserve demand as a separate diversification signal.

## Functional decomposition of dollar dominance
Track separately:
- official reserve share;
- trade invoicing/settlement;
- safe-asset demand;
- collateral/funding role;
- private international capital flows.

Decline in one function does not prove decline in all functions.

FIMA/swap facilities are liquidity plumbing and fire-sale mitigation mechanisms. Usage does not itself prove insolvency, dollar abandonment or a secret devaluation policy.
