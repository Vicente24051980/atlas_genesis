# ATLAS Ω — LOW STRUCTURAL VOLATILITY OBJECTIVE

The objective combines high expected compound return with protection against permanent loss, fragility and economically meaningful volatility/risk.

Low volatility is not permission to fill the portfolio with lower-return defensive names or to impose sector diversification quotas.

The relevant question is whether a candidate or portfolio set improves expected return/risk utility over 3–6 years, including downside asymmetry, balance-sheet resilience, funding dependence and correlated shocks.
