# ATLAS Ω — Time-in-Market Opportunity Cost + Capital Purpose Ω v1.0

**Status:** ACTIVE / CANONICAL MODULE
**Effective date:** 2026-08-20
**Authority:** compatible with ATLAS Ω ENTERPRISE Prompt Maestro Mini v3.1 and all current canonical gates.

## Purpose
Prevent two symmetric capital-allocation errors: (1) buying indiscriminately because long horizons are assumed to cure valuation risk, and (2) remaining in cash indefinitely while waiting for a perfect entry. Every euro must have an explicit economic purpose and its opportunity cost must be measurable.

## Canonical laws
- LONG HORIZON ≠ PRICE IRRELEVANT.
- VALUATION DISCIPLINE ≠ PERMANENT WAITING.
- CASH IS A POSITION WITH EXPECTED RETURN AND OPPORTUNITY COST.
- TIME IN MARKET ≠ LICENSE TO IGNORE FALSIFIERS.
- COMPOUNDING CLAIMS MUST PASS QUANTITATIVE INTEGRITY Ω.
- NOMINAL WEALTH ≠ REAL PURCHASING POWER.
- INDEX SURVIVORSHIP ≠ SINGLE-STOCK SURVIVORSHIP.
- CAPITAL WITHOUT PURPOSE ≠ OPTIMAL LIQUIDITY.

## Engine 1 — Time-in-Market Opportunity Cost Ω
Pipeline:
HORIZON → CURRENT CASH YIELD → EXPECTED ASSET RETURN → VALUATION/IMPLIED RETURN → ENTRY IMPROVEMENT SCENARIOS → PROBABILITY/WAIT TIME → DRAWDOWN RISK AVOIDED → FORGONE COMPOUNDING → TAX/EXECUTION FRICTION → COST OF WAITING Ω → ACTION.

Core conceptual metric:
COST_OF_WAITING = EXPECTED_RETURN_FORFEITED_DURING_WAIT - EXPECTED_BENEFIT_OF_IMPROVED_ENTRY - RISK_AVOIDED_WHILE_IN_CASH.

No universal numeric threshold is canonical until empirically calibrated. Scenario ranges and probability-weighted estimates must be shown rather than false precision.

States:
- TMW0_DEPLOYMENT_JUSTIFIED
- TMW1_STAGED_ENTRY
- TMW2_WAITING_JUSTIFIED
- TMW3_WAITING_COST_RISING
- TMW4_CASH_OPTIONALITY_VALUABLE
- TMW5_DATA_INSUFFICIENT

## Engine 2 — Capital Purpose Ω
Every euro is assigned to exactly one primary bucket before portfolio optimization:
- CP0_OPERATING_LIQUIDITY
- CP1_SAFETY_RESERVE
- CP2_STRUCTURAL_COMPOUNDING
- CP3_OPTIONALITY_RESERVE
- CP4_TACTICAL_OPPORTUNITY
- CP5_UNASSIGNED_VIOLATION

For invested capital, record:
CAPITAL_UNIT → POSITION → THESIS_STATE → EXPECTED_RETURN → RISK → HORIZON → LIQUIDITY_NEED → BEST_ALTERNATIVE → REPLACEMENT_SPREAD → PURPOSE_STATE.

CP5 is a governance warning, not an automatic instruction to invest. Safety reserves and optionality are legitimate purposes.

## Quantitative Integrity gate for compounding claims
Any educational or external compounding example must specify and reconcile:
INITIAL_CAPITAL; CONTRIBUTION_AMOUNT; CONTRIBUTION_FREQUENCY; CONTRIBUTION_TIMING; NOMINAL_RETURN; COMPOUNDING_FREQUENCY; HORIZON; FEES; TAXES; INFLATION; NOMINAL_END_VALUE; REAL_END_VALUE.

If published outputs cannot be reproduced from stated assumptions, label QUANT_MISMATCH. Do not import the numerical claim into ATLAS evidence.

## Interaction with existing engines
- Entry Timing Ω asks whether current execution conditions are acceptable.
- Market Bottom Detection Ω estimates dislocation/bottom evidence but cannot justify indefinite waiting.
- Good Companies Cheap Ω seeks valuation asymmetry but must account for foregone compounding.
- Optionality Reserve Ω values cash as an option rather than treating all cash as idle.
- Capital Safety & Leverage Discipline Ω protects required liquidity and solvency.
- Capital Replacement Ω compares current positions with alternatives through Replacement Spread Ω.
- Winner Preservation Ω prevents opportunity-cost logic from mechanically selling exceptional compounders.
- Falsifiers Ω retains absolute independent veto.

## Decision rule
Time-in-Market Opportunity Cost Ω never creates a BUY by itself. It contributes an independent estimate of the cost/benefit of waiting. Final deployment still requires valid thesis, evidence integrity, valuation/implied-return gate, portfolio constraints and falsifier review.

## Evidence classification
External educational newsletters are SOURCE_INPUT only. Metaphors and marketing language are not investment evidence. Separate FACT / HYPOTHESIS / INTERPRETATION / NOISE.

## Final law
The system must price both sides of timing: the risk of deploying too early and the economic cost of waiting too long. Every euro may legitimately remain liquid, but never without an explicit purpose and opportunity-cost accounting.
