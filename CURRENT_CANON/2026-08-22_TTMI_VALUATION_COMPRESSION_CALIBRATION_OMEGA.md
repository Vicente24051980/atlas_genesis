## Purpose
Use TTMI as a calibration case showing that genuine AI Economic Proof and strong business execution do not guarantee positive equity returns when entry valuation embeds excessive expectations.

## Canonical separation
`ECONOMIC PROOF != EQUITY MONETIZATION != EXPECTED RETURN`
`GREAT COMPANY != GREAT ENTRY`
`AI WINNER != POSITIVE EXPECTED RETURN`
`FAIR VALUE MODEL != FACT`
`PRICE COLLAPSE AFTER OVERVALUATION != PROOF THAT ONE VALUATION MODEL WAS CAUSALLY CORRECT`

## TTMI calibration
TTMI belongs to the PCB/components zone of the AI physical infrastructure chain (C9 reference classification). Evidence can simultaneously show strong AI/data-center demand and weak subsequent equity monetization through valuation compression.

The July-August 2026 episode is stored as a calibration example, not as proof that any one third-party fair-value model is authoritative. External valuation models materially disagree and can change as earnings forecasts, discount rates, multiples and assumptions change.

## Valuation Compression Risk Ω
For every ticker with strong thematic/Economic Proof, independently test:
1. verified P0 under Price & Target Integrity Gate Ω;
2. revenue/EPS/FCF trajectory;
3. current and normalized valuation multiples;
4. reverse valuation — what growth/margins/duration are embedded in P0;
5. bull/base/bear valuation scenarios;
6. Expected Return from verified P0, not historical return;
7. margin of safety;
8. sensitivity to multiple compression even if operating estimates are met;
9. consensus/target dispersion and model disagreement;
10. falsifiers.

## Hard gate
A layer becoming GANANDO or SALTO CONFIRMADO cannot automatically promote every constituent to BUY. Layer leadership is an opportunity-discovery signal. Each ticker must still pass Motor 13 Expected Return Ω and valuation-compression checks.

Possible state:
`LAYER = GANANDO` + `ECONOMIC PROOF = PASS` + `BUSINESS QUALITY = PASS` + `EXPECTED RETURN = FAIL` => `NO BUY / WATCH`.

## Fair-value integrity
Third-party fair value is an input, never evidence of intrinsic value by itself. Store model name, observation date, P0, assumptions when available, and model output. Never silently merge incompatible fair-value methodologies.

A later price decline toward a prior fair-value estimate must not be labeled model validation without testing alternative causes and whether the model assumptions remained unchanged.

## TTMI lesson
TTMI is a canonical warning against paying any price for a real AI beneficiary. The correct question is not only whether AI demand is real, but how much of that future is already capitalized into P0 and what return remains for the new shareholder.
