# ATLAS Ω — CAPITAL REPLACEMENT Ω v1.0

**Status:** CANONICAL / ACTIVE  
**Effective date:** 2026-08-20  
**Compatibility:** ATLAS Ω ENTERPRISE — PROMPT MAESTRO MINI v3.1  
**Authority:** independent exit/reallocation engine; no automatic SELL authority.

## Mission

Determine when an active position is no longer the best marginal use of capital even if the underlying business thesis remains valid.

Capital Replacement Ω is explicitly separate from Money Rotation Ω, Institutional Capital Rotation Ω and Falsifiers Ω. It evaluates forward expected return and opportunity cost, not past performance.

## Core laws

- **LARGE GAIN ≠ SELL SIGNAL.**
- **OVERVALUATION ≠ FUNDAMENTAL FALSIFIER.**
- **THESIS ACTIVE ≠ HOLD FOREVER.**
- **THESIS ACTIVE ≠ CAPITAL MUST REMAIN ALLOCATED.**
- **PAST RETURN ≠ FORWARD EXPECTED RETURN.**
- **MOMENTUM LOSS ≠ FUNDAMENTAL BREAK.**
- **SELL DECISION = CURRENT EXPECTED RETURN vs BEST AVAILABLE ALTERNATIVE, subject to evidence and friction.**
- **WINNER PRESERVATION and CAPITAL REPLACEMENT must be evaluated adversarially, not sequentially as automatic overrides.**

## Mandatory pipeline

**Position → Thesis State → Economic Proof Δ → Consensus/Revisions Δ → Valuation/Implied Return → Momentum/Relative-Strength Inflection → Event Risk → Alternative Opportunity Set → Tax/Execution Friction → Replacement Spread Ω → Winner Preservation Challenge → Falsifiers Review → KEEP / TRIM / EXIT / REDEPLOY.**

## Replacement Spread Ω

`Replacement Spread Ω = Expected Return(best eligible alternative) − Expected Return(current position) − rotation frictions`

Rotation frictions include at minimum:

- taxes where relevant;
- spread/fees/slippage;
- FX friction;
- liquidity/market-impact risk;
- timing and event-gap risk;
- loss of optionality from exiting a proven compounder;
- evidence uncertainty in the replacement candidate.

No universal numerical threshold is canonical until empirical calibration. The spread must be material relative to uncertainty and supported by independent evidence.

## Required evidence fields

For every position evaluated:

**THESIS_STATE → ECONOMIC_PROOF_DELTA → REVENUE/EPS/FCF_REVISIONS → IMPLIED_RETURN → VALUATION_EXPECTATION_DENSITY → MOMENTUM_STATE → RELATIVE_STRENGTH_STATE → VOLATILITY_STATE → EVENT_RISK → CROWDING → ALTERNATIVE_SET → ALTERNATIVE_EXPECTED_RETURN → ROTATION_FRICTION → REPLACEMENT_SPREAD → WINNER_PRESERVATION_STATE → FALSIFIER_STATE → DECISION.**

## Decision states

- `CR0_KEEP_CORE`: thesis + economics + forward return remain superior; no credible replacement spread.
- `CR1_KEEP_WATCH`: thesis active but expected return compressed or one warning layer emerging.
- `CR2_TRIM_CANDIDATE`: replacement spread is positive and material, but uncertainty or Winner Preservation still argues against full exit.
- `CR3_EXIT_FOR_REPLACEMENT`: thesis may remain active, but forward expected return is materially inferior to an eligible alternative after frictions.
- `CR4_EXIT_FUNDAMENTAL`: independent Falsifiers Ω confirms material thesis break; this is not a Capital Replacement-only decision.
- `CR5_NO_ACTION_DATA_INSUFFICIENT`: evidence does not support rotation.

## Evidence gate

Capital Replacement Ω cannot issue `CR3_EXIT_FOR_REPLACEMENT` solely because of:

- a 40%, 100%, 200% or larger gain;
- a new all-time high;
- price above analyst targets;
- one valuation multiple;
- one weak session/week;
- momentum loss without forward-return compression;
- a replacement candidate with lower quality but recent stronger price action;
- marketing claims or model backtests lacking complete trade history and methodology.

At least three independent layers must support a replacement decision, and one must be **forward expected return / valuation** or **material revisions/economic-proof deterioration**. Winner Preservation Ω must be explicitly challenged before full exit of a proven compounder.

## Interaction with Winner Preservation Ω

Winner Preservation Ω asks: **Are we at risk of selling a structurally exceptional compounder too early?**

Capital Replacement Ω asks: **Is this still the best risk-adjusted use of the next euro of capital?**

Neither overrides the other. Cross-Engine Synthesis Ω must resolve contradictions using evidence quality, implied return, economic proof, moat durability, alternative-set quality and falsifiers.

## Interaction with Falsifiers Ω

A replacement exit does **not** require a fundamental falsifier. Therefore:

- `CR3_EXIT_FOR_REPLACEMENT` may coexist with `THESIS_ACTIVE`.
- `CR4_EXIT_FUNDAMENTAL` requires Falsifiers Ω confirmation.
- valuation excess is not relabeled as a falsifier.

## Interaction with Money Rotation Ω

Money Rotation Ω may identify where capital is currently moving, but price leadership or flow alone cannot qualify a replacement candidate. A replacement must pass the full ATLAS evidence stack, Expected Return Ω and Trading 212 availability if it is intended to become actionable.

**MARKET_CAP_CHANGE ≠ CAPITAL_FLOW.**  
**RELATIVE_STRENGTH ≠ VERIFIED FUND FLOW.**

## Calibration rule

Promotional case studies such as ProPicks AI exits may be used only as `EXTERNAL_CALIBRATION_CASE`, never as evidence that the external strategy has persistent alpha until full trade history, benchmark construction, drawdowns, turnover, survivorship/selection bias, transaction costs and methodology are independently auditable.

## Portfolio authority

Capital Replacement Ω can generate `KEEP / TRIM_CANDIDATE / EXIT_FOR_REPLACEMENT` recommendations for Investment Committee Ω, but it does not modify the portfolio automatically. Portfolio changes require Cross-Engine Synthesis Ω, Decision Safety Gate Ω and explicit execution authority under the active ATLAS governance.

## Canonical output

**Current position → thesis state → forward expected return → warnings → best eligible alternative → replacement spread → Winner Preservation challenge → falsifiers → friction → decision state → permitted action.**
