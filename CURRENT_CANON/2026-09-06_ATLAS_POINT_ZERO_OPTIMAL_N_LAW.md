# ATLAS Ω — OPTIMAL_N LAW

`OPTIMAL_N` is a result, never an input preference.

Forbidden canonical behavior:

- `N must be at least 20`;
- `N must not exceed 35/37/50`;
- `target N = 27/30/32/etc.`;
- adding low/negative marginal utility names to hit a floor;
- rejecting positive marginal contributors merely because a ceiling is reached;
- calibrating complexity penalties specifically to recover a preferred historical N.

Canonical stopping rule: expand while the next best feasible portfolio at N+1 materially improves total return/risk utility; stop when it does not.

The finite eligible universe is the natural absolute upper bound.
