# ATLAS Ω — POINT ZERO ANTI-BIAS CONTRACT

Every canonical rebuild must explicitly audit whether its result could be explained by:

- incumbency bias;
- megacap bias;
- familiarity bias;
- recency bias;
- AI narrative bias;
- data-availability bias;
- index-membership bias;
- historical ATLAS conviction bias;
- false diversification.

A passing anti-bias audit requires evidence that current holdings were not privileged, raw duplicates were neutralized, market cap/fame/index membership carried zero score, external challengers received zero external-status bonus, and correlated risks were modeled economically rather than through aesthetic quotas.
