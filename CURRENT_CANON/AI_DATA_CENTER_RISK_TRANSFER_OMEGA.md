## 1 — Mission

Detect whether the AI/data-center CAPEX buildout is creating a durable second-order **risk-transfer bottleneck** and identify which listed companies, if any, convert that bottleneck into attributable transactions, revenue, margin, FCF and ROIC.

Canonical causal chain:

`AI / DATA-CENTER CAPEX → INSURABLE ASSET CONCENTRATION → PROTECTION GAP / CAPACITY DEMAND → PRIMARY INSURANCE → REINSURANCE → ALTERNATIVE CAPITAL / ILS → COMPANY-ATTRIBUTABLE REVENUE & MARGIN → FCF / ROIC → E2 FRU-MATH`

This is a **derived CAPEX branch**, not a new independent funding river.

`DATA-CENTER RISK-TRANSFER PREMIUM POOL ≠ NEW CAPEX FUNDING POOL`

Therefore it must not receive an extra CAPEX Convergence credit when the economic source is the same hyperscaler/data-center project already counted upstream.

## 4 — Two-axis architecture

Market formation and company monetization must never be collapsed into one score.

### Axis A — Market maturity

- `DCRT_M0_NARRATIVE` — thematic discussion only.
- `DCRT_M1_PROTECTION_GAP_PROVEN` — concentrated TIV / capacity gap evidenced.
- `DCRT_M2_DEDICATED_CAPACITY_FORMING` — dedicated data-centre insurance facility/exchange/program exists.
- `DCRT_M3_REINSURANCE_TRANSFER_ACTIVE` — a dedicated reinsurance transaction/portfolio transfer is evidenced.

`20% Causal Exposure + 25% Attributable Transaction Proof + 25% Revenue/Margin Attribution + 20% FCF/ROIC Conversion + 10% Capital-Light Capture`

This measures whether a listed company captures economics from the market.

No score can override the proof stage.

## 7 — Risk Transfer Fragility Ω · separate vector

`35% Modelability Gap + 35% Retained Tail Risk + 30% Geographic/Client Accumulation Risk`

Interpretation:

- high opportunity + low fragility = preferred research geometry;
- high opportunity + high fragility = potentially valuable but dangerous underwriting/capital exposure;
- broker/structurer economics and balance-sheet underwriting economics must not be conflated.

Perils tracked independently:

- natural catastrophe;
- severe convective storm / hail / tornado;
- fire;
- escaped liquids / water damage;
- power outage;
- business interruption;
- cyber;
- terrorism / sabotage;
- other emerging operational perils.

7. `DEDICATED FACILITY CAPACITY ≠ UTILIZED CAPACITY`.
8. `ISSUANCE / CAPACITY ≠ ECONOMIC PROFIT`.
9. `MARKET GROWTH ≠ COMPANY ALPHA`.

## 10 — Promotion gates

### R0 → E1 priority

Require at least one of:

- new dedicated data-centre facility/exchange;
- named reinsurance program;
- transaction-level evidence;
- dedicated alternative-capital placement;
- company disclosure of attributable premiums/fees/revenue;
- modeling/aggregation breakthrough that changes capacity economics.

### Company C1 → C2

Require identifiable company-attributable placement, contract, transaction, quota share, sidecar, bond structuring mandate or comparable economic event.

### C2 → C3

Require reported or otherwise defensibly attributable revenue and margin economics. Gross market premium is insufficient.

### C3 → C4

Require multi-period cash/ROIC proof after losses, capital charges, brokerage economics, commissions, retrocession/hedging and relevant operating costs.

### C3/C4 → E2 FRU-MATH

Handoff only. FRU-MATH remains the sole return-normalization framework and must model the company using its appropriate economic architecture. No risk-transfer score is added to IRR.

## 11 — Kill / falsification conditions

- protection gap disappears because traditional capacity expands faster than demand;
- dedicated capacity repeatedly goes unused;
- data-centre premium growth materially undershoots insured-value growth;
- pricing/spreads fall faster than exposure volume grows;
- dedicated reinsurance/ILS transactions fail to repeat or scale;
- modeling error, correlated loss or accumulation creates adverse economics;
- broker/structurer activity does not become material revenue/margin;
- reinsurer premium growth fails to convert into normalized underwriting profit/FCF/ROIC;
- client/geographic concentration creates unacceptable tail exposure;
- valuation discounts substantially more capture than can be economically attributed.
