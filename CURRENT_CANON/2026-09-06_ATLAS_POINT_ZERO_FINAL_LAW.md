# ATLAS Ω — POINT ZERO FINAL LAW

**No intentes justificar la cartera que ya tenemos. Intenta derrotarla.**

Operational meaning:

- every canonical entity begins from zero;
- the current portfolio has no presumption of survival;
- challengers have no presumption of inferiority;
- the rebuild must be capable of returning the same portfolio, a partially changed portfolio or a completely different portfolio;
- similarity to the current portfolio is evidence only if it emerges after the independent process, never because the process was seeded to preserve it.
