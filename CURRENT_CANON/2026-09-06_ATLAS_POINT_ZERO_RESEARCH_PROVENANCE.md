# ATLAS Ω — RESEARCH PROVENANCE WITHOUT INHERITED CONVICTION

Historical research remains valuable as sourced evidence, definitions, hypotheses, prior falsifiers and audit trail.

But at Point Zero:

- prior thesis confidence resets;
- prior portfolio membership resets;
- prior ATLAS score is not inherited;
- old valuation is stale unless refreshed;
- old Expected Return is stale unless refreshed;
- old catalysts/falsifiers must be checked for continued validity.

The rule is: **preserve evidence provenance, not portfolio privilege**.
