# ATLAS Ω — DATA_FAIL TREATMENT

`DATA_FAIL` means evidence/identity/measurement is insufficient for a valid conclusion. It does not automatically mean the business is poor.

At screening, a data failure should be logged and, if potentially decision-relevant, queued for resolution. At the final cut, unresolved decisive data failure reduces confidence and may prevent a positive membership claim.

`DATA_FAIL ≠ NO_BUY_BY_DEFINITION`
