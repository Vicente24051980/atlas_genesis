# PROPRIETARY INTELLIGENCE MOAT Ω v1.0

**Status:** CANONICAL MOAT LAYER  
**Date:** 2026-08-18

## Mission
Measure whether a company is building durable AI-era advantage from proprietary data, agentic skills, embedded workflows and learning loops rather than merely consuming broadly available foundation models.

## Core law
`AI adoption != AI moat`

Candidate moat pattern:
`proprietary data + proprietary skills + embedded workflow + continuous feedback + switching costs → compounding intelligence advantage`

## Four pillars
### P1 — Proprietary Data
Unique operational, customer, industrial, transaction, sensor or domain data that is difficult for competitors to recreate legally, economically or temporally.

### P2 — Proprietary Skills
Codified company-specific rules, processes, decision frameworks, agent skills, physical skills and exception-handling knowledge.

### P3 — Embedded Workflow
AI is integrated into customer or internal operating workflows such that replacement carries meaningful retraining, integration, data-migration or operational cost.

### P4 — Learning Loop
Every interaction, transaction, deployment or physical execution produces feedback that improves the system, creating a closed loop:
`usage → data → model/skill improvement → better outcome → more usage`.

## Mandatory variables
- years/depth of proprietary data history;
- exclusivity and replicability of data;
- frequency and quality of feedback loops;
- degree of workflow embedding;
- customer switching friction;
- ability to codify domain know-how into reusable agent/physical skills;
- rate at which deployed systems improve with use;
- evidence of superior outcomes vs generic-model baselines;
- dependency on third-party models and cloud providers;
- portability of the moat across model providers;
- monetization via pricing, retention, margin or share gains.

## State machine
- `PIM0_NONE` — AI use without proprietary intelligence advantage.
- `PIM1_SEED` — proprietary data/skills exist but are weakly monetized or weakly looped.
- `PIM2_EMBEDDED` — meaningful workflow embedding and recurring feedback.
- `PIM3_COMPOUNDING` — measurable learning-loop improvement plus switching costs and economic capture.
- `PIM4_STRUCTURAL_MOAT` — proprietary intelligence compounds across customers/deployments and remains durable across changes in base models.

## Red flags / downgrade patterns
- generic model substitution leaves economics unchanged;
- proprietary data is not unique or cannot be legally/operationally reused;
- learning loops are weak or manual;
- customer workflows are shallow and easily switched;
- third-party model provider captures most economics;
- model commoditization erodes pricing power;
- superior technical outcomes fail to convert into retention, margin, FCF or ROIC.

## Portability test
A strong proprietary intelligence moat should survive a foundation-model swap.

Test:
`If model provider changes, do data, skills, workflow embedding, learning loops and customer control remain?`

If the answer is no, the advantage is primarily rented intelligence, not owned intelligence.

## Integration

## Decision authority
No automatic BUY. Promotion from Discovery Ω requires independent evidence that proprietary intelligence converts into revenue, margins, FCF/share and/or incremental ROIC. Falsifiers Ω retains absolute veto.
