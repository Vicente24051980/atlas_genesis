# ATLAS Ω — UNKNOWN RULE

If a material variable cannot be verified at the declared data cutoff:

1. mark it `UNKNOWN`;
2. identify why it matters;
3. reduce confidence appropriately;
4. avoid fabricating a point estimate merely to complete a table;
5. if the unknown is decisive at the cut frontier, report the membership decision as conditional/pending rather than falsely precise.

`MISSING_DATA ≠ ZERO`
`UNKNOWN ≠ NEGATIVE_FACT`
