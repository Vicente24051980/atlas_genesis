# WORKFLOW PENETRATION OMEGA

**Status:** CANONICAL / ACTIVE / TRANSVERSAL  
**Effective:** 2026-08-28  
**Parent:** ATLAS Omega v4.0 / AI Value Distribution Omega  

## Purpose

Measure whether AI moves from availability and demos into economically valuable customer workflows, and identify which public company captures the resulting value.

ATLAS must distinguish AI adoption from AI monetization and durable shareholder economics.

## Workflow ladder

`W0_DEMO -> W1_TRIAL -> W2_RECURRENT_USE -> W3_WORKFLOW_INTEGRATION -> W4_MEASURED_PRODUCTIVITY -> W5_PAID_EXPANSION -> W6_PROVIDER_FCF_CAPTURE`

### W0 — DEMO
Prototype, announcement, demo, benchmark or isolated experiment. No adoption proof.

### W1 — TRIAL
Real users/customers test the AI capability. Adoption signal only.

### W2 — RECURRENT USE
Persistent/repeated use is evidenced. Still not proof of economic value.

### W3 — WORKFLOW INTEGRATION
AI is embedded in a recurring business process, system of record, operational decision or production workflow.

### W4 — MEASURED PRODUCTIVITY
Verified evidence of time reduction, throughput improvement, conversion improvement, lower error rate, lower unit cost, higher output or other measurable customer economics.

### W5 — PAID EXPANSION
Customer expands seats, consumption, modules, contract value or willingness to pay because of the AI-enabled workflow.

### W6 — PROVIDER FCF CAPTURE
The supplier demonstrates incremental revenue/margin economics that convert into incremental FCF and, ultimately, durable incremental ROIC.

## Workflow Penetration Score

Score 0-100 using:

- Workflow Depth: 25%
- Measured Productivity: 20%
- Paid Expansion / Retention: 20%
- Pricing Power / Monetization: 15%
- FCF Conversion: 20%

`WPS = 0.25*WD + 0.20*MP + 0.20*ER + 0.15*PP + 0.20*FCF`

Every component must be evidence-backed. Missing evidence is UNKNOWN, never silently imputed as positive.

## AI Narrative Penalty

Apply a negative adjustment when AI claims materially exceed verified workflow/economic proof.

Examples triggering penalty:
- AI mentions without customer evidence
- pilots presented as deployment
- user counts without recurrent-use evidence
- token/prompt growth without revenue linkage
- productivity claims without measurement methodology
- partnerships without deployment/utilization
- revenue claims without margin/FCF conversion

Narrative Penalty cannot create a SELL signal by itself; it reduces conviction and Expected Return confidence.

## Economic chain

Required causal chain:

`CAPABILITY -> TRIAL -> RECURRENT USE -> WORKFLOW INTEGRATION -> CUSTOMER PRODUCTIVITY -> PAID EXPANSION -> PROVIDER REVENUE -> MARGIN -> FCF -> INCREMENTAL ROIC`

A break in the chain must be explicitly marked.

## Evidence hierarchy

Highest conviction:
1. audited/company financial disclosures with quantified economics
2. customer disclosures with measured outcomes
3. contracts/renewals/seat or consumption expansion
4. credible independent datasets
5. management qualitative commentary
6. newsletters/social/anecdotes

Newsletter anecdotes are DISCOVERY/HYPOTHESIS only and cannot establish W4-W6 without stronger evidence.

## AI Value Capture ex-CAPEX radar

Prioritize discovery of companies controlling economically critical workflows with:

`PROPRIETARY_DATA + WORKFLOW_DEPTH + DISTRIBUTION + SWITCHING_COSTS + PRICING_POWER + FCF_CONVERSION`

Target domains include vertical software, legal, accounting, payroll, financial information, healthcare workflows, industrial software, cybersecurity and supply chain.

This radar must evaluate existing ATLAS candidates including, where relevant, SPGI, ADP, ROP, MANH, INTU, ICE, MSFT, GOOG, META and SNPS without granting automatic portfolio status.

## Integration with AI Value Distribution Omega

Workflow Penetration Omega extends the canonical proof states:

`T0_NARRATIVE -> T1_ADOPTION -> T2_REVENUE_LINKAGE -> T3_MARGIN_LINKAGE -> T4_FCF_PROOF -> T5_DURABLE_ROIC`

W-states describe workflow penetration; T-states describe investor economic proof. They are complementary, not interchangeable.

Indicative mapping only:
- W0 ~ T0
- W1/W2 support T1
- W3 is necessary but insufficient for T2+
- W4 strengthens revenue/margin causality
- W5 supports T2/T3
- W6 requires T4 evidence and may progress to T5 only with durable incremental ROIC

## Competition for Capital

A high WPS does not authorize purchase. It may increase conviction only after Valuation, Expected Return, Falsifier Veto, Portfolio Integrity, correlation/concentration and Competition for Capital.

The relevant investment question is not `Who uses AI?` but:

`Who controls the workflow, creates measurable customer value, captures the economics, converts them to FCF, and can reinvest at durable incremental ROIC?`

## Authority limits

This module has no autonomous BUY/SELL authority and cannot override portfolio constraints, AI exposure limits, Decision Safety Gate or verified evidence requirements.

Default when evidence is incomplete: `DISCOVERY / WATCH / WAIT FOR PROOF / NO PORTFOLIO CHANGE`.
