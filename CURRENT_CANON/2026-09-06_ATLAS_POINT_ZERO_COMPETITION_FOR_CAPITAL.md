# ATLAS Ω — COMPETITION FOR CAPITAL LAW

Passing quality, growth, valuation and hard gates makes a company eligible to compete; it does not guarantee a portfolio slot.

Finalists must compete for scarce capital against:

- economically comparable peers;
- alternative beneficiaries/captors of the same economic pool;
- the strongest cross-sector alternatives;
- the existing selected set through marginal portfolio utility.

A selected company should be removable when a challenger produces a materially superior clean portfolio outcome under the same evidence standard.

No incumbent protection applies during this comparison.
