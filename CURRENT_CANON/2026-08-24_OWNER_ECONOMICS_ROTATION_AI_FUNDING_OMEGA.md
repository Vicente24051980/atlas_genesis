## Surviving economic principles

- Current earnings, normalized earnings, durable earnings and owner earnings are distinct.
- High reported ROIC is not automatically high incremental ROIC.
- Low P/E at peak earnings is not automatically cheap.
- Price receiver, flow receiver, capital receiver and attractive investment are distinct states.
- AI demand, AI CAPEX, AI revenue, AI FCF and AI owner return are distinct.
- Sector-specific economics must be used where generic industrial FCF/ROIC is inappropriate.
- Evidence must not be double-counted across engines.
