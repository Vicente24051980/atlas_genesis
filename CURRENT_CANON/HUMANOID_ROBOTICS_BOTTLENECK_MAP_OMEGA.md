# Humanoid Robotics Bottleneck Map Ω v1.0

**Status:** ACTIVE / GLOBAL DISCOVERY SPECIALIST MODULE  
**Effective date:** 2026-08-22  
**Authority:** compatible canonical module under ATLAS Ω ENTERPRISE v3.1; subordinate to Evidence Integrity Ω, Primary Source Gate Ω and Humanoid Proof Ladder Ω.

## Mission

Map the scarce components, qualification bottlenecks and economic toll roads required to scale humanoid robotics, separating technology narrative from deployment proof and owner economics.

## Structural chain

**Humanoid OEM → actuators → precision reducers/gears → motors → force/torque sensors → encoders/control electronics → bearings/linear motion → memory/compute → power/battery → manufacturing automation → deployed robots → utilization → customer acceptance → revenue → margin → FCF/ROIC.**

## Constitutional laws

1. **ROBOT DEMO ≠ DEPLOYMENT PROOF.**
2. **SHIPMENTS CLAIM ≠ VERIFIED MARKET SHARE.**
3. **CHINA MANUFACTURING ADVANTAGE ≠ INVESTABLE TICKER.**
4. **BOTTLENECK NARRATIVE ≠ ECONOMIC PROOF.**
5. **SCARCITY ≠ PERMANENT MOAT.** Qualification, substitution and capacity expansion must be tested.
6. **COMPONENT CONTENT ≠ VALUE CAPTURE.** Revenue, margin, FCF/share and incremental ROIC must be traced.
7. All third-party market-share claims, including claims that China represents >80% of humanoid shipments, remain `VERIFY` until the source methodology and denominator are validated.
8. Every public-equity candidate must pass Trading 212 Accessibility Gate Ω before becoming executable.
9. This module discovers candidates; it cannot issue BUY/SELL orders by itself.

## Bottleneck families

- `ACTUATORS`: electromechanical / integrated actuation systems.
- `PRECISION_REDUCERS`: harmonic/cycloidal/planetary reduction and precision gearing.
- `FORCE_TORQUE_SENSING`: joint/load/force-torque sensing.
- `ENCODERS_CONTROL`: encoders, servo drives, motor controllers, control ICs.
- `MOTORS`: high-torque-density motors and winding/magnet dependencies.
- `BEARINGS_LINEAR_MOTION`: bearings, ball screws, linear guides and motion components.
- `MEMORY_COMPUTE`: edge compute, accelerators, DRAM/NAND and connectivity.
- `POWER_BATTERY`: cells, BMS, power semiconductors and charging.
- `MANUFACTURING_TEST`: automation, precision machining, metrology and test.

## Bottleneck Score Ω — 0 to 100

Suggested deterministic dimensions:

- scarcity / market structure: 20%
- qualification / switching cost: 20%
- performance criticality: 15%
- replication lead time: 15%
- capacity expansion difficulty: 10%
- gross-margin / pricing evidence: 10%
- customer diversification: 5%
- substitution resistance: 5%

The score is a research-priority measure, not a valuation or BUY score.

## Economic Proof ladder

- `RB0_NARRATIVE`: thematic exposure only.
- `RB1_TECHNICAL_RELEVANCE`: component is technically relevant, no commercial proof.
- `RB2_DESIGN_WIN_ORDER`: design win, order, qualification or traceable commercial commitment.
- `RB3_REVENUE_MARGIN`: robotics demand visibly contributes to reported revenue/margin.
- `RB4_FCF_ROIC`: multi-period translation into FCF/share or incremental ROIC.

Only RB2+ may be called a commercially evidenced robotics beneficiary.

## Primary Source Gate Ω

Preferred evidence:

- issuer filings and IR material
- earnings calls
- OEM supplier disclosures where public
- regulator/exchange filings
- customer acceptance / deployment disclosures
- audited segment revenue and margin

Media, YouTube, sell-side and industry reports enter as `DISCOVERY_SIGNAL`; they do not directly alter Economic Proof.

## China industrial advantage handling

Treat China's scale, supply-chain density, automation ecosystem and manufacturing cost advantages as a **structural discovery prior**, not a numeric market-share fact unless independently verified. ATLAS should search both Chinese and non-Chinese suppliers while keeping geopolitical/listing/accessibility risks separate.

## Initial hypothesis — 22 Aug 2026

The initial discovery map prioritizes actuators, precision reducers/gears, force/torque sensors, motors, bearings/linear motion, memory/compute and power electronics. No company is promoted solely because it appears in a humanoid supply-chain narrative.

## Final law

**Do not chase the robot brand. Find the component that every scaled robot must repeatedly buy, prove that scarcity survives qualification and capacity expansion, and then prove that the supplier converts that demand into FCF/share.**
