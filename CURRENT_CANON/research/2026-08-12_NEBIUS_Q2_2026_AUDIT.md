## Primary-source evidence — Nebius shareholder letter Q2 2026
- Group revenue: $582.3M, +454% YoY and +46% QoQ.
- Nebius AI cloud revenue: $574.9M, +514% YoY, ~98% of group revenue.
- ARR: $3.0B at June-end, +598% YoY and +56% vs Q1. ARR is annualized last-month revenue, not contracted backlog.
- AI cloud adjusted EBITDA: $285.7M; margin 49.7% (~50%). Group adjusted EBITDA: $236.2M, 41% margin.
- Revenue growth was driven by capacity scaling, higher pricing and improved utilization.
- Four landmark AI-cloud deals were signed in Q2 with average TCV >$1B each.
- Annual contract value per MW on Q2 core AI-cloud deals exceeded $20M; older-generation GPU pricing was >30% higher vs Q1.
- TCV of Q2 deals grew nearly 4x QoQ; TCV from new customers grew >9x.
- Roughly 70% of deals closed included customer prepayments.
- Management expects >$9B in customer prepayments in 2026.
- Management estimates Q2 deal payback at 1 year 10 months, versus historical 2–3 years. This is forecast-based and includes capacity not yet built; it is not realized ROIC.
- Majority of Q2 deals are tied to capacity arriving late 2026 and should contribute primarily to 2027 revenue.
- Contracted power target for year-end 2026 was raised to 5 GW; company plans to deploy >1 GW per year starting in 2027.
- Token Factory production inference workloads increased >3x in Q2.
- Q2 capex was approximately $5.7B, mainly GPUs/GPU-related hardware and data-center expansion.
- Q2 ending cash: ~$8B, including ~$2.3B positive operating cash flow.
- July asset-backed financing: ~$775M at SOFR + 2.50%, backed by deployed GPU infrastructure and contracted cash flows.
- Company introduced an asset-light partnership model intended to reduce capital burden for incremental capacity.
- 2026 full-year guidance was reiterated across all metrics, not raised.

## Important correction vs secondary reports
Some secondary commentary described a guidance raise. The Q2 shareholder letter explicitly states that 2026 full-year guidance was reaffirmed/reiterated across all metrics. The capacity target was raised to 5 GW, but full-year financial guidance was not raised in the primary source.

## AI CAPEX PAYBACK Ω
Status: GREEN / Productive Investment candidate.

Positive evidence:
1. Monetization vs CAPEX: very strong revenue acceleration and increasing ACV/MW.
2. FCF/financing quality: large customer prepayments and positive operating cash flow reduce reliance on external financing.
3. Incremental economics: estimated deal payback shortened to 1y10m.
4. Demand/backlog/utilization: four >$1B average TCV wins, improved utilization, >$40B customer commitments cited by management, capacity visibility into 2027.
5. Balance/funding: ~$8B cash plus secured asset-backed financing.
6. Inference: production inference workloads >3x in Q2, supporting broader utilization beyond training.

Open risks / falsifiers:
- Estimated payback is management forecast, not realized return.
- Capex remains extremely high: ~$5.7B in Q2 alone.
- D&A reached $259.7M in Q2 and is rising with GPU infrastructure expansion.
- Server/network useful life is now five years vs four before 2026; monitor accounting-life assumptions versus technological obsolescence.
- Large contracts are concentrated and depend on timely delivery of late-2026/2027 capacity.
- Need realized FCF, incremental ROIC and utilization data as new capacity comes online.
- Funding still may include additional debt/capital-markets transactions.
