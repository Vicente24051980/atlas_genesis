- US500 loses the active structural support zone with persistence;

## 4. Expectations Compression Ω

New diagnostic for cases where economic proof is positive but price falls sharply.

Purpose: prevent ATLAS from treating good results as automatic price protection.

Inputs:
- reported growth vs consensus;
- guidance vs consensus;
- pre-event implied expectations;
- valuation/multiple;
- positioning/crowding;
- yields/discount-rate move;
- post-event relative strength.

Classification:
E0 NORMAL
E1 HIGH EXPECTATIONS
E2 EXPECTATION COMPRESSION
E3 CROWDED UNWIND
E4 FUNDAMENTAL BREAK

Rule: `Economic Proof positive != stock protected from expectations compression`.

Use SNDK/WDC 2026-08 as calibration examples, not permanent signals.

## 8. Physical Metal Friction Ω

New execution layer; NOT a directional XAUUSD predictor.

Net physical acquisition economics:
`spot + dealer premium + sales tax/VAT + shipping + custody/storage + insurance + bid/ask/rebuy friction + capital-gains treatment`.

Track jurisdiction and product format. Small pieces can improve divisibility but often raise premium per gram.

Hard rule: US state sales-tax distortions affect physical-market execution/channel choice; do not elevate them to a major global gold-price driver without evidence.

For Spain distinguish qualifying investment gold VAT treatment from silver/collectibles; refresh tax law before execution advice.

## 9. Central Bank Gold Diversification Ω

Preferred label: `CENTRAL_BANK_GOLD_DIVERSIFICATION`, not blanket `BRICS_DEDOLLARIZATION_BUYING`.

Track bank-by-bank net purchases/sales, reserve share, sanctions/geopolitical incentives, FX reserve diversification and persistence.

## 10. AI Productivity Transmission Ω

New macro transmission branch attached to AI CAPEX PAYBACK Ω.

Pipeline:
`AI adoption -> task productivity -> firm productivity -> margins/revenue per employee -> sector productivity -> aggregate productivity -> potential GDP -> unit labor costs -> inflation transmission -> neutral rate / monetary policy`.

Evidence states:
- P0 MICRO PROOF: task-level productivity demonstrated.
- P1 CORPORATE PROOF: persistent effect visible in margins, revenue/employee, OPEX and/or FCF.
- P2 MACRO PROOF: persistent aggregate productivity/TFP acceleration plus compatible unit-labor-cost/inflation evidence.

Current canonical interpretation from this thread: `P1 emerging; P2 NOT CONFIRMED`.

## 11. AI inflation semantics

Do not encode `AI = deflation` as fact.
Use `AI = potential structural disinflation` as a hypothesis subject to transmission evidence.

Transition can be inflationary because investment, power, construction, financing and income effects can precede supply/productivity gains.

Therefore monetary-policy judgment must incorporate observed inflation, expectations, wages/unit labor costs, energy, productivity and financial conditions. Future AI productivity alone cannot falsify restrictive policy today.

## 12. Valuation doctrine extension

Never accept `AI changes everything` as a valuation exemption.
