## Purpose

**VC FUNDING ≠ PUBLIC EQUITY FLOW ≠ REVENUE ≠ FCF ≠ RETURN.**

## Promotion gate for listed equities
A candidate may only advance when evidence supports:

**CAPITAL → DEMAND → FUNDAMENTALS → FLOW → PRICE**

Required gates:
1. Customer Acceptance.
2. Orders/revenue/backlog attributable to the theme.
3. Margin quality and credible FCF conversion.
4. GREEN continuity / fundamental quality.
5. Valuation and Expected CAGR remain attractive after price reaction.
6. Public-equity breadth/flow confirms monetization rather than narrative-only repricing.

Forbidden shortcut:

**VC MONEY → NARRATIVE → PRICE** is not investment proof.

## Private-to-Public Transmission Ω
Track whether private funding migrates into listed suppliers through:
- incremental orders/backlog,
- capacity utilization and production,
- revenue revisions,
- margin/FCF revisions,
- multi-name breadth,
- relative strength and persistent public-equity flow.

A single ticker rally is insufficient.

## Concentration adjustment
Headline funding must be concentration-adjusted. Track at minimum:
- top-1 share,
- top-4 share,
- median/typical deal size where available,
- deal-count growth,
- funding excluding megadeals.

High dollars + weak ex-megadeal breadth = **CAPITAL FORMATION CONFIRMED, ECOSYSTEM BREADTH UNCONFIRMED**.
