## E. Thesis registry — ACCEPTED STRUCTURAL

### Leadership Transition Ω
`Mag-7 monopoly ↓` does not equal `AI leadership dead`. Current working hypothesis: leadership broadens through memory, networking, power, industrials, international markets and new platforms.

### Successor Migration Ω
Preferred signature:
`Rank ↑ + revisions ↑ + FCF ↑ + ROIC ↑ + TAM ↑ + sponsorship ↑ + index migration`.

### AI Infrastructure Financialization Ω
AI infrastructure is becoming a credit/infrastructure-finance asset class. This can extend the cycle while increasing reflexive financing risk.

### Semiconductor Sovereignty Ω
Advanced compute/foundry/memory/packaging/test/power increasingly behave as strategic infrastructure, not only cyclical technology.

### Agentic Compute Expansion Ω
Agentic AI can increase CPU/general-purpose orchestration around accelerators. This is horizontal TAM expansion, not proof of GPU displacement.

### Power/Grid Structural Bottleneck Ω
AI/data centers, electrification, European energy security and industrial reshoring can converge on electricity generation/transmission/grid equipment as a common bottleneck.

### Financial Toll-Roads Ω
Payments, exchanges, clearing, ratings, benchmarks and data can attract quality capital because they monetize economic activity without requiring the investor to predict one end-market winner.

### China Industrial Displacement Ω
China can lose financial inflows while gaining global industrial share. Industrial competitiveness and capital-market attractiveness are separate variables.

### US Capital Magnet Ω
Dollar reserve share can erode gradually while US capital markets remain the primary global liquidity/collateral destination. Track functions separately: reserves, trade invoicing, safe assets, collateral, private capital flows.
