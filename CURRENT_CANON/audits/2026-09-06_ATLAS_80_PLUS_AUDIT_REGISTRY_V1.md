## Purpose

Every ATLAS name with a previously recorded Opportunity/Composite score >=80 must enter a formal re-audit queue before that score is treated as current.

This registry does **not** claim that the 490-name universe has already been scored homogeneously 490/490 as of 2026-09-06. It consolidates the >=80 names already scored by prior ATLAS runs and revalidates them against the 2026-09-06 evidence cut. A later full-universe scoring pass may add more names.

## Canonical rules

1. Historical score != current score.
2. Score provenance is mandatory.
3. Latest comparable ATLAS score supersedes older score values, but not older evidence history.
4. Fresh evidence must be checked against primary company IR/filings where available.
5. Valuation must be refreshed separately; strong operating results do not automatically preserve an >=80 score.
6. Authority/guru ownership contributes 0 points.
7. Duplicate share classes are one economic entity for business scoring (e.g. GOOGL/GOOG), while instrument choice remains separate.
8. 80+ status means AUDIT PRIORITY, not automatic portfolio membership.
