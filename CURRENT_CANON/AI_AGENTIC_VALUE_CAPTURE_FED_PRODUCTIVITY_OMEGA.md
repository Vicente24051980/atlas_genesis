## Purpose

Operationalize five research branches without allowing narrative, adoption, CAPEX, macro speeches, or AI capability to become portfolio actions without verified economic proof.

## 1 — AI Agentic Economy Ω

Track the economic conversion chain:

Agent capability → successful task completion → deployment → paid usage → workflow penetration → retention → incremental revenue/ARPU → margin → FCF/share → ROIC.

Required fields: incremental AI revenue, pricing/ARPU, paid seats or consumption, workflow penetration, retention/NRR where applicable, gross-margin effect, incremental FCF, ROIC, switching costs, source quality.

States:
- T0_NARRATIVE
- T1_CAPABILITY
- T2_DEPLOYMENT
- T3_PAID_ADOPTION
- T4_FCF_CAPTURE
- T5_DURABLE_ROIC

Law: ADOPTION ≠ MONETIZATION ≠ OWNER ECONOMICS.

## 2 — AI Application vs Infrastructure Ω

Compare economic capture across COMPUTE, SEMICAP, NETWORKING, INTERCONNECT, POWER/DC, DATA_STATE, CYBER/IDENTITY, OBSERVABILITY, PLATFORM/DISTRIBUTION and APPLICATIONS.

Mandatory comparison: revenue growth, revisions, gross/operating margin, FCF margin, FCF/share, ROIC, CAPEX intensity, SBC/dilution, valuation, normalized Expected CAGR and persistence.

A layer can be declared VALUE_MIGRATION_CONFIRMED only when verified economics improve relative to competing layers; market-price momentum alone is insufficient.

Law: AI VALUE MIGRATION ≠ STOCK PRICE MIGRATION.

## 3 — Distribution Control Ω

Measure control of the final enterprise/consumer access point.

Inputs: installed base, default placement, workflow ownership, developer ecosystem, switching cost, cross-product distribution, monetization evidence, regulatory/platform dependency.

States: WEAK_GATE, ACCESS_GATE, DISTRIBUTION_GATE, MONETIZED_GATE, DURABLE_CAPTURE.

Distribution power cannot override valuation, antitrust/regulatory risk, FCF or ROIC gates.

Law: DISTRIBUTION ≠ MONETIZATION.

## 4 — AI Energy Constraint Ω

Audit whether AI physical demand becomes durable owner economics across grid, electrical equipment, power generation, data-center construction, cooling and related infrastructure.

Evidence chain:

AI/DC demand → contracted backlog/orders → capacity/utilization → pricing/margins → cash conversion → FCF/share → ROIC.

Required falsifiers: cancellation/deferment, customer concentration, overbuild, financing stress, power bottlenecks that prevent revenue recognition, margin compression and excessive valuation.

Law: POWER BOTTLENECK ≠ INVESTMENT RETURN.

## 5 — Fed × AI Productivity Ω

Separate monetary-policy restriction from structural productivity.

Inputs: core inflation trend, inflation expectations, labor conditions, activity indicators, 2Y/10Y/30Y Treasury response, real yields, USD, verified productivity data, AI investment/productivity evidence.

Regimes:
- PRODUCTIVITY_DISINFLATION: productivity evidence improves while inflation/long yields ease.
- HIGHER_FOR_LONGER_PRODUCTIVITY: growth/productivity resilient but inflation keeps policy restrictive.
- STAGFLATION_RISK: activity/labor deteriorate while inflation remains persistent.
- LONG_END_DEANCHORING: long yields/inflation expectations rise independently of front-end repricing.
- UNRESOLVED: evidence insufficient or contradictory.

A central-bank speech is policy evidence, not proof of realized AI productivity. One PMI, one payroll revision, one yield session or one speech cannot independently trigger a regime change.

Law: AI PRODUCTIVITY NARRATIVE ≠ REALIZED PRODUCTIVITY.
