# SYSTEMIC CASCADE Ω v1.0

**Status:** CANONICAL CROSS-ENGINE RISK GATE  
**Date:** 2026-08-16

## Mission
Detect when independent economic, credit, market-liquidity and sovereign-plumbing stresses begin to reinforce one another. It is a regime-escalation gate, not a crash predictor.

## AI / corporate-credit leg
`utilization ↓ → AI revenue revisions ↓ → FCF ↓ → collateral/residual values ↓ → LTV ↑ → credit spreads/refinancing cost ↑ → financing availability ↓ → CAPEX/orders ↓ → supplier earnings revisions ↓`

## Sovereign / liquidity leg
`fiscal/energy stress ↑ → long yields/term premium ↑ → FX/carry stress ↑ → foreign USD liquidity demand ↑ → Treasury market stress ↑ → credit spreads ↑ → forced deleveraging`

## Confirmation channels
1. Corporate fundamentals/revisions
2. AI utilization/pricing/payback
3. Private credit/collateral/refinancing
4. Public credit/HY/IG/CDS
5. Treasury term premium/auction quality
6. FX/carry/cross-currency basis
7. Equity breadth/market structure
8. Speculative Liquidity Canary
9. Gold/safe-haven confirmation
10. Sovereign/European fragmentation
11. Margin/leverage liquidation evidence
12. Global earnings revisions

## Escalation
- 1–3 independent signals: `WATCH`
- 4–6: `RISK_REDUCTION_REVIEW`
- 7–9: `REGIME_CHANGE_REVIEW`
- 10+ across economic + credit + sovereign channels: `SYSTEMIC_DELEVERAGING_RISK`

A count alone is insufficient: signals must be temporally aligned and not double-count the same source/event.

## De-escalation
State falls when credit/refinancing normalizes, breadth stabilizes, revisions recover, auctions/funding improve or the causal chain fails to propagate.

## Laws
- No single market drawdown activates systemic status.
- Korea/2000/2008 analogies are priors, not evidence.
- A systemic-risk state cannot create a structural SELL without Decision Safety Gate and company/portfolio impact analysis.
- Strong fundamentals can coexist with temporary deleveraging.

## Output
`state / active_channels / evidence_ids / first_detection / persistence / affected_engines / portfolio_review_required / confidence`.
