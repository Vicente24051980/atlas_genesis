# ATLAS Ω — CROSS-SECTOR CAPITAL DUEL RULE

Sector peer comparison is necessary but insufficient.

A finalist that wins its industry must still compete against the best opportunity in unrelated industries because the scarce resource is portfolio capital, not sector slots.

Cross-sector duels compare expected compound return, valuation, durability, permanent-loss/fragility risk, confidence and portfolio interaction.

No sector receives a reserved slot.
