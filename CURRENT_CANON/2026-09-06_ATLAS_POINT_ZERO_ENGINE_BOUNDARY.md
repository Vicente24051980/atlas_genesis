# ATLAS Ω — ENGINE SOVEREIGNTY BOUNDARY

No analytical engine is sovereign over portfolio membership.

An engine can produce one or more of: discovery candidate, component score, evidence, diagnostic state, scenario exposure, falsifier, risk flag, monitoring state or execution input.

An engine cannot by itself:

- emit a canonical BUY/SELL that bypasses master selection;
- admit or exclude a ticker contrary to hard gates/Competition for Capital;
- protect an incumbent;
- force portfolio cardinality;
- create a sector/factor quota;
- convert sponsorship, tape, price action or narrative into fundamental proof;
- bypass valuation or Expected Return.

Final membership belongs to the Point Zero master selection process.
