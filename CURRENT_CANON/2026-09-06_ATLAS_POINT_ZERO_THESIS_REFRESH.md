# ATLAS Ω — THESIS REFRESH AT POINT ZERO

A historical thesis may contribute sourced facts or hypotheses, but the rebuild must reconstruct the current thesis from refreshed evidence.

Required refresh questions:

- Is the moat still present?
- Are growth/revisions still favorable?
- Has valuation changed the Expected Return?
- Has financing or balance-sheet quality changed?
- Have competitors altered the opportunity?
- Has any prior falsifier triggered?
- Does the thesis still beat the best alternative for scarce capital?

A prior positive thesis cannot be carried forward by default.
