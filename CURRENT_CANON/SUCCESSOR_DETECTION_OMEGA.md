# SUCCESSOR DETECTION Ω v1.1

**Status:** CANONICAL DISCOVERY / LEADERSHIP ENGINE  
**Date:** 2026-08-16  
**Portfolio authority:** NONE

## Mission
Detect companies changing category before they become obvious leaders. The engine is designed to discover future global leaders and multibaggers without confusing price momentum with economic proof.

## Two separate objectives
1. **MEGACAP_SUCCESSOR Ω** — probability that a company can migrate into global Top 10–20 scale.
2. **EMERGING_LEADER / MULTIBAGGER Ω** — probability of extraordinary percentage return from a smaller starting base.

These are not the same problem and must never share one ranking without labels.

## Canonical score
- Revenue/EPS revisions + acceleration: 20%
- Rank migration / market-cap ladder: 15%
- FCF + incremental ROIC: 15%
- TAM expansion + new adjacencies: 15%
- Moat / critical bottleneck: 10%
- Institutional sponsorship / breadth / flows: 10%
- Valuation + crowding + execution risk: 10%
- Index migration / passive eligibility: 5%

## Mandatory internal gates

### Rank Velocity Ω
Track:
- ΔRank 3M
- ΔRank 6M
- ΔRank 12M
- acceleration/deceleration of rank migration

A one-off price spike cannot substitute for persistent rank migration.

### Economic Proof Gate Ω
Requires, when applicable:
- incremental ROIC;
- FCF/share;
- payback;
- operating leverage;
- market-share gains;
- customer concentration;
- dilution/SBC;
- debt/financing dependence.

### Through-Cycle Normalization Ω
Mandatory for memory, commodities, energy, cyclicals and any business with extraordinary peak pricing.

Compare:
- current earnings vs normalized cycle earnings;
- current FCF vs through-cycle FCF;
- market cap vs normalized economics.

## States
- `<60 WATCH`
- `60–69 EMERGING`
- `70–79 CONSOLIDATED_CHALLENGER`
- `80–89 SUCCESSOR_CANDIDATE`
- `90+ GENERATIONAL_LEADER_WATCH`

Scores are heuristics, not probabilities.

## Index migration law
S&P/Nasdaq/index inclusion is evidence of scale/liquidity/passive eligibility, not a BUY signal.

## Sponsorship law
13F/manager convergence can raise sponsorship confidence, but delayed ownership disclosure is not current flow.

## Winner Preservation integration
When a Successor Candidate becomes 2x/5x, do not mechanically take profit. Re-audit economic proof, valuation and runway. Price appreciation alone is neither confirmation nor falsifier.

## Falsifiers
- rank migration reverses while fundamentals deteriorate;
- EPS/revenue revisions turn persistently negative;
- FCF/share/ROIC fail to scale;
- market share/moat deteriorates;
- financing dependence overwhelms economics;
- valuation requires implausible terminal outcomes;
- through-cycle normalization reveals peak-cycle illusion.

## Operating cadence
Monthly snapshot, quarterly primary-evidence reset, event-driven update after material earnings/index/capital-allocation changes.

Each snapshot stores rank, ΔRank 3/6/12M, score components, evidence IDs, confidence, falsifiers and prior-state delta. Never rewrite old snapshots retrospectively.
