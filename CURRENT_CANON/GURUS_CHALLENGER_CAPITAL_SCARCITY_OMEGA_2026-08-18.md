## 2. CAPITAL SCARCITY Ω
New cross-sectional overlay for a regime in which long sovereign yields and private/public capital demand remain structurally elevated.

Core question: can the company grow and compound when capital is expensive?

Positive factors:
- high FCF conversion
- high incremental ROIC
- low incremental capital required for growth
- internal funding capacity
- pricing power
- strong balance sheet
- low refinancing dependence
- short/medium duration of equity cash flows

Negative factors:
- debt-funded CAPEX dependency
- weak or distant monetisation
- large refinancing needs
- low incremental ROIC
- valuation requiring near-perfect long-duration growth

Capital Scarcity Ω does not mechanically reject CAPEX-heavy companies. CAPEX is acceptable when Economic Proof and payback are demonstrated.
