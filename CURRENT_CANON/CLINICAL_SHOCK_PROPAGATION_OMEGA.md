# ATLAS Ω — Clinical Shock Propagation Ω

Status: ACTIVE CANON
Effective: 2026-08-22
Parent engine: Clinical Evidence Shock Ω
Authority: subordinate discovery/propagation module; no automatic BUY authority.

## Objective
Detect which listed companies retain economic and market benefit after a major clinical event reprices the primary issuer and spills into adjacent healthcare names.

## Constitutional rule
A clinical headline can create three distinct effects that must never be conflated:
1. PRIMARY EVIDENCE SHOCK — direct change in probability-weighted value of the drug/platform owner.
2. PARTNER ECONOMICS — direct contractual/economic exposure of a partner, royalty owner, manufacturer or commercial participant.
3. SYMPATHY / PROPAGATION — sector or platform repricing without yet-proven company-specific economic delta.

Price propagation is Money Rotation evidence, not Economic Proof.

## Pipeline
Primary clinical event → direct ownership/partner map → mechanism/platform map → peer/sympathy universe → 1D/3D/5D relative-strength persistence → estimate/revision check → company-specific Economic Proof → valuation → falsifiers → action.

## Propagation classes
- P0 NONE: no material causal link.
- P1 SYMPATHY: price/breadth response only.
- P2 PLATFORM READ-THROUGH: event increases credibility of a modality/platform class.
- P3 COMMERCIAL READ-THROUGH: plausible impact on addressable market, utilization, diagnostics, procedures or adjacent demand.
- P4 DIRECT ECONOMIC LINK: contractual economics, revenue share, royalty, manufacturing, distribution or owned asset exposure.

P1/P2 can create WATCH/AUDIT priority only. They cannot create BUY without company-specific Economic Proof and valuation support.

## Persistence test
For every propagated ticker record:
- event-day abnormal return vs sector and market;
- 3D cumulative relative return;
- 5D cumulative relative return;
- abnormal volume where available;
- breadth of the relevant peer basket;
- analyst EPS/revenue/target revisions after the event;
- whether the move survives after the headline decays.

State labels:
- FLASH: one-session move only.
- PERSISTING: relative strength survives 3D.
- CONFIRMED RECEIVER: survives 5D and has independent company-specific evidence/revisions.
- FAILED PROPAGATION: gives back the move and lacks economic linkage.

## Red-team falsifiers
Full clinical detail materially weaker than implied by topline; OS fails; safety offsets benefit; regulatory delay/label restriction; manufacturing/personalization economics impair margin; reimbursement friction; partner economics smaller than expected; sympathy names lose 3D/5D relative strength; revisions fail to follow price; valuation fully discounts the opportunity.

## Canonical principle
CLINICAL SHOCK → CAUSAL MAP → PROPAGATION → PERSISTENCE → COMPANY-SPECIFIC ECONOMIC PROOF → VALUATION.
Never reverse this chain by treating sympathy price action as evidence of fundamental benefit.
