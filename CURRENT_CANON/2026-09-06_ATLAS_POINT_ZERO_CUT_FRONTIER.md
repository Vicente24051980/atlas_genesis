# ATLAS Ω — CUT FRONTIER CONTRACT

Canonical portfolio output must expose the decision boundary.

Minimum disclosure:

- last 5 selected names;
- first 10 challengers;
- marginal portfolio utility at/around the cut;
- N→N+1 reason;
- best replacement challenge for each borderline selected name where computable;
- data/confidence caveats affecting the boundary.

The cut frontier exists to make `OPTIMAL_N` and scarce-capital decisions auditable rather than merely asserted.
