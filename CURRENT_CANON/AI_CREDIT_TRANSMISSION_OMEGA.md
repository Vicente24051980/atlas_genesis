## Mission
Map how AI infrastructure is financed and where credit/collateral/refinancing risk can amplify an otherwise healthy technology cycle.

## Canonical financing map
`buyer → lender → financing vehicle → collateral → guarantee/backstop → cash flow/debt service → refinancing`

## Vendor-financing / circular-demand extension
When a strategic supplier materially finances, guarantees, invests in, lends to, or mobilizes third-party capital for customers that purchase its own products, ATLAS must separately test whether reported demand is economically independent.

Canonical loop:

`supplier capital/backstop → customer/SPV financing → infrastructure CAPEX → supplier hardware purchase → supplier revenue → supplier cash/capital → renewed financing`

This loop is **not automatically bearish and is not a falsifier by itself**. It becomes a risk signal only when financing support grows faster than independently funded end-demand, utilization, monetization, customer FCF or debt-service capacity.

Required circularity fields:
- supplier equity/security holdings in ecosystem counterparties;
- direct supplier investment and loans;
- guarantees/backstops;
- capital mobilized through asset managers/private credit;
- customer purchases attributable to financed projects;
- end-customer revenue/utilization and cash generation;
- supplier revenue exposed to financed counterparties;
- concentration and related-party/economic-dependency mapping;
- maturity/refinancing schedule;
- primary-source verification status.

### Circularity states
- `C0_INDEPENDENT_DEMAND` — no material supplier-financing dependency observed.
- `C1_SUPPORTIVE_CAPITAL` — financing exists but demand economics remain independently demonstrated.
- `C2_REFLEXIVITY_WATCH` — supplier/partner financing is material enough to require explicit attribution.
- `C3_CIRCULARITY_RISK` — financing growth materially outpaces demonstrated end monetization/cash generation.
- `C4_DEMAND_QUALITY_BREAK` — financed demand cannot service itself without continued ecosystem support; candidate material falsifier subject to primary-source confirmation.

No transition to C3/C4 may be made from a newsletter, media headline or aggregate financing number alone. Evidence Director Ω must verify material claims against primary filings/releases/contracts where available.

## Funding concentration / outlier normalization
For venture, Physical AI, robotics, autonomous systems and other emerging-theme funding signals, headline dollars must never be treated as structural demand proof without concentration normalization.

Persist, where data permit:
- `headline_funding`;
- `largest_deal` and `largest_deal_share`;
- `funding_ex_largest`;
- `funding_ex_top_3`;
- `deal_count`;
- `deal_count_growth_yoy`;
- `mean_deal_size`;
- `median_deal_size` when available;
- `funding_growth_yoy_headline`;
- `funding_growth_yoy_ex_outliers`;
- sector/subsector concentration.

Interpretation rule:
`capital growth ≠ breadth growth ≠ economic demand ≠ listed-company capture`.

A funding surge dominated by one/few transactions is tagged `CONCENTRATED_SIGNAL`; it can seed Discovery Ω but cannot by itself upgrade Economic Proof Ω or generate BUY.

## Credit Transmission Score components
- 20% internal-cash-flow funding quality
- 15% debt-service coverage
- 15% collateral/residual-value robustness
- 15% refinancing dependency
- 10% guarantee/backstop quality
- 10% customer/counterparty concentration
- 10% financing cost/spread trend
- 5% structural transparency / off-balance-sheet risk

Higher score = healthier financing transmission. Score does not equal credit rating.

## Reflexivity loop
Positive:
`financing available → capacity bought → utilization/revenue ↑ → collateral confidence ↑ → financing remains available`.

Negative:
`utilization/pricing ↓ → residual value ↓ → LTV/spreads ↑ → refinancing ↓ → orders/CAPEX ↓`.

## NVIDIA / strategic-supplier checkpoint protocol
For NVDA and any analogous strategic supplier, earnings/checkpoint analysis must explicitly separate:
1. organic/unassisted demand evidence;
2. demand associated with supplier-supported financing or investments;
3. customer utilization and monetization;
4. customer FCF/debt-service evidence;
5. supplier balance-sheet exposure to ecosystem counterparties;
6. whether financing intensity is accelerating faster than end economics.

The output is a `Circularity Ω` state plus evidence confidence. A C1/C2 state does not invalidate Economic Proof. C3 reduces confidence and requires Red Team escalation. Confirmed C4 enters Falsifiers Ω as a potential material veto.

## Laws
- Private credit growth is not itself bearish.
- Tangible infrastructure is not automatically safe collateral.
- Vendor/backstop support must be treated as economic exposure when material.
- Supplier financing is not automatically circular or fraudulent; economic dependency must be demonstrated.
- Headline funding is not breadth and not demand proof.
- 'AI subprime' is a stress-test analogy, not a factual classification.
- Undefined GPU-utilization claims remain UNVERIFIED.
