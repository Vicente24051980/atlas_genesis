# ATLAS Ω — ENDOGENOUS-N PORTFOLIO UTILITY NOTES

`OPTIMAL_N` is meaningful only if portfolio utility does not mechanically reward or punish cardinality for cosmetic reasons.

Canonical requirements:

- Expected Return must be evaluated consistently across N.
- Risk penalties must correspond to economic risk, not sector-count aesthetics.
- Complexity may be modeled only if it represents a real implementation/monitoring cost and must not be tuned to force a preferred N.
- Correlated financing/factor exposure may be penalized only as empirically modeled common risk.
- Confidence/uncertainty may reduce utility where data are missing or stale, but cannot be used as a familiarity bonus for heavily covered companies.
- The N→N+1 decision must be auditable with the incremental change in expected return, risk, fragility, correlation, convexity/robustness where legitimate, uncertainty and complexity.
- `reasonNPlusOne` should explain the economic reason the next name failed, not merely state that a ceiling was reached.

This note is interpretive and subordinate to the Master Universe Prompt.
