# ATLAS Ω — PORTFOLIO UTILITY AUDIT FIELDS

For the selected N and neighboring frontier points, record enough information to explain the choice:

- expected return;
- permanent-loss risk;
- volatility/tail risk where modeled;
- fragility/financing correlation;
- legitimate robustness/offset capacity where modeled;
- uncertainty/confidence penalty;
- real complexity cost if any;
- total portfolio utility;
- ΔU from N to N+1;
- economic reason for rejecting N+1.

No utility term may exist solely to recover a cosmetically diversified or historically familiar portfolio.
