# ATLAS Ω — Rotation, Bias & Specialist Gates Completion

Date: 2026-08-20
Status: CANONICAL
Authority: ATLAS Ω v3.1 + Investment Committee Ω

## Purpose
Close the engine gaps exposed by the Aug-18/19 semiconductor drawdown: structural quality, CAPEX capture and current capital destination must never be conflated.

## Mandatory pipeline
Ingest → Normalize → Capital Destination → Rotation Velocity → Cross-Sectional Relative Strength → Flow Confirmation → MEAN neutralization → Economic Proof → Structural Capture → Forward Asymmetry → Price/Crowding → specialist gates → Falsifiers → GREEN → Decide → Persist → Learn.

## Engines completed

### 1. CAPITAL DESTINATION Ω
Finds where the marginal dollar is moving before fundamental preference is applied. Measures sector/industry/subindustry/ticker breadth, equal-weight confirmation, relative volume and 1D/3D/5D/20D persistence. Output R1–R5 plus confidence.

### 2. ROTATION VELOCITY Ω
Measures level + first derivative + second derivative of flow, breadth acceleration, RS acceleration and volume acceleration. States: EMERGING, ESTABLISHED, EXHAUSTING, EXITING.

### 3. CROSS-SECTIONAL RELATIVE STRENGTH Ω
Percentile-normalized excess returns vs market and peer group over 1D/3D/5D/20D. No market-cap bonus.

### 4. FLOW CONFIRMATION Ω
Requires price, relative volume, industry direction and breadth confirmation. Isolated news gaps do not qualify as broad flow.

### 5. EVENT PURITY Ω
Decomposes sector moves into broad component, event component and large-constituent effect. Prevents a single clinical/news shock from being labelled sector rotation.

### 6. MEAN Ω / ANTI-QUALITY & ANTI-MEGACAP BIAS
Peer and universe percentile/z-score normalization with winsorization. Market cap, index membership, analyst coverage, familiarity and evidence quantity contribute zero points. Evidence quality matters; evidence volume does not.

### 7. BIAS AUDIT Ω
Pre-publication control. Tests correlation GREEN↔market-cap, GREEN↔Quality and concentration in megacaps. Excessive dependence invalidates the ranking and forces recalculation. Quality may confirm but cannot manufacture flow.

### 8. GREEN OPERATING GATE Ω
G1 Capital Destination; G2 Relative Strength; G3 Economic Proof; G4 Forward Asymmetry; G5 Flow/Price Confirmation. 5/5 requires all. Rotation-driven 4/5 requires G1. Failure of both Capital and Confirmation caps output at 3/5. Exceptional accelerating companies may be retained below threshold only as explicitly labelled EXCEPTION, never promoted synthetically.

### 9. MEMORY SCARCITY Ω
AI demand elasticity → HBM/DRAM/NAND pricing → capacity tightness → contract duration/prepayments → FCF conversion. Structural signal is independent of Money Rotation.

### 10. CAPITAL MIGRATION Ω
Tracks PE/sponsor capital: valuation compression, recurring revenue, switching costs, FCF, margin expansion, financing capacity, AI integration and sponsor-sized enterprise value.

### 11. FALSE AI DISRUPTION Ω
Detects software punished for assumed AI displacement while retention, recurring revenue, FCF/share, dilution/SBC discipline, buybacks and AI monetization contradict the narrative.

### 12. REGULATORY BREAKUP Ω
Probability → remedy → affected business → revenue/EBIT/FCF exposure → ecosystem synergies → stranded costs → standalone valuation → SOTP → probability-weighted IV. Scenarios: INTACT, BEHAVIORAL_REMEDY, STRUCTURAL_REMEDY. A breakup is not automatically bearish.

### 13. CUSTOMER PREPAYMENT COVERAGE Ω
Beginning current/non-current contract liabilities + new customer advances − revenue recognition/reclassifications → ending liabilities; relates customer funding to backlog, WIP/inventory and incremental capacity commitments. Never compares current and non-current classifications as if they were identical series.

### 14. CAPACITY WORKING-CAPITAL STRESS Ω
Inventory growth + AR growth + customer advances + CFO + PP&E/CAPEX. Distinguishes customer-funded expansion from supplier-funded customer growth.

## Separation invariant
Quality Ω answers company quality. Structural Capture Ω answers economic capture. Money Rotation Ω answers where marginal capital is moving now. GREEN answers whether enough independent evidence converges for an actionable state. No engine may overwrite another engine's raw result.
