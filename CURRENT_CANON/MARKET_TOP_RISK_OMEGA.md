## Purpose

Detect when a normal bullish regime is becoming vulnerable to a tactical correction or a structural risk transition. This engine is a RISK REGIME GATE, not an automatic SELL engine.

Canonical sequence:

`complacency -> positioning extreme -> breadth deterioration -> volatility divergence -> bond/credit stress -> structural price break`

## INVIOLABLE LAW

**No market-top, sentiment, seasonality, VIX, bond-yield or positioning signal may emit an automatic portfolio SELL by itself.**

A SELL/reduction decision requires deterioration across multiple independent layers plus price/credit confirmation and must pass the ATLAS Thesis Falsifier / portfolio decision process.

Likewise, a high-quality long-term thesis must NOT be abandoned solely because a tactical 5–10% correction is probable.

## Core layers

1. **Complacency / positioning**
   - Bull & Bear / positioning extremes.
   - Low panic / excessive optimism.
   - Crowding and systematic exposure.
   - Extreme readings are alerts, never standalone SELL signals.

2. **Breadth deterioration**
   - % above SMA50/SMA200 deteriorating while headline index holds/rises.
   - A/D line divergence.
   - New highs narrowing.
   - Equal Weight underperforming cap-weighted index.
   - Small caps / cyclicals failing to confirm.

3. **Volatility divergence**
   - VIX stops making lower lows while equity index makes higher highs.
   - VIX term structure deterioration.
   - Rising realized volatility beneath apparently calm index levels.

4. **Rates / Treasury liquidity — PRIMARY MACRO FALSIFIER**
   - Absolute yield level is contextual; `10Y > 4%` is NOT a universal alarm.
   - Escalation becomes material when long yields break higher together with worsening liquidity, inflation expectations and equity breadth.
   - High-risk cluster: 10Y approaching/breaking ~5% + 30Y breakout + deteriorating breadth/price structure.

5. **Credit confirmation**
   - HY spreads widening.
   - HY relative to Treasuries deteriorating.
   - Funding/liquidity stress.
   - Credit stress has greater evidentiary weight than sentiment alone.

6. **Structural price confirmation**
   - Loss of SMA20/SMA50 followed by failed recovery.
   - Lower high + lower low.
   - Break of important reaction/support structure.
   - Nasdaq/S&P breadth and price deterioration occurring together.

## Operational regimes

- `GREEN — BULL REGIME`: trend and breadth healthy; normal execution.
- `YELLOW — COMPLACENCY WATCH`: sentiment/positioning stretched; no mechanical reduction.
- `ORANGE — CORRECTION RISK`: several tactical warnings; tighten ENTRY TIMING Ω / NO-CHASE.
- `RED — STRUCTURAL RISK`: rates/credit + breadth + volatility + price structure confirm deterioration; mandatory portfolio/falsifier audit.

## Hard gates

- Sentiment extreme alone = NOT RED.
- VIX spike alone = NOT RED.
- Seasonal/midterm pattern alone = NOT RED.
- 10Y yield threshold alone = NOT RED.
- One weak index session = NOT RED.
- RED requires independent confirmation from macro/credit AND market internals/price.

When regime is ORANGE:

- Do not chase extended entries.
- Prefer highest-quality companies with intact fundamentals undergoing confirmed recovery/supply exhaustion.
- Use ENTRY TIMING Ω more strictly.

When regime is RED:

- Run full portfolio stress audit.
- Re-score implied returns and funding sensitivity.

`Hyperscaler monetizes spare capacity != semiconductor demand collapse`.
