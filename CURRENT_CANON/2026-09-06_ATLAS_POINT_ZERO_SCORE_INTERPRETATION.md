# ATLAS Ω — SCORE ≠ MEMBERSHIP

An individual ATLAS score ranks/evaluates company-level opportunity under its defined inputs. Portfolio membership is a separate portfolio-level decision.

High standalone score does not guarantee selection if valuation, risk, falsifier state, correlated exposure or a stronger scarce-capital alternative makes the marginal portfolio contribution inferior.

Likewise, portfolio interaction must not be used to smuggle aesthetic diversification rewards into selection.

`COMPANY_SCORE ≠ PORTFOLIO_SLOT`
