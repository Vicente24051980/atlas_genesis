# ATLAS Ω — DATA AVAILABILITY BIAS

Companies with richer analyst coverage, U.S. filings, English-language coverage or cleaner data pipelines must not receive a fundamental score bonus merely because they are easier to research.

Where evidence is insufficient, confidence should fall and UNKNOWN fields should remain visible. The epistemic disadvantage must not be misreported as fundamental inferiority.

Discovery/research allocation should actively avoid allowing data convenience to collapse the candidate universe toward familiar megacaps.
