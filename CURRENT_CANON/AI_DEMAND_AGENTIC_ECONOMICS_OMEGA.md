# ATLAS Ω — AI Demand & Agentic Economics Ω

Status: ACTIVE · IMPLEMENTED · MAIN
Date: 2026-08-27
Runtime: `runtime/agentic_omega/ai_demand_engines.py` + `runtime/agentic_omega/agentic_workload_economics.py`

## Purpose

Extend AI Demand & Monetization Proof Ω beyond raw CAPEX and model capability by measuring whether efficiency expands aggregate compute demand, whether agents produce economic proof, whether machine-originated inference/workloads become material, whether apparent demand is circularly financed, and which software/infrastructure layer converts agent activity into owner economics.

## Canonical chain

AI CAPEX → Compute → Model Capability → Efficiency → Cheap Intelligence → Agents / Edge → Deployment → Economic Productivity → Revenue → FCF → ROIC → Expected Return.

Agentic workload feedback loop:

Human objective → agent run → subagents → tool calls → compute + networking + data/state + observability + identity/security events → paid usage → revenue/ARR → FCF/share → ROIC.

Agents and machines can feed back into infrastructure demand:

Agents / Robots → more autonomous work → more inference/tool calls/machine transactions → more total workloads → more infrastructure.

## Engine 1 — AI Efficiency / Compute Elasticity Ω

Inputs:
- change in cost per inference
- change in inference volume
- change in aggregate compute
- evidence of new workloads

Regimes:
- `DESTRUCTIVE_EFFICIENCY` — aggregate compute falls despite efficiency.
- `JEVONS_EQUILIBRIUM` — efficiency gains and usage growth broadly offset.
- `JEVONS_ACCELERATION` — aggregate compute rises despite efficiency; stronger when new workloads are documented.

This engine may strengthen or weaken the AI-demand evidence packet. It cannot generate a portfolio action by itself.

Law: **COMPUTE EFFICIENCY ↑ ≠ COMPUTE DEMAND ↓**.

## Engine 2 — Agentic Economic Value Ω

Mandatory proof sequence:

Capability → successful task completion → deployment → paid usage → productivity/revenue → FCF → ROIC.

Capability, benchmark performance, demos, token consumption, or deployment cannot be promoted directly to owner economics. Only reaching ROIC proof makes the engine eligible to pass evidence onward; Valuation, Falsifiers, Competition for Capital, sizing and execution remain independent.

Law: **CAPABILITY ≠ DEPLOYMENT ≠ PRODUCTIVITY ≠ MONETIZATION ≠ OWNER ECONOMICS**.

## Engine 3 — Agentic Token Multiplier / Machine-Originated Demand Ω

Tracks three demand origins independently:
- human-originated inference
- agent-originated inference
- machine/robot-originated inference

Agent-to-human multiplier states:
- `<1x`: RED
- `1x–<2x`: AMBER
- `2x–4x`: GREEN
- `>4x`: GREEN_STRONG

This is a leading demand indicator only.

Law: **TOKEN GROWTH ≠ FCF GROWTH**.

## Engine 4 — AI Circular Financing Gate Ω

Measures:
- investor capital supplied to recipient
- recipient compute spend
- spend returning to the investor/vendor
- external revenue
- external customer share

A high capital-recirculation ratio combined with weak external-customer share is classified RED because financed demand can masquerade as organic end demand.

Law: **FINANCED DEMAND ≠ ORGANIC DEMAND**.

## Engine 5 — Quantum Readiness Ω

Status is `WATCH_ONLY` until independent economic proof exists. Quantum patents, qubit counts, error-correction progress or technical advantage cannot create BUY, Expected Return uplift, or override normal gates.

Law: **QUANTUM READINESS ≠ ECONOMIC PROOF**.

## Engine 6 — Agentic Workload Multiplier Ω

One human request can generate multiple agent runs, subagents and tool calls. ATLAS now measures independently the resulting:
- compute units
- network transactions
- database/state operations
- observability events
- identity/security events

Task success and human intervention reduce the effective autonomous workload. The multiplier is a demand-leading indicator only.

Law: **ONE HUMAN REQUEST ≠ ONE INFERENCE**.

## Engine 7 — Agentic Permission & Security Demand Ω

Autonomous tool/file access raises productivity and risk simultaneously. The engine measures gross attack surface versus identity, least privilege, auditability and runtime-security coverage. High cyber demand is not treated as cyber monetization.

Laws:
- **AGENTIC ATTACK SURFACE ↑ ≠ CYBER MONETIZATION**.
- **IDENTITY + LEAST PRIVILEGE + AUDITABILITY ARE PRODUCTION INFRASTRUCTURE FOR AUTONOMOUS AGENTS**.

## Engine 8 — Agentic Layer Economics Ω

Economic capture is audited independently for COMPUTE, NETWORKING, DATA_STATE, OBSERVABILITY, IDENTITY_SECURITY and APPLICATION layers.

Mandatory sequence:

Machine workload growth → paid usage → recurring revenue → margin → FCF → FCF/share → ROIC.

Attribution confidence and source quality are gates. SBC/revenue and dilution are owner-economics penalties. Layer evidence can make a challenger eligible for Competition for Capital, but cannot authorize execution.

Canonical use cases:
- MDB — data/state consumption
- DDOG — observability/security event monetization
- OKTA — agent identity and permission infrastructure
- CRWD/PANW/FTNT/CHKP/S/RBRK — agentic/frontier cyber economics
- cloud and infrastructure names — attributable workload capture

Law: **WORKLOAD GROWTH ≠ FCF GROWTH**.

## Engine 9 — Agentic Efficiency / Total Workload Ω

Cheaper task completion can coexist with higher total workload if the number of tasks, subagents and machine-to-machine actions grows faster than cost/compute per task falls.

Regimes:
- `AGENTIC_JEVONS_EXPANSION`
- `AGENTIC_JEVONS_EQUILIBRIUM`
- `AGENTIC_EFFICIENCY_DESTRUCTION`

Law: **EFFICIENCY_PER_TASK ≠ LOWER_TOTAL_WORKLOAD**.

## Governance

These engines are subordinate to the existing ATLAS Ω fail-closed runtime. They do not modify CORE-00, do not bypass Falsifiers Ω, and do not turn green technical signals into investment actions. Outputs must enter the evidence packet and be evaluated with Economic Proof Ω, Valuation / Implied Return Ω, CAPEX Productivity Ω, Moat Ω, Competition for Capital, Trading 212 availability and execution/sizing gates.

The Competition for Capital hurdle remains **≥50 ATLAS Ω points on the 0–1000 scale OR ~3pp normalized Expected CAGR**. Trading 212 availability is an explicit fail-closed execution gate.
