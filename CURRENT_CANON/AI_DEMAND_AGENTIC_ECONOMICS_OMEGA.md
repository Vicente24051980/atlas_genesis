# ATLAS Ω — AI Demand & Agentic Economics Ω

Status: ACTIVE · IMPLEMENTED · MAIN
Date: 2026-08-25
Runtime: `runtime/agentic_omega/ai_demand_engines.py`

## Purpose

Extend AI Demand & Monetization Proof Ω beyond raw CAPEX and model capability by measuring whether efficiency expands aggregate compute demand, whether agents produce economic proof, whether machine-originated inference is becoming material, and whether apparent demand is circularly financed.

## Canonical chain

AI CAPEX → Compute → Model Capability → Efficiency → Cheap Intelligence → Agents / Edge → Deployment → Economic Productivity → Revenue → FCF → ROIC → Expected Return.

Agents and machines can feed back into infrastructure demand:

Agents / Robots → more autonomous work → more inference → more compute → more infrastructure.

## Engine 1 — AI Efficiency / Compute Elasticity Ω

Inputs:
- change in cost per inference
- change in inference volume
- change in aggregate compute
- evidence of new workloads

Regimes:
- `DESTRUCTIVE_EFFICIENCY` — aggregate compute falls despite efficiency.
- `JEVONS_EQUILIBRIUM` — efficiency gains and usage growth broadly offset.
- `JEVONS_ACCELERATION` — aggregate compute rises despite efficiency; stronger when new workloads are documented.

This engine may strengthen or weaken the AI-demand evidence packet. It cannot generate a portfolio action by itself.

Law: **COMPUTE EFFICIENCY ↑ ≠ COMPUTE DEMAND ↓**.

## Engine 2 — Agentic Economic Value Ω

Mandatory proof sequence:

Capability → successful task completion → deployment → paid usage → productivity/revenue → FCF → ROIC.

Capability, benchmark performance, demos, token consumption, or deployment cannot be promoted directly to owner economics. Only reaching ROIC proof makes the engine eligible to pass evidence onward; Valuation, Falsifiers, Competition for Capital, sizing and execution remain independent.

Law: **CAPABILITY ≠ DEPLOYMENT ≠ PRODUCTIVITY ≠ MONETIZATION ≠ OWNER ECONOMICS**.

## Engine 3 — Agentic Token Multiplier / Machine-Originated Demand Ω

Tracks three demand origins independently:
- human-originated inference
- agent-originated inference
- machine/robot-originated inference

Agent-to-human multiplier states:
- `<1x`: RED
- `1x–<2x`: AMBER
- `2x–4x`: GREEN
- `>4x`: GREEN_STRONG

This is a leading demand indicator only.

Law: **TOKEN GROWTH ≠ FCF GROWTH**.

## Engine 4 — AI Circular Financing Gate Ω

Measures:
- investor capital supplied to recipient
- recipient compute spend
- spend returning to the investor/vendor
- external revenue
- external customer share

A high capital-recirculation ratio combined with weak external-customer share is classified RED because financed demand can masquerade as organic end demand.

Law: **FINANCED DEMAND ≠ ORGANIC DEMAND**.

## Engine 5 — Quantum Readiness Ω

Status is permanently `WATCH_ONLY` until independent economic proof exists. Quantum patents, qubit counts, error-correction progress or technical advantage cannot create BUY, Expected Return uplift, or override normal gates.

Law: **QUANTUM READINESS ≠ ECONOMIC PROOF**.

## Governance

These engines are subordinate to the existing ATLAS Ω fail-closed runtime. They do not modify CORE-00, do not bypass Falsifiers Ω, and do not turn green technical signals into investment actions. Outputs must enter the evidence packet and be evaluated with Economic Proof Ω, Valuation / Implied Return Ω, CAPEX Productivity Ω, Moat Ω, Competition for Capital and execution/sizing gates.
