# AI PHYSICAL BOTTLENECK TRANSMISSION Ω

**Status:** ACTIVE RESEARCH CANON — HYPOTHESIS, NOT FACT  
**Effective date:** 2026-08-21

## Purpose
Track whether AI capital expenditure is monetizing first in physical bottlenecks—power, grid, copper and mining—before or more strongly than in higher software/model layers.

## Causal chain under test
AI workloads ↑ → data-center CAPEX ↑ → MW/GW demand ↑ → grid/power equipment demand ↑ → copper intensity ↑ → copper scarcity/price ↑ → miner revenue ↑ → EBITDA/FCF per share ↑ → equity repricing.

Every arrow requires independent evidence. A green mining tape is not proof that AI caused the move.

## Evidence Director classification
The statement **“AI money is in the mines”** is classified as `HYPOTHESIS_HIGH_PRIORITY`, never FACT, until it passes causal evidence, breadth, Economic Proof/FCF, institutional-flow and persistence gates.

## Required confirmation gates
1. **Causality:** traceable AI/data-center/grid demand into copper demand.
2. **Breadth:** multiple independent copper/mining equities participate; one ticker is insufficient.
3. **Economic capture:** revenue → EBITDA → FCF/share or NAV/share must improve; commodity-price narrative alone is insufficient.
4. **Institutional flow:** price action alone is not institutional flow. Confirm using fund/sector/positioning evidence where available.
5. **Persistence:** signal must survive repeated observations rather than one session.
6. **Falsifiers Ω:** veto remains absolute.

## Layer Rotation Ω
Measure relative leadership across the chain:
`MINING → GRID/POWER → DATACENTER_PHYSICAL → SEMIS → CLOUD/SOFTWARE`.

A layer jump is a leading indicator/watch signal. It is not Economic Proof and cannot by itself trigger BUY.

## Engine routing
- **Expected Return Ω:** forward probability-weighted CAGR from current price only; Economic Proof is survival gate.
- **Economic Proof Ω:** validate economic capture, FCF/share and persistence; no return-ranking bonus.
- **AI Physical Layer Ω:** causal transmission chain.
- **Layer Rotation Ω:** relative leadership and jump detection.
- **Breadth Ω:** cross-sectional participation.
- **CAPEX Productivity Ω:** project CAPEX → production → FCF/share/NAV/share.
- **Commodity Convexity Ω:** sensitivity to copper and project delivery.
- **Money Rotation Ω:** institutional flow confirmation, not inferred from price.
- **Debasement Ω:** scarce-asset macro overlay.
- **Stagflation Ω:** pricing power, FCF, ROIC and leverage resilience.
- **GREEN Ω:** price continuity only; strictly separate from Economic Proof.
- **AI Equity Monetization Ω:** compare economic capture versus equity repricing by layer.
- **Falsifiers Ω:** independent absolute veto.

## Falsifiers / alternative explanations
Copper/mining strength can arise from China demand, supply disruptions, FX/USD, general electrification, tariffs, geopolitics or commodity positioning rather than AI. Material project delays, permitting failure, CAPEX inflation, grade deterioration, balance-sheet stress, dilution, sustained copper-price weakness or failure to convert EBITDA into FCF/share weaken or invalidate the thesis.

## Return Objective Separation invariant
This module may raise research priority or provide causal/context diagnostics. It must never contaminate a pure Expected Return ordering. `EXPECTED RETURN = probability-weighted forward CAGR`, subject only to survival gates and Falsifiers Ω.
