# ATLAS Ω — Treasury Liquidity & Political Calendar Overlay

**Status:** CANON ACTIVO  
**Effective date:** 2026-08-20  
**Scope:** Macro Regime Ω / Correction Risk Ω / Investment Committee Ω context

## 1. Objective

Incorporate the interaction between U.S. Treasury liquidity operations, long-duration rates, fixed-income volatility, political calendar and equity correction risk without converting political narratives into evidence.

This module is a contextual macro layer. It cannot independently create a BUY, override observable money flow, or certify a market bottom.

## 2. Mandatory evidence architecture

Every assertion generated by this module MUST be classified as one of:

- **FACT** — independently verifiable observations: Treasury buybacks, dates, maturity buckets, yields, MOVE/VIX, credit, oil, breadth, options positioning, liquidity metrics.
- **INTERPRETATION** — economic inference about transmission into duration, financial conditions, liquidity or equities.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — unverified causal claims, including electoral intent, an alleged "Bessent Put", or an administration's ability/intention to prevent an equity correction.
- **NOISE / NO EVIDENCE** — unsupported causal narratives or market commentary without adequate evidence.

Evidence provenance remains mandatory under the ATLAS Ω constitutional evidence rules.

## 3. Treasury Liquidity Ω

### Inputs

- Treasury buyback size and frequency
- maturity/duration bucket of purchases
- UST 10Y and 30Y yields and changes
- MOVE and rate-volatility regime
- market liquidity / bid-ask / depth where available
- announced and effective dates
- Treasury financing composition and relevant issuance context

### Hard rule TL-01

**Treasury buyback != Federal Reserve QE.**

A Treasury buyback MUST NOT automatically be labelled monetary stimulus, QE, liquidity injection by the Federal Reserve, or an equity BUY signal.

The engine must separately evaluate the actual liquidity and duration effects.

3. post-2026-11-03

The calendar is a probabilistic context modifier only.

Political incentives are not evidence of market control.

Any alleged electoral support mechanism or "Bessent Put" remains **HYPOTHESIS** unless supported by independently verifiable evidence.

### Hard rule PC-02

## 5. Correction Risk Ω

Correction Risk Ω integrates, when evidence is available:

- options positioning / dealer gamma context
- VIX
- MOVE
- market breadth
- UST yields and curve dynamics
- credit spreads / stress
- oil and inflation-sensitive inputs
- seasonality
- Treasury liquidity context

### Regime states

- `SUPPORTIVE`
- `NEUTRAL`
- `FRAGILE`
- `CORRECTION`
- `CAPITULATION`
- `BOTTOM_CONFIRMING`

No state may be inferred solely from a political-calendar narrative.

## 6. Market Bottom Detection Ω authority

Market Bottom Detection Ω retains authority for confirming a market bottom.

A nominal drawdown of -10%, -20%, -25% or -35% is NOT, by itself, a BUY signal or evidence of a bottom.

Required confirmation sequence remains:

**capitulation → oversold conditions → divergence → breadth improvement → reclaim/breakout → flow confirmation**

The precise implementation may use additional verified inputs, but political support cannot substitute for these gates.

## 7. Money Rotation Ω independence

Money Rotation Ω remains independent and upstream when the question is where capital is moving now.

Macro support, Treasury liquidity or political-calendar hypotheses MUST NOT manufacture money-flow evidence that does not exist.

For current rotation, observable evidence retains precedence, including:

- relative volume
- relative strength
- sector/industry breadth
- persistence of flow
- intraday/closing confirmation as appropriate

Only after observable rotation is established may the broader ATLAS fundamental and macro filters contextualize it.

This module can increase or decrease macro confidence, correction risk, liquidity support assessment and position-sizing context.

It cannot independently output a security-level BUY based solely on Treasury operations or the political calendar.

Valid output includes `NO REGIME CHANGE` and `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`.
