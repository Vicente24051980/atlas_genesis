# ATLAS Ω — CONFIDENCE SEMANTICS

Confidence measures evidence quality/completeness and model reliability; it is not a substitute for Expected Return.

A famous, heavily covered company must not receive high confidence solely from familiarity. A less-covered company can receive high confidence when primary evidence is sufficient and coherent.

Unknown/stale/contradicted material inputs reduce confidence explicitly.

`CONFIDENCE ≠ CONVICTION_INHERITANCE`
