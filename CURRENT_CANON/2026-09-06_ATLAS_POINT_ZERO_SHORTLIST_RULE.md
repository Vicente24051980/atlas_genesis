# ATLAS Ω — SHORTLIST GENERATION RULE

The shortlist is generated from the current full-universe screen.

A company may enter the shortlist because of verified fundamentals, forward revisions/inflection, valuation/Expected Return, structural economics or authorized discovery requiring deeper verification.

A company may not enter simply because it is currently held, was historically highly ranked, is famous, is a megacap or was previously discussed often.

Shortlist provenance should record the reason for advancement.
