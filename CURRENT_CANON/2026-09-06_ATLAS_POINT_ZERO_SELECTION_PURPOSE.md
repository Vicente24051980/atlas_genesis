# ATLAS Ω — SELECTION PURPOSE

The portfolio selector optimizes scarce-capital membership for a 3–6 year horizon under the Master Universe Prompt.

It is not a watchlist generator, equal-weight recommendation list, sector allocation model, thematic basket builder or reconciliation engine.

The governing question is:

**Does this entity deserve scarce portfolio capital versus every available alternative, after fundamentals, valuation, Expected Return, risk, falsifiers and portfolio interaction?**
