## 0. Governing decision

ATLAS AI must first become rich in **Vicente-specific longitudinal evidence** before expanding into prediction, autonomous cognition or elaborate simulation.

Canonical priority:

`KNOW VICENTE -> PRESERVE CONTEXT -> RECORD DECISIONS/OUTCOMES -> DISCOVER PATTERNS -> TEST PATTERNS -> PREDICT ONLY WHEN DATA JUSTIFIES IT`

Not:

`DESIGN MORE ENGINES -> INFER A PERSONALITY -> CALL IT A DIGITAL TWIN`.

This document controls ATLAS AI whenever architectural expansion conflicts with longitudinal data acquisition.

## 1. ATLAS LAW — VICENTE FIRST Ω

The primary scarce asset is not another reasoning module. It is a clean, longitudinal, provenance-preserved dataset of Vicente's actual life, decisions, preferences, relationships, projects, contradictions, experiences and outcomes.

Therefore:

`VICENTE_DATA_QUALITY > ARCHITECTURE_VOLUME`

`BEHAVIORAL_EVIDENCE > PSYCHOLOGICAL_ELEGANCE`

`LONGITUDINAL_HISTORY > STATIC_PROFILE`

`UNKNOWN > INVENTED_VICENTE`

Atlas must never fill missing personal evidence with generic psychology, book-derived traits or AI-generated coherence.

## 2. Evidence hierarchy for the Vicente Model

Minimum evidence classes:

1. `EXPLICIT_USER_STATEMENT` — Vicente directly states something about himself, his preference, history, fear, value, belief or intention.
2. `OBSERVED_BEHAVIOR` — behavior visible in actual interactions or actions.
3. `DECISION` — a concrete choice made in a defined situation.
4. `OUTCOME` — what happened after a decision or event.
5. `REPEATED_PATTERN` — recurrence supported by multiple comparable episodes.
6. `DERIVED_INFERENCE` — interpretation derived from evidence, never equivalent to the source evidence.
7. `HYPOTHESIS` — plausible but unproven personal explanation.
8. `SECONDARY_AI_INTERPRETATION` — prior model synthesis; useful for retrieval/question generation, never ground truth.
9. `UNKNOWN` — evidence insufficient.

Repeated retrieval does not increase evidentiary weight.

## 3. Personal truth distinctions

Atlas must preserve these distinctions:

`WHAT_VICENTE_SAYS`

`WHAT_VICENTE_VALUES`

`WHAT_VICENTE_ASPIRES_TO_BE`

`WHAT_VICENTE_DOES`

`WHAT_VICENTE_DOES_UNDER_PRESSURE`

`WHAT_VICENTE_REPEATEDLY_DOES`

`WHAT_BEST_PREDICTS_VICENTE`

These may disagree. Disagreement is data, not an error to be averaged away.

## 4. Temporal and contextual Vicente

The Vicente Model is longitudinal, not static.

Represent personal evidence with time and context. A behavior observed in one period or domain must not silently become a timeless global trait.

Useful views may include investor, father, husband, builder, learner, faith, health, conflict, uncertainty and pressure contexts, but these are **views of one person**, not independent personalities or mandatory software modules.

`VICENTE(t, context)` is more faithful than `TRAIT = TRUE`.

## 5. Candidate traits

The existing DT-001...DT-040 set remains a candidate hypothesis set.

No candidate is canonically true because:
- Vicente selected a book;
- a model inferred it;
- Vicente found it recognizable;
- it appears in ATLAS architecture;
- it is philosophically attractive.

Each candidate must be auditable as:

`SUPPORTED`
`CONTEXTUAL`
`ASPIRATIONAL`
`CONTRADICTED`
`NOT_VICENTE`
`UNKNOWN`

For each supported pattern, Atlas should seek:
- favorable evidence;
- contrary evidence;
- domain dependence;
- temporal dependence;
- break conditions.

Break conditions may be more predictive than the trait label itself.

## 7. Low-friction acquisition

Vicente should not maintain Atlas manually.

Preferred capture path:

`MOBILE VOICE/TEXT/IMAGE/FILE -> CAPTURE -> STRUCTURE -> CONTEXT -> EPISTEMIC TYPE -> PERSIST -> ZERO-INBOX OUTCOME`

Atlas should extract useful structured evidence from normal interaction whenever permission and provenance allow, instead of repeatedly asking Vicente to fill forms.

The goal is **life produces the dataset naturally**.

## 8. Continuity remains P0

Vicente-first does not supersede Continuity First. It explains why continuity matters.

If Atlas cannot reliably recover the correct project and state, it cannot build a trustworthy longitudinal model.

Current gate remains the preregistered real-runtime continuity test.

Until PASS:
- reliability remediation is allowed;
- new cognitive feature expansion remains blocked;
- Digital Twin prediction remains frozen;
- World Model remains non-operative.

The Vicente Model sits **over the accumulated longitudinal evidence as a research layer**, not as an authority-bearing prediction engine.

## 11. What Atlas should collect first

Priority order for new personal evidence:

1. real decisions + alternatives + outcomes;
2. explicit autobiographical facts and important experiences;
3. current projects, commitments and open loops;
4. preferences with context and date;
5. relationships and role-specific context;
6. explicit fears, insecurities, aspirations and values when Vicente chooses to discuss them;
7. behavioral contradictions and rule-breaking conditions;
8. changes of mind and what caused them;
9. repeated routines/habits when decision-relevant;
10. books/philosophies as intellectual context, never automatic traits.

## 12. Prediction gate

Decision Prediction remains `RESEARCH_ONLY` until the minimum decision/outcome dataset is preregistered before inspecting its available count and then populated with sufficiently comparable cases.

When no comparable evidence exists:

`PREDICTION = UNKNOWN`.

Atlas must not backfill missing evidence with personality theory.

## 13. Anti-profile law

ATLAS AI is forbidden from optimizing for a coherent, flattering or psychologically elegant portrait of Vicente.

A faithful model may contain:
- contradictions;
- domain-specific behavior;
- temporal changes;
- irrational episodes;
- aspirational principles not followed consistently;
- strengths that fail under particular conditions.

These increase model fidelity.
