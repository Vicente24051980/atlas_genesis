1. Insurance/reinsurance demand generated by an already-counted data-center project is **derived demand**, not a new independent CAPEX funding pool.
2. DCRT scores have **zero direct weight** in Fundamental Score and FRU-MATH.
3. T5 is not authorized from market formation alone. Default handoff requires traceable company `C3/E3` or `C4/E4` economics.
4. A zero/invalid `own capital at risk` denominator returns `NO_CALCULABLE`, never infinity.
5. No dedicated facility, exchange, CAT bond, sidecar or ILS structure is a BUY signal.
