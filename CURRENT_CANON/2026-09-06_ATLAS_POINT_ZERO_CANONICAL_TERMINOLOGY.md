# ATLAS Ω — CANONICAL TERMINOLOGY

Preferred terms:

- `MASTER_UNIVERSE_PROMPT`
- `POINT_ZERO_REBUILD`
- `CANONICAL_ECONOMIC_ENTITY`
- `CLEAN_SELECTED_PORTFOLIO`
- `CURRENT_OPERATIONAL_PORTFOLIO`
- `OPTIMAL_N`
- `CUT_FRONTIER`
- `SLOT_COMPETITOR`
- `REBUILD_DIFF`
- `EXECUTION_TRANSITION_PLAN`

Avoid ambiguous uses of “current portfolio”, “canonical portfolio”, “target portfolio” and “incumbent” without the relevant state qualifier.
