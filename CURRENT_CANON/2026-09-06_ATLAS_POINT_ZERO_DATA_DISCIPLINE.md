# ATLAS Ω — POINT ZERO DATA DISCIPLINE

Canonical selection must distinguish `VERIFIED`, `PARTIAL`, `UNKNOWN`, `CONTRADICTED` and stale evidence.

Rules:

- missing data is never silently filled with a narrative estimate;
- better data availability does not itself increase company quality or selection score;
- insufficient evidence lowers confidence and may prevent a candidate from outranking a better-verified alternative, but this must be labeled as epistemic uncertainty rather than fundamental weakness;
- stale evidence must carry a cutoff/freshness warning;
- every final candidate needs enough evidence to support thesis, valuation, expected return, risk and falsifier analysis;
- data-source provenance survives historical supersession even when old portfolio conclusions do not.
