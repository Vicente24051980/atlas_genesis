# ATLAS Ω — EVIDENCE REFRESH REQUIREMENT

A full Point Zero rebuild requires a declared `DATA_CUTOFF` and sufficiently current evidence for variables that materially determine Expected Return, risk or falsifier state.

Historical research can accelerate retrieval but cannot substitute for refreshing stale price-sensitive or time-sensitive variables such as valuation, revisions, guidance, balance-sheet changes, financing conditions, material regulation and major competitive developments.

If adequate refresh cannot be completed across the decision frontier, the run must reduce confidence or be labeled partial/shadow.
