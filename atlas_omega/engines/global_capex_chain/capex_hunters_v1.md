# ATLAS Ω — CAPEX HUNTERS v1.0
Date: 2026-08-17

## Status
Transversal discovery module attached to Global CAPEX Chain Ω. It does NOT overwrite or merge Principal Ω, Good Companies Cheap Ω, Historical Dislocation/Burry Ω, Money Rotation Ω, Challenger Gate Ω, or other engines.

## Mission
Discover companies that capture economic value from other companies' or governments' CAPEX before consensus fully prices the transfer.

## Core distinction
CAPEX spender != CAPEX captor.
A CAPEX Hunter searches for suppliers, bottlenecks, enablers, infrastructure owners, service providers and contractual beneficiaries that receive revenue/cash flow because another actor must spend.

## Canonical chain
CAPEX INTENT -> FUNDING -> PERMITS/SOCIAL LICENSE -> POWER/WATER/LAND -> EQUIPMENT -> CONSTRUCTION -> CONNECTION -> UTILIZATION -> REVENUE -> CASH FLOW -> ROIC

## New variable: Buildability & Social License Ω
Measure whether announced CAPEX can actually be built and operated.
Dimensions:
- regulatory/permitting friction
- local/community opposition
- power availability and interconnection queues
- water availability
- land/zoning
- grid upgrade dependency
- environmental constraints
- customer-borne vs community-borne infrastructure cost

State: B0 clear, B1 manageable, B2 material friction, B3 bottleneck, B4 thesis-threatening.

This variable is separate from Economic Proof Gate E0-E4 and CAPEX Fragility Ω.

## CAPEX Hunter categories
H1 PICKS_AND_SHOVELS — indispensable equipment/components.
H2 BOTTLENECK_OWNER — scarce capacity/IP/grid/interconnection/critical input.
H3 BUILDOUT_ENABLER — engineering, construction, cooling, electrical, testing.
H4 POWER_ENABLER — generation, turbines, grid, transformers, LNG/power systems.
H5 CONTRACTUAL_CAPTOR — long-duration contracts/backlog directly tied to funded projects.
H6 SECOND_ORDER_CAPTOR — benefits because the first-order build creates a new constraint.
H7 SOVEREIGN_CAPEX_CAPTOR — captures government/defense/grid/industrial-policy spending.

## CAPEX Capture Score Ω /100
Economic dependency / necessity: 20
Backlog/order/revenue proof: 20
Incremental margin & FCF conversion: 15
ROIC / incremental ROIC: 15
Bottleneck persistence: 10
Pricing power: 10
Balance-sheet/funding quality: 5
Buildability exposure: 5

Penalties are recorded separately, never hidden inside quality:
- CAPEX Fragility Ω
- customer concentration
- valuation/expectation risk
- cyclicality
- regulatory/geopolitical dependency
- dilution/financing dependency

## Hard evidence gate
No company receives CONFIRMED CAPEX CAPTOR status from narrative alone. Require at least one primary economic proof: disclosed backlog/order growth, contracted capacity, project award, segment revenue acceleration, customer-funded commitment, or measurable incremental cash-flow evidence.

## States
90-100 ELITE_CAPTOR
80-89 STRONG_CAPTOR
70-79 EMERGING_CAPTOR
60-69 WATCH
<60 INSUFFICIENT

Additional timing labels: EARLY, CONFIRMED, CROWDED, NO_CHASE, DECELERATING.

## Discovery rule
Search upstream and downstream from every large CAPEX announcement. For each spender identify 3-10 potential captors, then test whether revenue/order/FCF evidence is already appearing. Prefer captors whose own CAPEX burden is materially lower than the economic value they capture.

## Anti-error laws
PRICE != FUNDAMENTAL EVIDENCE.
MARKET CAP CHANGE != CAPITAL FLOW.
CAPEX ANNOUNCEMENT != REALIZED DEMAND.
BACKLOG != REVENUE != FCF.
CUSTOMER COMMITMENT != GUARANTEED REVENUE unless contract terms support it.
A winner in CAPEX Hunters does not become a Principal Ω BUY automatically.
