## Key model conclusions
1. The largest CAPEX captor is not necessarily the best investment at today’s price. ETN/TT/EME/VRT can be elite businesses and still lose the entry contest to CB/LMT/BKR/ICE.
2. TSM currently combines unusually strong realized growth with a materially lower late-July earnings multiple than most AI-infrastructure peers; geopolitical concentration is the dominant reason not to size it like a normal industrial compounder.
3. CB is the clearest non-theme winner: underwriting, investment income, tangible-book growth, low beta and a ~12.5x late-Jul forward multiple produce unusually clean asymmetry.
4. BKR’s order/RPO acceleration is real, but revenue has not yet accelerated; it remains a CAPEX-conversion thesis rather than a fully realized revenue-growth thesis.
5. LMT and GD convert sovereign spending into cash/backlog with much lower valuation expectations than many AI/power captors.
6. NVDA remains one of the strongest businesses in the entire universe, but the Aug-26 earnings event and financing/crowding concerns make entry timing separate from thesis quality.
7. GOOGL and META are not rejected because of CAPEX; they are held to the Economic Proof/Payback Gate because quarterly FCF has been compressed by the buildout.
