## Purpose
Convert the six unresolved seat-pressure questions into a decision-ready map without altering the frozen five-session test. This gate separates:
1. BUSINESS/SEAT STATE = PROTECT / CHALLENGE / REPLACE-CANDIDATE
2. TIMING OVERLAY = STARTER / WAIT / NO-CHASE

A business can be PROTECT and simultaneously NO-CHASE. A challenger can be superior on implied return but still fail diversification or timing.

`AMZN = CHALLENGE`

No replacement is authorized during freeze. Post-freeze deciding metrics: FCF/share recovery, incremental ROIC, AWS vs Meta core-monetization growth, and updated entry valuation.
