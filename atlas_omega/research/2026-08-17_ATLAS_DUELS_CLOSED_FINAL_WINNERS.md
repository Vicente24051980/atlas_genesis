## Governance
This file closes the current research duels. It does not modify the official frozen five-session portfolio. A research winner can become a post-freeze replacement proposal only after the scheduled portfolio evaluation.

## Decision rule
Winner = best combined expected 3–6y shareholder return under ATLAS after considering:
- latest economic proof,
- per-share growth / FCF conversion,
- ROIC / capital intensity,
- balance sheet,
- valuation and multiple-compression risk,
- funding-pool duplication,
- concentration / regulatory / geopolitical risk,
- timing / NO-CHASE.

Price-based valuation anchors are validated S&P Global / StockAnalysis snapshots through 2026-08-10. They are not represented as live 2026-08-17 quotes. ATLAS CAGR figures are scenario outputs, not promises.

## DUEL 1 — META vs AMZN
### Winner: META
- META Q2 revenue $60.801B, +28%; ad impressions +14%; price/ad +12%; OCF $31.862B; capex incl. finance-lease principal $31.08B; FCF $0.784B.
- AMZN Q2 sales $200.6B, +20%; AWS $42.2B, +37%; AWS operating income $16.6B; TTM OCF $161.4B, +33%; TTM FCF -$7.6B due mainly to AI-related PP&E acceleration.
- Valuation snapshots: META forward P/E ~16.03x; AMZN ~27.03x.
- Updated normalized 4y ATLAS base: META ~20.0% price CAGR vs AMZN ~14.7%.

Verdict: `META_WINS_CURRENT_RETURN_DUEL`.
AMZN remains an excellent business and retains stronger direct AWS monetization proof, but META currently offers materially more valuation-adjusted upside. Both remain under AI CAPEX PAYBACK Ω.

## DUEL 2 — FIX vs PWR
### Winner: FIX
- FIX validated forward P/E ~32.09x; PWR ~40.08x.
- Both have strong real buildout proof. PWR owns broader transmission/grid/large-load exposure; FIX is more directly leveraged to mission-critical mechanical/electrical construction.
- Updated normalized 4y ATLAS base: FIX ~16.0% vs PWR ~7.9% price CAGR.

Verdict: `FIX_WINS_CURRENT_RETURN_DUEL`.
PWR remains a stronger breadth-of-infrastructure business; FIX wins because the expected-return gap after valuation normalization is too large to ignore.

## DUEL 3 — CRDO vs APH
### Winner: CRDO
- CRDO forward P/E ~32.82x; APH ~27.27x.
- CRDO: much higher growth/asymmetry but extreme customer concentration and ~3.9% YoY share-count growth.
- APH: superior diversification, order quality, cash conversion and lower concentration risk.
- Updated normalized 4y ATLAS base: CRDO ~22.2% vs APH ~18.8% under explicit normalized assumptions.

Verdict: `CRDO_WINS_ASYMMETRY_DUEL`.
APH is the automatic quality challenger if CRDO concentration, pricing, revisions, margins, FCF/share or dilution deteriorate.

## DUEL 4 — AXON vs IOT
### Winner: AXON
- AXON forward P/E ~62.65x; IOT ~51.73x.
- IOT current S&P Global snapshot: revenue $1.73B TTM; FCF $234.9M; FCF margin 13.6%; shares +3.1% YoY; forward P/E ~51.7x; P/FCF ~98.5x.
- AXON remains the stronger business proof / contracted-demand platform, but its multiple requires NO-CHASE discipline.
- Normalized 4y ATLAS base under explicit assumptions: AXON ~16.9% vs IOT ~12.5%.

Verdict: `AXON_WINS_BUSINESS_RETURN_DUEL / NO_CHASE`.
IOT remains a valid successor watch, but FCF/share must catch valuation before it challenges the AXON seat.
