## What the ranking says

1. The strongest current combination is not one sector. It is a barbell of semiconductors/platforms with very high capital efficiency (TSM, NVDA, MSFT, META), physical bottleneck captors with verified orders/FCF (ETN, BKR, TT, FIX, APH, EME), and unusually cheap high-quality toll/defense/insurance businesses (CB, ICE, LMT, NOC, GD).
2. The purest CAPEX winner is not automatically the best investment. GEV and PWR have extraordinary economic proof but rank below cheaper names because expectations are already very high.
3. POWL improved materially after Q3: record $934M orders, ~3.0x book-to-bill, $2.4B backlog, no debt. It is promoted to a serious challenger, but live valuation must be rechecked before entry because the last verified forward multiple was still high.
4. Consumer staples are receiving fund flows, but KO/PEP do not beat the growth/FCF/valuation combination of the leaders. PEP remains a turnaround rather than a confirmed marginal-dollar winner.
5. Europe is a confirmed regional receiver, which supports SU.PA, SAF.PA, ALV.DE, ABB and the defense names, but regional flow is not sufficient by itself to override company valuation or cash conversion.
6. Gold is a confirmed flow receiver and AEM has direct FCF proof, making it a stronger diversifier than forcing another expensive AI/power supplier into a concentrated portfolio.
