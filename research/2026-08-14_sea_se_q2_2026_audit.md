## ATLAS interpretation
Revenue growth remains exceptional, but profit/EBITDA growth is much slower than top-line growth. This means reinvestment intensity and credit costs are absorbing operating leverage. Shopee remains the core growth engine; Monee is the fastest-risk-growing engine because loan balances and credit-loss provisions are scaling rapidly. Garena remains supportive but no longer offsets group cost pressure by itself.

Key positive: management still targets roughly US$1bn Shopee adjusted EBITDA for FY2026, indicating continued focus on profitable growth.
Key risk: if Monee credit losses rise faster than loan growth or Shopee marketing intensity remains structurally elevated, the current growth quality deteriorates.

## Decision
SE is not a clean Quality 5/5 compounder today. It is a high-growth platform with improving scale but material margin and credit-cycle risk. Keep on priority watchlist; no portfolio replacement until current valuation, post-results price continuity and Monee credit quality (NPL/coverage/vintages) are validated.
