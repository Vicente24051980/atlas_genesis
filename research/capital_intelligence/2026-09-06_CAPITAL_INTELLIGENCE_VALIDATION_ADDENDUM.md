## OUTCOME-INDEPENDENT SAMPLE LAW Ω

The sample is defined before outcomes are observed.

If the complete eligible population is observable, use the complete population. No holding/event may enter or leave the sample because it became a winner, loser, interesting anecdote, famous position or convenient negative control.

For allocator disclosure research this means:

`SAMPLE = ALL ELIGIBLE FILINGS × ALL ELIGIBLE LINES/EVENTS`

not selected CVNA / TEVA / RRC / MOH / PAR / DAKT examples.

Examples may illustrate a result only after the population-level test is frozen.

## NEGATIVE CONTROL DISCIPLINE

A failed remembered holding is not a negative control. A negative/control cohort must be generated mechanically by an ex-ante rule.

Default control hierarchy:

1. all disclosed events from the same allocator/time window;
2. sector-matched securities/events;
3. same-quarter comparable events from other allocators when available;
4. simple factor baselines.

## PERSISTENCE SURPRISE Ω

Absolute holding duration is not evidence of conviction for a low-turnover manager.

Replace raw persistence with a manager-relative statistic:

`PERSISTENCE_SURPRISE = observed holding duration - expected duration conditional on manager turnover/holding-life distribution`

Implementation may use survival percentiles/hazard models rather than arithmetic difference, but the denominator is mandatory.

## HARVEST / SELL DIRECTION

A disclosed decrease or exit has `DIRECTIONAL_INFORMATION = 0` by default.

It may trigger research or reduce confidence that the previously observed position remains current, but it does not create a negative expected-return signal unless a validated rule demonstrates directional information.

## BLIND PROMOTION GATE Ω

Candidates sourced from CAPITAL INTELLIGENCE must be evaluated by ATLAS MAIN with provenance hidden.

Procedure:

1. strip allocator/source label;
2. mix with candidates from unrelated discovery routes;
3. score/audit under Main using the same PIT evidence pack;
4. freeze verdict;
5. reveal provenance only after verdict.

Without this procedure, a promotion is `CONVERGENCE`, not independent validation of the discovery source.

## SCORE CALIBRATION

Until sufficient OOS backtest evidence exists, Capital Intelligence outputs tiers rather than pseudo-precise 0-100 scores:

- `HIGH`
- `MEDIUM`
- `LOW`
- `UNRESOLVED`

Numeric calibration requires empirical mapping from signal value to subsequent sector-adjusted outcome.

## DISCLOSURE PARADOX / REALISTIC CEILING

The null hypothesis remains that post-publication alpha disappears after disclosure latency. Public observability can erode the very edge that made a concentrated low-turnover allocator successful.

A surprisingly large public-copy alpha is treated first as a leakage/design warning, not as proof of discovery.

## INCREMENTAL-VALUE TEST

Primary architecture test:

`MODEL_A = fundamentals + valuation + revisions + risk`

`MODEL_B = MODEL_A + validated Capital Intelligence variables`

Capital Intelligence earns promotion only if:

`OOS_PERFORMANCE(MODEL_B) > OOS_PERFORMANCE(MODEL_A)`

on the pre-registered outcome metric after sector/factor adjustment and disclosure latency.

Raw alpha versus SPY is insufficient.
