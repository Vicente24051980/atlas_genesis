## Purpose

Separate higher-information beneficial-ownership disclosures from ordinary delayed 13F observations. A Schedule 13G event is not automatically alpha, but a fresh threshold crossing can reveal capital direction closer to real time than a quarterly 13F.

## Canonical hierarchy

1. `NEW_13G_CROSS_5` — initial Schedule 13G associated with a fresh crossing above 5% ownership.
2. `13G_A_MATERIAL_DIRECTION` — amendment showing a material increase/decrease in beneficial ownership.
3. `13G_A_PERSISTENCE` — amendment confirming continued >5% ownership without evidence of fresh accumulation.
4. `13F_SHARE_DIRECTION` — normalized share increase/decrease after corporate-action reconciliation.
5. `13F_VISIBLE_WEIGHT_CHANGE` — lowest-information direction layer because price effects contaminate weights.

This is an information hierarchy only. None of the states contributes direct points to ATLAS Company Score, BUY/SELL or sizing until validated OOS.
