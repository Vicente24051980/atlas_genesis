## AI infrastructure red team
### Bear case
- hyperscaler/AI capex increasingly depends on debt, leases, guarantees and complex financing;
- return on AI capex remains difficult to quantify and rating agencies are focusing on leverage and commitments;
- power/grid interconnection, upfront transmission costs, water and permitting can become binding project constraints;
- hardware residual values and circular financing can transmit losses if demand/utilization weakens;
- elevated expectations mean strong operating results can still produce poor equity returns.

### Bull case
- current 2026 evidence still shows strong AI bookings, revenue growth and deployment visibility across compute, networking and power/cooling;
- bottlenecks can strengthen pricing and backlog for scarce enabling infrastructure;
- demand is broadening from GPUs toward custom accelerators, networking, cooling, microgrids and electrical equipment.

### Conclusion
The thesis remains alive but should be underwritten as a financing-and-returns problem, not merely a demand-growth story. Prefer companies with verified orders/backlog, cash conversion, pricing power and low dependence on speculative utilization.
