**Purpose:** Preserve the call prospectively so ATLAS Ω can measure how long market commentators/gurus take to be right or wrong about major declines, bottoms, recoveries and regime changes. This is an observation log, not endorsement.

## Claim snapshot

Finanzix argues that the recent gold/silver correction is a cyclical correction inside a secular precious-metals bull market rather than a confirmed secular trend reversal.

### Claims to audit
1. The correction can be deep (roughly 20–40%) without invalidating the secular bull market.
2. Current conditions may be forming an important bottom after speculative excess/sentiment has been purged.
3. A large rotation from equities into precious metals has not yet occurred, implying the cycle may still be early.
4. Yield-curve steepening, real rates, inflation expectations, Fed policy and USD dynamics may become supportive of precious metals.
5. Three proposed paths: gradual recovery over 12–18 months; explosive move if equity capital rotates into metals; or prolonged consolidation.
6. Explicit falsification/risk factors include unexpectedly higher/stronger rates and renewed USD strength.

## Prospective scoring protocol

Do not move the baseline after the fact. Evaluate the original 2026-08-16 call at fixed checkpoints:

- +1 month
- +3 months
- +6 months
- +12 months
- +18 months

At every checkpoint record:

- Gold return from capture date
- Silver return from capture date
- Maximum drawdown after capture
- Time to recover the pre-correction reference level / prior high
- AEM return and relative performance vs gold
- S&P 500 return and Gold/S&P relative ratio
- 2Y/10Y Treasury curve
- real yields
- DXY/USD regime
- gold ETF flows, especially North America
- central-bank demand when available
- whether an equity-to-metals rotation actually occurred

## Outcome labels

- EARLY_CORRECT: direction/regime right and material confirmation within 3 months
- CORRECT: thesis confirmed within stated 12–18 month horizon
- LATE_CORRECT: eventually right, but outside stated horizon
- PARTIAL: direction right but mechanism/timing materially wrong
- WRONG: trend/regime contradicts the thesis through the evaluation horizon
- UNFALSIFIABLE: claim was framed too broadly to test objectively

## Anti-hindsight rule

Future reviews must quote this frozen snapshot and must not rewrite the original thesis using information learned later. Track both **directional accuracy** and **time-to-correctness**. A guru who eventually becomes right after an undefined delay is not scored the same as a correctly timed forecast.
