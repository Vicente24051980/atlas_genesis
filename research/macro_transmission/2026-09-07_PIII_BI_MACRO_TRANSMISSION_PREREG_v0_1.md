## 4. Hypotheses and falsifiers

### H1 — Labor resilience increases the inflation/Fed sensitivity of incoming data

**Prediction:** if PPI/CPI are firm, the 2Y should remain elevated or rise and the market should price a more hawkish Fed path.

**Falsifier:** inflation materially cools and the 2Y falls despite strong payrolls.

**Control:** wage growth. Strong payrolls without wage acceleration are not sufficient evidence of overheating.

### H2 — Energy matters only if the geopolitical premium transmits into observable physical/economic variables

**Prediction:** persistent Brent/products strength plus evidence of constrained flows should support inflation expectations and yields.

**Falsifier:** physical flows normalize and crude/products retrace sustainably even if geopolitical headlines remain loud.

**Prohibited inference:** `headline severity → inflation impact` without observable flow/price transmission.

### H3 — Current credit stress is quality discrimination, not yet broad crisis

**Prediction:** CCC stress may remain severe while BB/IG and quality equity remain functional.

**Falsifier toward systemic deterioration:** stress broadens materially into BB/IG, defaults/restructurings rise, and equity stops absorbing high yields.

**Falsifier toward normalization:** sustained CCC spread compression without worsening recoveries/defaults and without contagion.

### H4 — Equity resilience is conditional, not proof that rates no longer matter

**Prediction:** indices can remain resilient while earnings hold and credit stress stays contained, but sensitivity to valuation/guidance should remain high.

**Falsifier:** a persistent rise in yields produces no observable degradation in duration-sensitive equities, breadth, valuation or funding-sensitive segments across the test window.
