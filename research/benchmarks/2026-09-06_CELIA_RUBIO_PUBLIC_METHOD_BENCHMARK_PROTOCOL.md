## Identity / evidence firewall
This protocol concerns the Spanish personal-finance educator Celia Rubio (`celiarubio.com`, LinkedIn `celiarubiom`). Do not attribute unrelated GitHub accounts or repositories to her without independent identity proof.

Only publicly stated principles are admissible. Paid-course contents, private portfolio holdings, weights, transactions, or a purported proprietary algorithm are UNKNOWN unless publicly disclosed by Celia herself.

## Publicly observed method
Public material supports these components:
- budget / cash-flow control before investing;
- regular saving;
- emergency liquidity buffer before risky investment;
- diversified funds / index funds as a simple default;
- explicit attention to fees, fund structure, manager/index, distribution/accumulation and currency exposure;
- periodic contributions / DCA rather than waiting for a perfect entry;
- diversification to reduce single-stock risk.

These are OBSERVED PRINCIPLES, not evidence of a secret quantitative stock-selection formula.

## Research question
Does ATLAS stock selection add measurable risk-adjusted value over a simple, low-maintenance passive process consistent with the publicly observable Celia Rubio method?

## Critical anti-hindsight rule
We will NOT reconstruct an exact 'Celia portfolio' unless exact holdings/weights/date-stamped rules are independently public and verified. Until then the benchmark is named `PUBLIC_PASSIVE_METHOD_PROXY`, not `CELIA_PORTFOLIO`.

## Cash-flow equality
Every arm receives identical starting capital, contribution amount, contribution date and currency-conversion convention. No arm may receive cash earlier/later because of market information.

## Spain friction layer
Report two views:
1. pre-tax market performance;
2. implementation-aware performance including documented transaction/FX/product costs and a separate tax-structure note.
Do not invent tax drag. Fund-transfer tax deferral and ETF/share taxation must be modeled only from verified Spanish tax rules and actual realization assumptions.

## Metrics
Primary:
- time-weighted total return;
- money-weighted return/XIRR under identical cash flows;
- annualized volatility when horizon permits;
- maximum drawdown;
- downside deviation / Sortino when sample permits;
- turnover;
- implementation costs.

Secondary:
- Sharpe with the same risk-free convention;
- Ulcer Index;
- hit rate by month/quarter only after sufficient observations;
- concentration and contribution-to-risk.

## Decision rule
ATLAS is not credited for complexity merely for higher raw return. Evidence of value requires a persistent advantage that survives risk, drawdown, turnover, implementation friction and the same cash-flow schedule.

If ATLAS does not discriminate economically from passive proxies after a sufficiently long prospective sample, record `COMPLEXITY_NOT_JUSTIFIED_BY_EVIDENCE` rather than changing the benchmark after seeing results.

## Minimum horizon
The Tuesday-Friday 4-session portfolio experiment is a microstructure/implementation observation only and CANNOT validate long-run superiority over passive investing. The benchmark ledger begins prospectively now and should be evaluated at predeclared checkpoints; no claim of structural alpha from a few sessions.

## Falsifiers
- benchmark definition changed after observing relative performance;
- missing dividends in one arm but not another;
- unequal contribution dates;
- survivorship/look-ahead in ATLAS constituents;
- inferred Celia holdings presented as observed;
- tax assumptions chosen ex post;
- comparing ATLAS net-of-cost with passive gross-of-cost or vice versa.

Any of these invalidates the comparison until repaired.

## Authority
This benchmark can falsify ATLAS complexity. It cannot generate BUY/SELL orders, alter target weights, or promote any Celia-derived stock-selection signal.
